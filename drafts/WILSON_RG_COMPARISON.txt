THE PROBLEM

Fix a disjoint pair Gamma=(Gamma_1,Gamma_2), representations R_1,R_2, and
a fixed physical volume and renormalization prescription. The aim is
ultraviolet convergence of their renormalized joint expectation.

Balaban changes gauges between RG steps to obtain estimates. Complete closed
gauge-invariant insertions must therefore remain the objects carried and
estimated throughout the induction. Gauge invariance allows these gauge
changes; it supplies no quantitative remainder estimate by itself. Retain
the exact measure, gauge-fixing factors, Jacobians and physical denominator.

ONE-STEP OPERANDS

rho_h: the full physical density entering step h, with its actual retained
older fields, exterior interactions, cutoffs and history data.

T_h: the exact block integration of RG1 (0.13), applied to that density
and its insertion. Its CRE/LFR representations must retain every printed
operation and factor, including postponed integrations. Subsequent gauge
changes and R-operations must preserve the physical integral being compared.

V: all output and retained coordinates of the step. The notation integral dV
below includes the remaining physical integrations and required history sums;
it is not integration under a newly assigned law for inherited coordinates.
The exact base measures and integrated/frozen labels must be instantiated
from the particular physical RG formula before a calculation starts.

W_hat_{h,Gamma}: the complete renormalized JOINT closed source entering the
step. At the initial step its convention is

  W_hat_{h,Gamma}(U)
    = [Z_{1,h} Z_{2,h}]^{-1}
      [d_{R_1}^{-1} tr R_1(U(Gamma_{1,h}))]
      [d_{R_2}^{-1} tr R_2(U(Gamma_{2,h}))].                  (WC.0)

Gamma_{i,h} are the prescribed lattice representatives of the fixed loops.
Z_{i,h} have the bare-equals-Z-times-renormalized convention defined in
WC-Z1 below; their scheme and contour prescription must be fixed. At subsequent steps
the source includes every inherited complete closed-source contribution;
it is not replaced by the product of two separately integrated insertions.

W_hat^{ref}_{h+1,Gamma}: the prescribed next-scale representative in the
inductive closed-source class, including its renormalization and inherited
closed remainders. Constructing this class and representative is still open.
The exact conditional image is a legitimate effective-observable definition.
Using it as the reference makes this particular comparison zero, but proves
no closure of an inductive source class or ultraviolet control. A useful
comparison requires that quantitative control as well. Renormalization
factors may not be fitted afterwards to the joint expectation.

Both insertions already include their prescribed renormalization. No extra
perimeter scalar is divided out outside the comparison below. Disjointness
does not imply independence, separate RG images, or factorization of the
joint source correction.

THE MINIMAL PROPOSED ESTIMATE -- WO-P1 / WC.1

Put

  rho_{h+1}(V) = T_h[rho_h](V),
  D_h = integral dV rho_{h+1}(V) > 0,
  F_h(V) = T_h[rho_h W_hat_{h,Gamma}](V) / rho_{h+1}(V).    (WC.1a)

The ratio is required only almost everywhere for the physical output law;
the numerator formula below avoids division at zero-density outputs.
rho_{h+1} is the exact effective density, not an assumed bare coarse action.

The target is

  | integral dV {
        T_h[rho_h W_hat_{h,Gamma}](V)
        - rho_{h+1}(V) W_hat^{ref}_{h+1,Gamma}(V)
    } | <= epsilon_h D_h.                                  (WC.1)

Equivalently, for dmu_{h+1}=rho_{h+1}(V)dV/D_h,

  | E_{mu_{h+1}}[F_h-W_hat^{ref}_{h+1,Gamma}] |
       <= epsilon_h.                                       (WC.1b)

The absolute value is OUTSIDE the complete physical integral. Neither a
pointwise bound nor a bound for the integral of the absolute value is being
requested as the minimal target. Gauge changes act simultaneously on the
closed source and every relevant background/inherited operand.

FIRST DOWNSTREAM USE AND REQUIRED UNIFORMITY

expectations under the exact physical RG pushforward. Compatible induction

PERIMETER AND CORNER DEFINITIONS -- WC-Z1

This is a source/definition result, not a WC.1 estimate. The archived target
at section 1, lines 270--290, consists of simple rectilinear loops. For an
isotropic axis-link prescription with the required lattice symmetries, use

  W_R^ren(C;a,mu,S) = Z_R(C;a,mu,S)^(-1) W_R^bare(C;a),
  Z_R(C;a,mu,S)
    = exp[-m_R(a;S) P_a(C)]
      product_{v in physical Corn(C)} z_R(theta_v;a,mu,S).       (WC.Z1)

P_a is the actual physical length of the specified lattice contour.
m_R is a real scalar of dimension inverse length; z_R is a nonzero
dimensionless scalar, with a specified real-positive or analytic branch.
Both also depend on the regulator/action, representation, coupling trajectory
and complete fixed scheme S. Neither depends on retained fields or the
target pair. theta is the angle between forward incoming and outgoing unit
tangents: zero means a smooth join, which receives no factor (z_R(0)=1).
Backtracking and intersections are outside this scalar simple-loop formula.
Direction-dependent discretizations require corresponding line/corner types;
one common m_R is not justified for arbitrary staircase paths.

This sign convention is authoritative. Musch et al. use the multiplying
factor exp[-delta_m P-4 nu(90 degrees)] for a rectangle, so m=-delta_m
and z_90=exp[nu(90 degrees)]. Brandt--Neri--Sato and Korchemsky--Radyushkin
use multiplying cusp factors, the inverses of our z_R. Representation
dependence must be retained; no all-orders Casimir-scaling rule is assumed.

Scheme data are essential: ultraviolet locality specifies the form, not the
finite parts. A concrete FINITE-VOLUME right-angle scheme can be specified
without an infinite line or an infinite-time limit. Fix a physical reference
ensemble (its action, coupling trajectory, volume and boundary conditions),
two closed axis-aligned reference rectangles Q_A,Q_B of different perimeters,
and their literal cutoff-compatible link paths. They are chosen once,
independently of the target loops and of retained-field values. For each R,

  M_{R,i}(a) = [integral rho_a^ref(U) W_R^bare(Q_{i,a};U) dU]
                /[integral rho_a^ref(U) dU],  i=A,B.            (WC.Z2)

Here rho_a^ref is the complete physical density of that fixed reference
ensemble; dU is its full base measure, with all its physical integrations.
Admit only cutoffs with positive denominator and positive M_{R,A},M_{R,B}.
Let P_i(a) be the actual physical perimeters, with P_A(a) != P_B(a).
Define the real m_R and positive z_{R,90} by the two CONDITIONS

  exp[m_R P_A(a)] z_{R,90}^(-4) M_{R,A}(a) = 1,
  exp[m_R P_B(a)] z_{R,90}^(-4) M_{R,B}(a) = 1.               (WC.Z3)
