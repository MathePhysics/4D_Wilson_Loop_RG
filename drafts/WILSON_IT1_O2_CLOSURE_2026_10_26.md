## What the Objects Are and What That Forces

- *The label Λ₁ is not a free label.* CRE defines the first small-field domain from the step's own sums: Λ₁ = Ω₁^{∼−2} ∩ (R₁′^∼)ᶜ with R₁′ the LMR₁-cover of R₁ (CRE (1.20), p.251 l.2–6), followed by the filling of the small components of Λ₁ᶜ (p.251 l.8–14), and R₁ is determined by S₁ (p.253 l.40: "the set S₁ determines the set R₁"; p.255 l.7–8: R_j = S_j ∖ (Ω_j ∩ (Ω_jᶜ)^∼)). So Λ₁ = Λ₁(Ω₁, S₁). In the defect construction Λ₁ is CRE's unfilled domain and Λ₁^{(0)} = Fill(Λ₁) is CRE's filled one (D2.6 closure l.105 (b); IT-1 l.738); both are functions of (Ω₁, S₁). CRE displays Λ₁ as an outer label of (1.28) and sums S₁ inside 𝐓₁ ((1.29), p.254 l.5–6; p.257 l.49–50), and it writes the same resummation for every later step: "We write this sum as a sum over domains Λ_{k+1}, and for a fixed domain Λ_{k+1}, as a sum over the admissible sets S_{k+1}" (p.270 l.2–3). Hence the sum over (Ω₁, Λ₁, Λ) with S₁ inside is the sum over (Ω₁, Λ) with S₁ inside and Λ₁ := Λ₁(Ω₁, S₁) evaluated in the integrand (§3.1). The "extra label" carries no summation index of its own.
- *What this forces for the later steps.* The step-1 density with the defect is a sum of terms of CRE's form (2.18) at k = 1 with the outer labels (Ω₁, Λ₁, Λ) and the inner label S₁, in which Λ plays CRE's Λ₁, the outer label Λ₁ is a parameter of the first-step factors only and is a function of (Ω₁, S₁), and the first one-step operation 𝐓^{(0)} has, at fixed outer labels, the form (2.21) with Z₁ = Λᶜ, the covariance C^{(0)}(Λ), and a characteristic function χ(Z₁ ∩ Ω₁ ∩ X) that includes CRE's large-fluctuation function of R₁ and three more indicator factors (§3.2). Equivalently, by the resummation of §3.1, the outer labels are (Ω₁, Λ) and Λ₁(Ω₁, S₁) stands in the integrand of the S₁-th term. CRE's inductive assumption leaves the 𝐓-operations "undefined, only their basic general properties are formulated" (p.254 l.37–40), so the inductive form at k = 1 is satisfied by this term exactly as by CRE's own (§3.2). The later steps 2 ≤ k ≤ N act on 𝐓_k as a carried factor at three places only ((3.1) p.264 l.47, the display p.267 l.46–53, and (3.23)–(3.25) p.270 l.8–31) and never evaluate the first step's χ (§4.1); the large-field induction of LFR II pp.384–389 takes from step 1 the numbers κ₁(Z), the index j(Z) = 1, and the geometric property (1.76), per component Z of Z₁ = Λᶜ (§4.2). All of these are already established on the labelled term (IT-1 §3f(2) Minor 4, §3j(6); RC_PAYMENT_PASS), and none depends on how Λ₁ is displayed.
- *The fine variables on Ret(Λ).* They are fluctuation variables A₀ on bonds with both endpoints in Λᶜ and one in Λ₁, i.e. on Z₁ ∩ Ω₁ with Z₁ = Λᶜ: the variables (2.21) integrates in 𝐓^{(0)}. CRE's convention (2.3), Z_j = Λ_jᶜ, governs them; the determining set (2.2) is a set of block variables V and is unchanged by the retreat (§3.3).

## 2. Λ₁ Is a Function of CRE's Labels (Ω₁, S₁), Locally per Component of Λᶜ

- *(a) Globally.* CRE (1.20), p.251 l.2–6: "R₁′ the union of LMR₁-cubes intersecting the set R₁", Λ₁ = Ω₁^{∼−2} ∩ (R₁′^∼)ᶜ, S₁ = (Ω₁ ∖ Ω₁^{∼−1}) ∪ R₁. R₁ = S₁ ∖ (Ω₁ ∩ (Ω₁ᶜ)^∼) is the printed definition (p.255 l.7–8; p.253 l.40), so R₁ is a function of (Ω₁, S₁), and so is Λ₁. The filling (p.251 l.8–14: components of Λ₁ᶜ "contained in cubes of sizes smaller than 100LMR₁" are replaced "by the smallest rectangular parallelepipeds containing them") is a function of Λ₁. So Λ₁ (unfilled) and Λ₁^{(0)} = Fill(Λ₁) are functions of (Ω₁, S₁), and so are 𝔅₀ = (Λ₁^{(0)})ᶜ and I = D(Λ₁) = {b ∈ E_{Ω,*} : b has an endpoint in Λ₁} (D2.6 closure l.105 (a)–(b); IT-1 §3g(1) l.1422).
- *(b) Per component.* Let X be a ∗-component of Λᶜ (IT-1 l.741: the regions of (2.19) are the ∗-components of Λᶜ; distinct ones are at ℓ^∞-distance ≥ ℓ). Every connected subset of Λᶜ lies in one X. Λ₁ᶜ = (Ω₁ᶜ)^{∼2} ∪ R₁′^∼ ⊂ Λᶜ, and each of its two parts is a union of connected sets, each of which is the 2-layer neighbourhood of a component of Ω₁ᶜ or the 1-layer neighbourhood of a component of R₁′; so (Ω₁ᶜ)^{∼2} ∩ X = (Ω₁ᶜ ∩ X)^{∼2} and R₁′^∼ ∩ X = ((R₁ ∩ X)′)^∼, and Λ₁ᶜ ∩ X = Λ₁ᶜ(Ω₁ ∩ X, S₁ ∩ X). Each component of Λ₁ᶜ lies in one X, and the filling acts on components, so Λ₁^{(0)ᶜ} ∩ X = Fill(Λ₁ᶜ ∩ X) and 𝔅₀ ∩ X is a function of (Ω₁ ∩ X, S₁ ∩ X) (this is IT-1 l.741–743's "each component of Ω₁ᶜ and of 𝔅₀ lies in one X" and "χ_{Λ,X} depends on 𝔅₀ only through 𝔅₀ ∩ X", now with 𝔅₀ ∩ X itself a function of the component's labels). A bond of I ∖ D(Λ) has no endpoint in Λ (D(Λ) is the set of bonds of I with an endpoint in Λ, IT-1 l.1422), so it lies in Λᶜ, in one X, and (I ∖ D(Λ)) ∩ X is the set of bonds of X with an endpoint in Λ₁ ∩ X: a function of (Ω₁ ∩ X, S₁ ∩ X).
- *(c) The carriers of the observable do not depend on Λ₁.* D(Λ) = {b ∈ E_{Ω,*} : b has an endpoint in Λ}, since a bond with an endpoint in Λ ⊂ Λ₁ is in I; and J(Λ) = E_{Ω,*} ∖ D(Λ) (D2.6 closure l.105 (a), with Λ for Λ₁). So the reference and t > 0 species, the words, and the leading word, which read the carrier D(Λ) and the exterior data on J(Λ) (IT-1 §3g(1), §3j(0) l.1699), depend on Λ₁ only through the values of the exterior data A^{Λ,Ψ}, i.e. through CRE's change of variables Ψ_ξ at Λ₁, a function of the block variables V and of Λ₁ (IT-1 l.718: "V3 keeps U₁(Ω₁∩Λ₁ᶜ,V), S₁ and Ψ_ξ of the original Λ₁") and through which bonds of the term carry box factors ((b)); both are functions of the integration variables of 𝐓^{(0)} and of (Ω₁, S₁).

## 3. The Step-1 Term in CRE's Inductive Form at k = 1

### 3.1 The Resummation Identity

- IT-1 l.739 writes the refined output as Σ_{Ω₁,Λ₁,Λ} χ₁(Ω₁) T₁^{Λ₁,Λ}[F^Λ(1) · exp A₁^Λ], with T₁^{Λ₁,Λ} = Σ_{S₁ : Λ₁(Ω₁,S₁) = Λ₁} ∫ (…) as CRE's (1.29) has Σ_{S₁} inside 𝐓₁({Ω₁,Λ₁}) (p.254 l.5–6). In every summand Λ₁ = Λ₁(Ω₁, S₁) (§2(a)), so

  Σ_{Λ₁} T₁^{Λ₁,Λ}[…] = Σ_{S₁} ∫ (…)|_{Λ₁ := Λ₁(Ω₁,S₁)} =: 𝐓^{(0)}_Λ[…],

  and the step-1 density is Σ_{Ω₁,Λ} χ₁(Ω₁) 𝐓^{(0)}_Λ[F^Λ(1) · exp A₁^Λ]. The range Λ ⊂ Λ₁^{(0)} needs no side condition: χ_Λ = 0 outside it (IT-1 l.739). Two equivalent readings of the same sum, both used below:
  - *(Ω₁, Λ₁, Λ) outer, S₁ inner.* This is CRE's (2.18) at k = 1 with one more outer label, Λ₁, which is a parameter of the step-1 factors (T₁^{Λ₁,Λ}, V^{(0)}(S₁) in 𝐁^{(1)}) and a function of (Ω₁, S₁). At fixed outer labels the term factorises over the components of Λᶜ exactly (IT-1 (4⁗)(d)), as CRE's own does at fixed (Ω₁, Λ₁) (§3.2 item 4). The checks of §3.2 are made in this reading.
  - *(Ω₁, Λ) outer, S₁ inner, Λ₁ = Λ₁(Ω₁, S₁) in the integrand.* This is CRE's own label set, and it shows that the extra outer label has zero entropy given (Ω₁, S₁): Σ_{Λ₁} Σ_{S₁ compatible with Λ₁} = Σ_{S₁}. The label count of §4.2 is made in this reading.
  CRE's own first step has the same two readings: its Λ₁ is Λ₁(Ω₁, S₁) by (1.20), displayed as an outer label in (1.28) with S₁ inside 𝐓₁ (p.254 l.5–6), and p.270 l.2–3 repeats the display at every step.
- The integrand's dependence on (Ω₁, S₁) is CRE's kind: p.257 l.49–52, "Summation over the sequences {S_j} is included in the operation 𝐓_k, and the effective action A_k depends on the sequences {Ω_j}, {Λ_j}, {S_j}"; p.259 l.2–3, "All the expressions above depend on the sequences {Ω_j}, {Λ_j}, the expression 𝐁_k depends also on the sequence {S_j}". The defect adds, to the integrand of the S₁-th term, factors that depend on Λ₁(Ω₁, S₁): the box factors on I ∖ D(Λ), the collar tests, χ_Λ (through 𝔅₀), and the exterior data of the observable through Ψ_ξ (§2(c)). These are dependences on the inner label S₁ and the outer label Ω₁, which CRE's form admits for every factor of 𝐓_k and A_k.

### 3.3 The Fine Variables on Ret(Λ): The (2.3) Convention

- *Which variables.* Ret(Λ) := Λ₁^{(0)} ∖ Λ (D2.6 closure l.105 (c)). The de-younged variables are the A₀-variables on I ∖ D(Λ), the bonds of I with an endpoint in Λ₁ and none in Λ (§2(b)); their endpoints lie in Λᶜ ∩ Λ₁ = Ret(Λ) ∪ (Λ₁ ∖ Λ₁^{(0)}) and in Λᶜ ∩ Ω₁ (I ⊂ E_{Ω,*}). So they are fluctuation variables A₀ on bonds of Z₁ ∩ Ω₁, Z₁ = Λᶜ, carrying the box indicator {|a_old|_σ < r} (IT-1 §3j; l.733: "the box factors on I ∖ D(Λ)").
