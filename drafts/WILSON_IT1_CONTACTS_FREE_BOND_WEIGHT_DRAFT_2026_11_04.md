## 1. The Weight

- Let Λ be the large-field region of the T-step that evaluates at a point, with its layers Λ_j, the weights W_j, and W_ms := max_jW_j of LF #A.11.42, whose distances t^{(j)} and t₃^{(j)} are in the units of L^j and capped below by ρ₀ = C(d)log L (LF #0.1.18-4). A distance at a bond is read at its midpoint. κ is LF's exponent (LF #0.1.18-4; table #T12.8), and 0 < κ < 1, since LF's α is below 2 (LF #A.7).
- A *free bond* is a bond of Λ's VAR problem that is not a data bond of Λ₀ (LF #A.10). At a free bond b put w_{c,j}(b) := max(1, (L^{1/2}/t₃^{(j)}(b))^{1−κ}) and w_c(b) := max_jw_{c,j}(b), the contact weight.
- *The stratum weight.* Let α_P be the exponent of the passage draft's Lemma B (its §5; #5.10.5-1), γ_S := min(α_P, 1)/2, s the fine distance from b to the strata of codimension ≥ 3 of the interface Λ₀/Λ₁ (the passage's ∂C; the passage draft, §3 (b₀)), s̄ := max(s, ρ₀), r_S := min(κ, 1 − γ_S), and a_S := max(½, ½(1 + (κ − γ_S)/r_S)).
  - Then r_S > 0 and r_S > κ − γ_S, since 0 < γ_S ≤ ½ and 0 < κ < 1, so a_S < 1. Put w_S(b) := max(1, (L^{a_S}/s̄(b))^{r_S}).
- Put w(b) := max(w_c(b), w_S(b)) at a free bond, and w := 1 at every other point. κ, γ_S, r_S, and a_S depend on d only.
- *The size of w.* Since t₃^{(j)}, s̄ ≥ ρ₀ ≥ 1, w_c ≤ L^{(1−κ)/2} and w_S ≤ L^{a_Sr_S}. So 1 ≤ w ≤ L^{c_w}, with c_w := max((1−κ)/2, a_Sr_S) < 1, since a_S < 1 and r_S ≤ κ < 1.
- On the layers n ≤ k₀, which contain Λ (#47), the phase room of 𝒟^λ_k is λw(b) times its unit, in place of λ times it (#52.1); on the layers n > k₀ it is unchanged. The gradient and current rooms carry instead the factor μ^{(k)}_n of §4a, on every layer (#52.1-1).
- *w varies slowly among the free bonds.* t₃^{(j)} and s̄ are 1-Lipschitz and ≥ ρ₀ ≥ 2 for L ≥ L₀(d) (LF #A.11.42-1), and the midpoints of two bonds that share a site are at distance ≤ 1. So for two free bonds b, b′ that share a site, t₃^{(j)}(b′) ≥ (1 − 1/ρ₀)t₃^{(j)}(b), and w_c(b′) ≤ (1 − 1/ρ₀)^{−(1−κ)}w_c(b) ≤ 2w_c(b); likewise w_S(b′) ≤ (1 − 1/ρ₀)^{−r_S}w_S(b) ≤ 2w_S(b). Hence w(b′) ≤ 2w(b).
