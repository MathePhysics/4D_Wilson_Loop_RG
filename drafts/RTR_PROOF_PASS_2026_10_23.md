# R-tr: definition-by-definition pass (started 23:31, 23 Oct 2026, date)

Task (user, 23 Oct, "Yes" to the offer): check every printed definition that D2.3 uses on ℓ₀ and in (Z) for an input other than the lattice, the partitions and the fields (a fixed origin, a coordinate-dependent ordering of positions, an unstated choice). Outcome: R-tr derived (covariance under translations preserving every partition follows from the definitions), or a named non-canonical input with its repair.

Translations considered: τ by a multiple of Π_{k+1} step-k bonds (D2.3(2)), which preserves every partition of steps 1, …, k+1 (nesting, D2.3(1)). Coordinate directions are preserved by τ, so a rule that refers to the coordinate axes (orders of axes, positive/negative directions) is covariant; a rule that refers to absolute positions (an order of sites or cubes, a distinguished site) is not, unless the result does not depend on it.

All are grids through 0 with sides powers of L. A cube of side L^a centred at p is the union of the L^{(a−b)d} grid cubes of side L^b in it, and p is the centre of the middle one (L^{a−b} odd). So p is a site of every T^{(k)}, k ≤ K, and a cube centre of every grid; the covers by unions of 2^d cubes (RG1 p.270, PBG's 𝒟_j) are centred at grid vertices, not at p, and are mapped to themselves since the vertex lattice aℤ^d (a | s_max) is; each p_μ is an odd multiple of half the coarsest side s_max, so x_μ ↦ 2p_μ − x_μ and the coordinate permutations about p (p_μ − p_ν ∈ s_maxℤ) map every grid and lattice to itself.
