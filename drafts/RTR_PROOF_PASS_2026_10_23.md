# Translation Covariance on ℓ₀: The Printed Definitions

{#TC.0} *Statement.* On ℓ₀ (Ω_j = Λ_j = T for every j, no large fields) let τ be a translation by a multiple of Π_{k+1} step-k bonds along each axis (map #MD2.3.4.1). τ maps every partition and cube family of the steps 1, …, k+1 to itself (map #MD2.3.4.2). Every object of these steps that the map uses on ℓ₀ is carried by τ to the same object built from the translated data. This is the input of map #MD2.3.4.4: c_{τ𝒢} = c_𝒢 and Φ°(τΓ) = Φ°(Γ).
{#TC.0-1} *Method.* Each definition is checked for an input other than the lattice, the partitions, the fields, and the arcs of C. A rule that refers to the coordinate axes (an order of the axes, a positive direction) is covariant, since τ preserves the axes. A rule that refers to absolute positions (a distinguished site, an order of sites or cubes) is covariant only if its result does not depend on that reference. Where a print leaves a choice free, a covariant convention is fixed (Section 8).
{#TC.0-2} Sources are the transcriptions Balaban\tex\<paper>\parts\pNNN_corrected.tex (REG: pNNN.tex). In RG1's references (p.299), [12] is CMP98, [13] PBG, [14] REG, [15] VAR, and [16] UV3.

## 8. Results

- {#TC.8-1} *Printed.* The invariance of each summed term 𝐄^{(j+1)} under every Euclidean transformation of T^{(j+1)} (RG1 p.263 and Theorem 3, with Bałaban's proof for 𝐄^{(k+1)} on p.269); the covariance of the z-localised terms (CRE (2.29)); and, on ℓ₀, of the new terms (CRE (3.39)), under all Euclidean transformations of the lattice. These are stronger than map #MD2.3.4.4 needs.
- {#TC.8-2} *Derived.* The localised objects whose covariance is not printed are the polymer activities H(Z) and 𝐄^{(k+1)}(X) of RG2 (2.9)–(2.13), the s-localised walks, and the run of RG2 (2.1)–(2.11) with marked supports in map #MSD2.2.5. Sections 1–7 build them from the lattice, the grids, the fields, and fixed one-variable profiles. Every rule refers to grid cubes, block centres, the axes, or sums over all subsets, walks, graphs, or points, and none uses a fixed origin or an order of positions. So τ carries each object to the object of the translated data.
- {#TC.8-3} *Conventions for the free choices.* Each is covariant by construction and admissible, since the prints' results hold for every choice with the stated properties.
  1. ζ̃_□ (RG1 p.274) is the translate, by the centre of □, of one fixed function ζ̃ invariant under the hypercubic group about the origin; ζ̃ exists because the support conditions are hypercubic symmetric, and every ζ̃_□ has the same derivative bounds.
  2. The random-walk localisation domains (RG2 p.13) are PBG Sect. C's grid families. X₀ of #TC.5-8-1 is the largest one, the union of the M₁-cubes Q with Q̃⁵ ⊆ Z̃₀. If some X₀′ has X̃₀′⁵ = Z̃₀, then every cube of X₀′ is such a Q, so X₀′ ⊆ X₀ and X̃₀⁵ ⊆ Z̃₀ = X̃₀′⁵ ⊆ X̃₀⁵.
  3. A tie in PBG p.411's "closest" cube is resolved by the equal-weight average of the expansions over the m tied choices. The average is exact by #TC.6-3-1; each term is a term of one single-partition expansion with weight 1/m, m bounded, so the per-term structure of PBG pp.411–412 and the summed bounds are unchanged; and the rule uses only the grids.
  4. The one-variable profiles ζ of RG1 p.270 and h of PRT1 (1.118) are taken even (Section 9). CRE (3.40)'s h is even as printed.
  These choices are local and lift to T′ ⊃ T with the other local choices (the lifted-partition convention).

## 9. Hypercubic Covariance

{#TC.9-1} Covariance of the step under the hypercubic group about the placement point of C (map #MD2.3.4.9) is not claimed. RG1 p.265 writes the fluctuation fields in REG (1.19)'s axial gauge followed by REG's u_k; REG p.80 notes that the restricted transformations "do not agree with the group structure", and p.81 takes "the axial gauge given by the Eq. (1.19)" as the preliminary gauge, built on CMP98's axis-ordered contours.
  {#TC.9-1-1} 𝐇_k(B′) and 𝐇_{k+1}(□₀, ·) (RG1 p.265, p.276) and the activities built from them are therefore not shown invariant under permutations of the axes. The map uses translations only, and keeps κ^{(k+1)}_v and ζ^{(k+1)}_{v,w} per orientation and corner pair (map #MD2.3.5.12-3).
{#TC.9-2} The free choices of #TC.8-3 are nevertheless taken hypercubic symmetric. The tie average is invariant under every symmetry of the tied set, the walk-domain families are symmetric about block centres, and ζ̃ is hypercubic invariant.
{#TC.9-3} *Even profiles exist.* Take 0 < δ < 1/6 and φ smooth with φ = 0 on (−∞, ⅓ + δ] and φ > 0 beyond, and put β(s) := φ(s)/(φ(s) + φ(1 − s)). The denominator is positive, since s ≤ ⅓ + δ implies 1 − s ≥ ⅔ − δ > ⅓ + δ. So β is smooth, β = 0 on [0, ⅓ + δ], β = 1 on [⅔ − δ, ∞), and β(s) + β(1 − s) = 1.
  {#TC.9-3-1} Put ζ(t) := 1 − β(|t|) and h(t) := cos(½πβ(|t|)). Both are even, smooth (β(|t|) = 0 near t = 0), equal to 1 for |t| ≤ ⅓ and to 0 for |t| ≥ ⅔. On [0, 1] only the translates n = 0, 1 are nonzero, and there |t − 1| = 1 − t, so ζ(t) + ζ(t − 1) = 2 − β(t) − β(1 − t) = 1 and h²(t) + h²(t − 1) = cos²(½πβ(t)) + sin²(½πβ(t)) = 1. By periodicity Σ_nζ(t − n) = 1 and Σ_nh²(t − n) = 1.
{#TC.9-4} RG1 p.270 also requires that "ζ has derivatives up to the second order bounded by 5". No ζ with the printed supports satisfies this. ζ(⅓) = 1, ζ(⅔) = 0, and ζ′ vanishes at both points, so a bound A on |ζ″| gives 1 ≤ |ζ(½) − 1| + |ζ(½)| ≤ 2·A(1/6)²/2, that is, A ≥ 36. The map uses no derivative bound of ζ.
