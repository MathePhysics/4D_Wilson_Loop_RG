## What the Objects Are

The step-(k+1) fluctuation field is one field. CRE (3.15) (pp.267–268) integrates one fluctuation field A_k over the bonds of Ω_{k+1}, under χ_k′(Ω_{k+1}), and every term of its action is evaluated at the configuration U_k(exp i[g_kCA_k − hD̃(g_kCA_k)]V^{(k)}), or is a term of 𝐏^{(k)}(g_k, U_{k+1}, A_k), or is the Gaussian form −½⟨A_k, C*Δ^{(k)}CA_k⟩. CRE (3.25) (p.270) renames the restrictions: "Here A_k denotes the fluctuation field on Ω_{k+1} ∩ Λ_{k+1}^c, and A denotes this field on Λ_{k+1}", and writes the same action as 𝐏^{(k)}(g_k, (A_k, A)) + {…} + V^{(k)}(S_{k+1}, (A_k, A)). So the pair B := (A_k, A) is the field A_k of (3.15), and the outer part A_k (the project's σ_k) is a variable of the same kind as the integration variable A, entering every term through the same formulas.

The real support. On Ω_{k+1} the function χ_k′ gives |A_k′| < O(1)ε_k with A_k′ = g_kA_k (CRE p.267: (3.14) and "We define A_k′ = (1/i)log V_k′, hence |A_k′| < O(1)ε_k also"); the O(1) is read off (3.14): c₃ := 2A₁/A₀ + (4dL)²(1 + β₀)B₃, since 2δ_k = 2(A₁/A₀)ε_k. After (3.21) the bonds of Ω_{k+1} ∩ Λ_{k+1}^c carry χ^{(k)} (g_k|A_k| < δ_k) off R_{k+1} and χ_k′(S_{k+1}) on R_{k+1} ⊂ S_{k+1}, so g_k|A_k(b)| < c₃ε_k on every bond of Ω_{k+1} ∩ Λ_{k+1}^c. On Λ_{k+1} the field A has g_k|A| < δ_k (χ^{(k)}(Λ_{k+1}), read in the phase variable: the D2.6 closure's R-fr (a)).

The composite field. B′ := g_kCB − hD̃(g_kCB) on the bonds of the unit lattice in Ω_{k+1} (CRE (3.15); RG1 (3.2) p.270; RG2 (1.19) p.6), with C and D̃ the operators "determined by the sequence {Ω_j}" (CRE p.267: "All the applied formulas are the same as in Sect. 2 [I], only the symbols represent different operators, determined by the sequence {Ω_j}. Their properties are essentially the same as before, because of the localization of the field A_k"). C is linear (RG1 p.268: "B′ = CB, C is the operator determined by the configuration V^{(k)}"; it eliminates the variables B′(b₀(c)) through δ(Q̃B′), block by block); h is supported on {b₀(c)} and D̃ is the analytic solution of a block-local equation (RG1 p.267), so B′ is "a local function of B" (RG2 p.3 and p.4). The bound is RG2 (1.20): |B′| ≤ O(1)g_k|B| + 4C₂(O(1)g_k|B|)² ≤ C₁^{RG2}g_k|B| < C₁^{RG2}ε₁ for g_k|B| < ε₁, "where C₁ is an absolute constant".

The terms of O6. The action of (3.27) is −⟨A, C*Δ^{(k)}CA_k⟩ + 𝐏^{(k)}(g_k, (A_k, A)) + {…}_{s,t} + tV^{(k)}(S_{k+1}); that of (3.29) is the same with (tA_k, A), the 𝐄_k-bracket only, and no V^{(k)}. The bracket {…} is (𝐄_k + 𝐑_k″ + 𝐁_k)(U_k(exp iB′V^{(k)})) − (…)(U_{k+1}) + A(g_k^{−2}(·) − g_k^{−2}, U_k(exp iB′V^{(k)})) − A(…, U_{k+1}) (CRE (3.15), p.268, with 𝐑_k′ included into 𝐄_k, p.271). After the localisation of CRE p.276 these are sums over Y ∈ 𝐃_k of localised terms; the terms with Y ⊂ Λ_{k+1} "satisfy exactly all the conditions of those lemmas", and the terms with Y ⊄ Λ_{k+1} are stated on the space (3.43), Ũ^c_{k+1}(Y, (1+β)α̃₀, (1+β)α̃₁) × {A : supp A ⊂ Y ∩ Λ_{k+1}^{(k)*}, |A| < C₁^{CRE}p₁(g_k)}, which has no A_k variable. O6 asks for the joint bound of these terms at complex A_k = σ_k on D(k+1)(iii)'s ω-polydiscs.

What that forces. Since B′ is a local function of B and every localised term depends on B′ restricted to the interior of Y (RG2 (1.34)–(1.35), p.9: the term "depends on configurations 𝐔, 𝐉, B restricted to the interior of Y"), a term with Y ⊂ Λ_{k+1} does not depend on A_k at all, and a term with Y ⊄ Λ_{k+1} depends on A_k restricted to Y ∩ Λ_{k+1}^c. The A_k-dependence is therefore confined to exactly the terms O6 names, and it enters them only through B′ and the entire Gaussian form (and, for V^{(k)}, through the change of variables of CRE p.269 feeding both). The question is then whether the functions of the variable field B′ that RG2 §1 constructs extend analytically when B′ is complex on the bonds of Ω_{k+1} ∩ Λ_{k+1}^c, on which domain, and what their bounds are there; §1–§3 answer this.

### §2. The σ_k-Dependence of the Actions of (3.27) and (3.29) Passes Through B′ Alone, Apart from Entire Quadratic Forms (Derived)

(a) 𝐏^{(k)}. RG1 (2.12) (p.268) lists its terms: Tr log(I − h(δD̃/δB)(g_kCB)), log σ(g_kCB − hD̃(g_kCB)), (1/g_k²)⟨H₁hD̃₃(g_kCB), J⟩, −(1/g_k²)𝐆₃(g_kCB), (1/g_k²)⟨H₁g_kCB, Δ₁H₁hD̃(g_kCB)⟩, −(1/g_k²)⟨H₁hD̃(g_kCB), Δ₁H₁hD̃(g_kCB)⟩, −(1/g_k²)V(H₁(g_kCB − hD̃(g_kCB))). Each is a function of A′ := g_kCB and of D̃(A′), D̃ analytic in A′ (RG1 p.267, which states the analyticity without a radius; the radius that matters is the composite's, RG2 (1.20) for g_k|B| < ε₁); the composite B′ = A′ − hD̃(A′) is analytic in A′. On the multi-domain lattice the same formulas hold with the {Ω_j}-operators (CRE p.267, quoted above). With B = (A_k, A) the whole field of (3.15), A_k enters 𝐏^{(k)}(g_k, (A_k, A)) exactly as A does: through A′ = g_kC(A_k, A). 

(b) The brackets. 𝐄_k, 𝐑_k″, 𝐁_k and A(g_k^{−2}(·) − g_k^{−2}, ·) are evaluated at U_k(exp iB′V^{(k)}) (CRE (3.15), p.268), a function of the configuration exp(iB′)V^{(k)} on the unit lattice, hence of B′. (c) The quadratic forms. −⟨A, C*Δ^{(k)}CA_k⟩ is bilinear in (A, A_k), hence entire in A_k; it is the D2.6 closure's source term (§2.2 (2), derived there with K = C*Δ_kC through the random-walk expansion; the map l.956: "linear in σ_j, hence entire"). The full Gaussian form −½⟨A_k, C*Δ^{(k)}CA_k⟩ of (3.15) is part of "the previous action" whose change under the change of variables enters V^{(k)} (p.269: "All terms of the previous action contribute to it"); it is entire in A_k, with the nonlocal kernel K := C*Δ^{(k)}C, and is handled in (d) and §6.

(d) V^{(k)}(S_{k+1}, A_k, 𝐇^{(k)}). CRE p.269: for b ∈ S_{k+1}^{(k)*} the variable A_k(b) is changed by (1.22) with 𝐇^{(k)}(b) in place of 𝐇_{1,Ax}(b) (the cutoff g(A_k′(b)) kept on (R_{k+1}^{∼2} ∩ S_{k+1})^{(k)*}, replaced by 1 elsewhere), and "The action is changed by terms corresponding to (1.24), and by terms obtained by the first order expansion of the action with respect to the perturbative parts of the changes of variables." Writing Ψ_b for the change of variables at frozen cutoff value θ_b (the map l.958: Ψ_b(σ) = (1/ig_k)log(exp(ig_kσ(b))exp(iθ_b𝐇^{(k)}(b))), analytic in σ for g_k|σ| small), the action S(B) of (a)–(c) evaluated at the old field is S((Ψ(σ_k), A)) = S((σ_k, A)) + ∫₀¹ ⟨(δS/δA_k)((σ_k + u(Ψ(σ_k) − σ_k), A)), Ψ(σ_k) − σ_k⟩ du, and the integral is V^{(k)}'s action part, while (1.24)'s Jacobian terms are its other part. ### §3. The Analytic Extension in (A_k, A) Jointly on R-m's Domain (Derived from §1–§2, R-m, and RG2 (1.19)–(1.20))

On 𝒫_k^m (Notation) the map B ↦ A′ = g_kCB is linear and bounded by O(1)g_k|B| (the first term of (1.20)), the composite B′ = A′ − hD̃(A′) is analytic and satisfies (1.20): |B′(b)| ≤ C₁^{RG2}g_k sup_Y|B| < r_{α,k} on every bond of Y. By §1 every localised term T(Y; 𝐔, 𝐉, B′) is analytic in B′ on the B′-domain stated for it: {e^{16κ₁}|B′| ≤ a₁ on Y} for 𝐏^{(k)}'s terms (RG2 p.10; absolute, no α₂-condition), and, for the bracket terms 𝐄_k, 𝐑_k″, 𝐁_k, A(·), the domain |B′| < r_{α,k} on which the shift exp(iη𝐇_k(B′)) keeps the configuration inside the g-scaled analyticity spaces of CRE (2.28), (2.34)–(2.39) (R-m, p.277; the shift's size is (1.21): |𝐇_k| ≤ 4B₀C₁^{RG2}e^{16κ₁}g_k|B|, and the margin is min(β,1)α_{1,k}/2, map l.697–701). RG2's own radius ε₁/g_k for the brackets (p.7's 4B₀C₁^{RG2}e^{16κ₁}ε₁ ≤ ¼α₂ with RG1's absolute α₂) is the single-domain statement and is not available for CRE's boundary terms, whose spaces are g-scaled; r_{α,k} ≪ ε₁ is the operative radius. The composition T(Y; 𝐔, 𝐉, B′(B)) is therefore analytic in B on 𝒫_k^m|_Y (analytic maps compose), jointly in A_k and A, uniformly over (𝐔, 𝐉) in the term's analyticity space. The Cauchy integrals in s(Δ) and t_□ (RG2 (1.23), (1.38); RG1 (3.15)) preserve this: their integrands are analytic in (s, t_□, B) jointly, the s-circles are B-independent, and the t_□-radius (1.22) depends on B only through sup|B|; since the integrand is analytic in t_□ on the annulus between any two admissible radii, the integral is locally independent of the radius and so analytic in B. Where [I]'s single-domain operators are replaced by CRE's {Ω_j}-operators, CRE p.267 and p.276 ("Thus, for terms connected with such a cube □, we obtain all the formulas and bounds of Sects. 3, 4 [I] with η replaced by L^{−n}"; "The same remarks apply to the expression 𝐏^{(k)}, and we obtain Lemmas 5, 6 [I] with the corresponding changes") carry §1's statements over; these are Bałaban's, accepted, and they concern the operators, not the variable B, so the composition argument is unchanged.

Conclusion of §3: every term of the expansion of the actions of (3.27) and (3.29) with Y ⊄ Λ_{k+1} (𝐏^{(k)} with the 𝐄_k-bracket, RG2 (1.41); the 𝐑_k″-bracket, CRE p.276 "treated in exactly the same way as 𝐄_k"; the (I.3.6)–(3.7)-localised 𝐁_k-terms, p.276; A(·)'s terms, p.277 "treated as above"; the action part of V^{(k)} by §6 below) extends to a function analytic in B = (A_k, A) on Ũ^c_{k+1}(Y, (1+β)α̃₀, (1+β)α̃₁) × 𝒫_k^m|_Y, depending on B restricted to Y; (3.43)'s A-factor, |A| < C₁^{CRE}p₁(g_k) with A supported in Y ∩ Λ_{k+1}^{(k)*}, is Bałaban's analyticity space in the integration field, and §7 relates it to 𝒫_k^m. The analytic extension in A_k that O6 asked for is this function, on R-m's domain.

### §4. The Consumers' Points Lie in 𝒫_k^m (Derived; under (R-m′) or the Constant Condition)

The consumers (D2.6 closure §5.0, §2.2, §4.3) evaluate at σ_k = σ_{k,0} + ζ with σ_{k,0} real in the support and |ζ(b)| < r_{ω,k} = g_k^{1/2} per bond, at tσ_k with t ∈ [0, 1] for (3.29), at the slot-value moves |Δσ_j| ≤ 4S_H/g_j ≤ r_{ω,j}/4 (map l.222, l.976–979), and with A real on the support. The field fed to B′ is B = (A_k′, A) with A_k′ = Ψ(σ_k) on S_{k+1}^{(k)*} and A_k′ = σ_k elsewhere (§2(d)). Bounds per bond:

- g_k|σ_{k,0}(b)| < c₃ε_k off S_{k+1}^{(k)*} (the real support, "What the Objects Are"), and < c₃ε_k + 3h_k on S_{k+1}^{(k)*}, where the support of the new variable σ_k after the change of variables is derived from the old support by the chart bound of the next item (CRE p.269 states the new support constant only as "similar"; it is not read, the derived value is used);
- g_k|ζ(b)| < g_k^{3/2};
- g_k|Ψ_b(σ_k) − σ_k(b)| ≤ (2 + O(ε_k))|𝐇^{(k)}(b)| ≤ 3h_k, where the factor 2 + O(ε_k) is the norm bound of the derivative of the chart logarithm X ↦ (1/i)log(e^{iy}e^{−iX}) for |y| + |X| ≤ c₂ (map l.978, derived there, with the O(ε_k) from the size of y; the segment from X = 0 to X = θ_b𝐇^{(k)}(b) lies in that ball), and |𝐇^{(k)}(b)| ≤ h_k covers the real backgrounds (c_He^{−R_k}ε_k, p.269) and the complex backgrounds of D(k+1)(iii) (S_H, map l.222);

### §6. The Action Part of V^{(k)} (Derived)

By §2(d) the action part of V^{(k)} on a localisation domain Y is V_act(Y) = ∫₀¹ ⟨(δT(Y)/δA_k)(B_u), Ψ(σ_k) − σ_k⟩ du, summed over the localised terms T(Y) of §3, and it is nonzero only when Y meets S_{k+1} (the support of Ψ − id), which is CRE p.269's "additional localization in S_{k+1}" and p.277's "at least one localization in S_{k+1}". It is analytic in σ_k on the consumers' domain as an integral of directional derivatives of the analytic functions of §3 at points B_u ∈ 𝒫_k^m (§4). Its bound is a Cauchy estimate in the direction δ := Ψ(σ_k) − σ_k: for F analytic on {B_u + zδ : |z| ≤ ρ/sup|δ|}, |D_δF(B_u)| ≤ sup|F|·sup|δ|/ρ. Take ρ := r_{α,k}/(2C₁^{RG2}g_k), half R-m's radius in the fluctuation field; under (C3), C₁^{RG2}ε_k^♯ < r_{α,k}/2 (strict), the closed disc lies in the open domain 𝒫_k^m, so sup|F| is the §5 bound of T(Y) with the fluctuation factor ε_k′ replaced by r_{α,k}, and sup|δ| ≤ 3h_k/g_k (§4). Since the §5 bound is linear in the fluctuation factor, |V_act(Y)| ≤ [the §5 bound of T(Y) per unit fluctuation factor]·r_{α,k}·(3h_k/g_k)·(2C₁^{RG2}g_k/r_{α,k}) = [the §5 bound of T(Y) per unit fluctuation factor]·6C₁^{RG2}h_k, which is p.269's "bounded … by O(1)ε_kexp(−R_k), hence by any positive power of g_k" with the constants named (h_k is e^{−R_k}-small), and is smaller than the §5 bound of T(Y) itself (fluctuation factor ε_k′ ≥ C₁^{RG2}c₃ε_k) once 6h_k ≤ c₃ε_k (condition (C4), true for g_K small since R_k ≥ (log g_k^{−2})^r with r ≥ 2, CRE p.245, (2.4)–(2.7), and S_H ≤ g_k^{3/2}/16). The Gaussian-form part of V_act (§2(d)): −⟨K(σ_k, A) + ½Kδ, δ⟩ with K = C*Δ^{(k)}C; per bond b of S_{k+1}^{(k)*} it is bounded by Σ_{b′}|K(b, b′)|·(ε_k^♯/g_k + 3h_k/(2g_k))·(3h_k/g_k) ≤ ‖K‖_{decay}·(2c₃ε_k/g_k)·(3h_k/g_k) under (C2), with ‖K‖_{decay} the row-sum norm of K's random-walk expansion (the D2.6 closure §2.2 (2), where the same kernel localises the source term), and the sum over b′ ∈ Λ_{k+1} is exponentially small since S_{k+1} is separated from Λ_{k+1} by a layer of LMR_{k+1}-cubes ((3.20)), so the part coupling to A has size ≤ ‖K‖_{decay}e^{−cLMR_{k+1}}(2c₃ε_k/g_k)(3h_k/g_k); its ratio to the real-case value (δ at the real point, |σ_k| ≤ c₃ε_k/g_k) is ≤ 2 by the same arithmetic. The Jacobian part is the map l.958's (derived there: bond-local, analytic in σ_k at frozen θ, of size O(|𝐇^{(k)}|)). This closes "through R-x V^{(k)}".

### §8. Count Check

Per scale: count × size × weight. For a cube Δ of Ω_{k+1}∖Λ_{k+1} (an M-cube of π_k on the layer k, a 2ML^n-cube on the layer n), the terms with Y ∋ Δ and d_k(Y) = m number at most e^{cm} with c absolute (this is what RG2 (1.26), Σ_{X∋□′}exp(−κd_j(X)) ≤ O(1) "for κ sufficiently large", encodes); the size of each is ≤ E₀ε_k′C₁M^qe^{C₂κ₁} (§5); the weight is e^{−(1 − 2δ)κm}, and a Kotecký–Preiss weight e^{a·m(Y)} costs at most e^{24am} since m(Y) = M^{−4}|Y| ≤ 3·2³d_k(Y) (RG2 (1.28)).
