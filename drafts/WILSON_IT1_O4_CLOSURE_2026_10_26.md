# O4 Closure: The Carried t > 0 Species at Complex Backgrounds

Companion of WILSON_IT1_D26_CLOSURE_2026_10_24.md ("the closure") and of WILSON_IT1_LINE_DEFECT_2026_10_21.md ("IT-1"). It bounds the carried t > 0 species (Υ¹_W, the terms of 𝓑^Λ, and the marks) as analytic functions of the later steps' block field 𝐔. Sources: the closure; IT-1 (D1(iii)′, §3e(3.1), §3e(3.6), §3e(3.12), §3e(4), §3j); the map WILSON_IT1_D2_STEP_MAP_2026_10_22.md; the step-1 paper V4 (WILSON_FIRST_RG_STEP_V4.tex, cited by label); the transcriptions Balaban\tex\<paper>\parts\pNNN_corrected.tex of CRE pp.254–256 and 276–277, RG2 pp.6–7, and RG1 pp.262–263.

The carried t > 0 species, the leading word, and the lw slots of a term are the terms of IT-1 §3j(4)'s representation at a real centre tm̂(U′, ω₀,₀), with U′ a real background on the species' region and ω₀,₀ the term's real exterior data of step 1. For every fixed real centre this is one expansion of the term with fixed members and lineages, exact at every real point and analytic in every field (§1). At step n it is read at the centre of the step's own external background 𝐔(U_{n+1}), which is independent of the step's fluctuation, in the step's gauge frame (§2(c)).
Every evaluation point of every step lies in the polydisc 𝒫(𝐔(U_{n+1})) (§2(b)), so the step-n bounds hold with constants independent of the centre; the carry lemma reads fixed members at every step, and the change of centre between two steps is the change between two exact expansions of the same term. The bounds are IT-1 §3e(3.12)'s with the tilt constant (1 + C_ω)C_δ (§1(d), §2(e)) and no new per-bond factor: the costs of the complex tilt are inside closure 7.1's C_h and the host bound ω̃′_t (§3).

## What the Objects Are and What That Forces

*The species are terms of one expansion of one integral.* On a labelled term with final region Λ, V4 integrates the young variable a ∈ 𝔤^{D(Λ)} against K^Λ_{ξ;t}(a) = 𝟏_{𝒟^Λ}(a)exp[−½⟨a,Ha⟩ − t⟨a,ℓ_Λ⟩ + P^Λ_t(a)] (V4 sec:c5-4), with H = H_{D(Λ)D(Λ)}, ℓ_Λ = H_{D(Λ)J(Λ)}A^{Λ,Ψ}, and the cutoff 𝟏_{𝒟^Λ}(a) = Π_{b∈D(Λ)}𝟏{|a_b|_σ < r} on the support of the exterior cutoffs (V4 sec:c5-4, the paragraph before eq:c5-4-1; the norm is |·|_op or |·|_HS, V4 eq:marginal-two-norms and eq:literal-cutoff).
Every cutoff is an indicator of one bond's a_b (V4 proof of lem:c5-4-1, property (i): "each χ_b a Borel function of a_b alone with values in [0,1]"). The translation B := a − tm (V4 sec:c5-4-2) centres the Gaussian: by eq:c5-4-2, ∫F(a)K^Λ_{ξ;t}(a) da = e^{½t²⟨m,Hm⟩}∫F(B+tm)Θ^t(B)exp[−½⟨B,HB⟩ + Σ_Y𝐖_t(Y,B)] dB with χ^t_b(B) = 𝟏{|B_b + tm_b|_σ < r}, Θ^t = Π_bχ^t_b, 𝐖_t(Y,B) = 𝐕^Λ_{ξ,t}(Y,B+tm), and the constant e^{½t²⟨m,Hm⟩} "is the same in the numerator and the denominator of every conditional expectation" (eq:c5-4-3) "and is never estimated".
The species are the terms of the polymer and source-insertion expansions of these normalised integrals (V4 lem:c5-4-1, lem:c5-4-2, sec:c5-5): cluster integrals of products of Mayer factors, cutoffs, cutoff differences (V4 lem:c5-5-6), and the source, against the conditioned Gaussian density V4 eq:fixed-kernel-density. The shift m is a parameter of the expansion, not of the integral: any real vector m with |m_b|_σ ≤ r/4 (eq:c5-4-1 for V4's m = −H^{−1}ℓ_Λ; IT-1 §3j(3) for the local m̂) gives a valid expansion of the same integral, and different shifts give different species. Closure 6.4 *The centre* uses this.

*What a complex background does.* At a complex 𝐔 = e^{ia′}U₀ with ‖a′‖_∞ < ϱ₁ := α_{1,1}/L around a real base point U₀ (IT-1 §3e(0), D1(iii)′), the data H_𝐔, ℓ_𝐔, and the primitives are analytic in 𝐔 (IT-1 §3e(3.1), (3.6)), but the real integration variable can only be translated by a real vector.
The frozen-centre representation (F) translates by the real tm̂₀, m̂₀ := m̂(U₀), and puts the residual linear term −t⟨B,δ^𝐔⟩, δ^𝐔 := H_𝐔m̂₀ + ℓ_𝐔, into the host potentials as a tilt (IT-1 §3e(3.12)); nothing but analytic data enters it (§1).

*The carried factor.* (F) expands the same integral at every real point of the polydisc (IT-1 §3e(3.12) *The centre*: "The translation is exact for every real centre with |m̂₀_b|_σ ≤ r/4"), is analytic on the polydisc (§1(c)), and is bounded there by §1(d). At the base point it is the real step-1 species of D(1)(ii).
The later steps use the carried factors on polydiscs around real base points (map D2.2(6)(e); closure 5.0), through their values at real points, their bounds, and Cauchy estimates on the polydisc (closure 5.1–5.2); none uses a single analytic function on Bałaban's whole space U^c_j(X, α₀, α₁, γ₀) of RG1 p.262 (Definition, (1.11)–(1.14)). Their labels and dependence sets are those of IT-1 §3j(4) and (6), and their bounds are §1(d)'s, with constants independent of the centre; this is all the carry lemma reads.

*One centre per term.* The leading word and the lw slots are the expansion of the source f_ξ at B = 0 (IT-1 §3j(4) *Identity*; closure 6.5), so they are terms of the same expansion as the t > 0 species and use its centre: at step n the leading word is f_ξ(m^{(n)}) and the lw slots are Y_i at m^{(n)} (§2(c); at 𝐔, IT-1 §3e(4) and (6) for f_ξ(m̂₀)). The touching of the lw slots acts through the stencil background only.

## 1. The Frozen-Centre Representation on a Polydisc

*(a) Definition.* Let U₀ be a real background in the support of a term's indicators, 𝒫(U₀) := {𝐔 = e^{ia′}U₀ : ‖a′‖_∞ < ϱ₁} its polydisc (IT-1 §3e(0), D1(iii)′), and m̂₀ := m̂(U₀) the real local shift of IT-1 §3j(1) (closure 6.4 *Shift*), with |m̂₀,b|_σ ≤ r/4 and h(m̂₀,b) ≤ 2C_mϑ_VrΣ_{e∈J₁(Λ)}e^{−μ_xd(b,e)} (IT-1 §3j(3), (5)); J₁(Λ) ⊆ J_col(Λ) (V4 sec:c5-2-3, sec:c5-5-0), so the bound holds with J_col(Λ) as well.
For 𝐔 ∈ 𝒫(U₀) the real translation a = B + tm̂₀ is IT-1 §3e(3.12) *The completed square* with U₁ = U₀: the linear term becomes −t⟨B,δ^𝐔⟩, δ^𝐔 = δ̂ + δℓ, with the real tilt δ̂ := H_{U₀}m̂₀ + ℓ_{U₀} of IT-1 §3j(3) and the complex tilt δℓ := (H_𝐔 − H_{U₀})_{DD}m̂₀ + (H_𝐔 − H_{U₀})_{DJ}A^{Λ,Ψ}, and the constant e^{−t²(½⟨m̂₀,H_𝐔m̂₀⟩ + ⟨m̂₀,ℓ_𝐔⟩)} cancels (*The constant*).
By IT-1 §3e(3.12) *The size of δℓ*, h_ℂ(δℓ(b)) ≤ C_δϱ₁ϑ_VrΣ_{e∈J_col(Λ)}e^{−μ_xd(b,e)}. δℓ vanishes beyond R_t + ρ_L of J_col(Λ): m̂₀ vanishes beyond R_t of J₁(Λ) (IT-1 §3j(2), (6) *Range*), the entries of H_𝐔 − H_{U₀} vanish for d(b,b′) > ρ_L, and every e ∈ J(Λ) within ρ_L of D(Λ) lies in J_col(Λ). At complex exterior data the tilt gains a third term, bounded in §2(e) by the factor 1 + C_ω on C_δ.
The (F)-species at 𝐔 are the terms of V4's expansions (lem:c5-4-1, lem:c5-4-2, sec:c5-5, as modified by IT-1 §3j(4)(a)–(e)) for the data H_𝐔, the fixed real cutoffs χ^t_b(B) = 𝟏{|B_b + tm̂₀,b|_σ < r}, and the tilted hosts W̃_t(Y,B;𝐔) of IT-1 §3e(3.12) *The tilt in the host potentials*, built from V4's 𝐏_t at 𝐔 and the probe hosts Y_b.

*(b) Exactness at real points.* At every real U ∈ 𝒫(U₀), (a) is V4's change of variables eq:c5-4-2 at U with the real vector m̂₀ in place of m: its proof completes the square and translates, which works for every real vector, and the linear term −t⟨B,δ^U⟩ is what remains when m̂₀ ≠ −H_U⁻¹ℓ_U. On the support of Θ^t every Θ^t_{E(Y)} is 1, so putting the tilt into the hosts is exact there (IT-1 §3e(3.12) *The tilt in the host potentials*).
So the (F)-species at a real U expand the same integral as any other real centre, with a different split between the reference Gaussian and the hosts; the integral, the conditional expectations (eq:c5-4-3), and Ξ^{f,t} are the same functions of U. At the base point the (F)-species are the real species of D(1)(ii) at U₀ in IT-1 §3j(4)'s form, since δ^{U₀} = δ̂.

*(c) Analyticity on the polydisc.* Every (F)-species is a finite sum of integrals ∫Φ(B;𝐔) dB, with V4's s- and σ-integrations, which do not involve 𝐔, over the fixed real set cut out by the bond factors χ^t_b (b ∈ E(Y₀)), 1 − χ^t_b (b ∈ 𝖯) and 𝟏_{S_b} at the real centre m̂₀, with Y₀ and 𝖯 of lem:c5-4-1 (V4 proof of lem:c5-5-6(a)); on it |B_b|_σ < 5r/4 for b ∈ E(Y₀). Φ(B;𝐔) is a product of the following factors.
  - The bond factors, which do not depend on 𝐔.
  - The Gaussian e^{−½⟨B,H_𝐔B⟩} and the conditioned density eq:fixed-kernel-density with C_{E₀}(𝐔) = (ι*H_𝐔ι)⁻¹, analytic in 𝐔: the entries of H_𝐔 are analytic, and ι*H_𝐔ι is invertible, its real part being ≥ γ − s > 0 (IT-1 §3e(1), (3.1), (3.3)).
  - The Mayer factors of W̃_t: 𝔭_𝐔 is analytic in the background on the enlarged box (IT-1 §3e(3.6) and §3e(2)(★)), and the tilt ⟨B_b,δ^𝐔(b)⟩ is linear in (H_𝐔, ℓ_𝐔).
  - The source f_ξ, analytic in 𝐔 (IT-1 §3e(4)), and the Dirac tensor's pushforward, which does not depend on 𝐔: the Dirac tensor is ⊗_{b∈𝖩}(δ_{B_b} − δ₀) ⊗ ⊗_{b∈𝖫_D∖𝖩}δ₀ (V4 eq:probe-selection-measure), a function of B and the label sets alone, and its integral against F is the finite difference eq:c4-4-1 of F at B.
  For fixed B the integrand is analytic in 𝐔 on 𝒫(U₀). For 𝐔 in a compact subset of 𝒫(U₀) its modulus is at most a constant times e^{−½(γ−s)|B|²}: for real B, |e^{−½⟨B,H_𝐔B⟩}| = e^{−½⟨B,(Re H_𝐔)B⟩} with Re H_𝐔 ≥ γ − s (IT-1 §3e(3.1)), likewise for the conditioned density, whose normalisation det C_{E₀} is a nonzero number (IT-1 §3e(3.3)), and the other factors are bounded: the hosts by V4's sup bounds (proof of lem:c5-5-6) with ω̃′_t for the tilted ones ((d)), and the source by IT-1 §3e(4). So the integrals converge locally uniformly in 𝐔, and each (F)-species is analytic on 𝒫(U₀).

*(d) The bound on the polydisc.* By IT-1 §3e(3.12) and (4), on 𝒫(U₀) the (F)-species obey V4's bounds (lem:c5-4-2, lem:c5-5-6, prop:c5-5-9, and the consumers of sec:c5-5R) with three changes, in which the exterior-data term of §2(e) multiplies the complex tilt's constants by 1 + C_ω:
  - (i) the host bounds ω_t and ω̄ are replaced by ω̃′_t := ω_t + e^{κs_p}((1 + C_ω)τ̄ + τ̂_N) and ω̄̃′ := ω̄ + e^{κs_p}((1 + C_ω)τ̄ + τ̂_N), IT-1 §3e(3.12)'s ω̃_t and ω̄̃ with τ̄ multiplied by 1 + C_ω (*Bounds*);
  - (ii) the host marks gain the tilt marks of §3e(3.12) *Tilt marks*, 𝔪^δ_Y ≤ (Ĉ^δ_bdC_she^{3μ_Hρ_L/20}g₀ + C^δ_bdϱ₁)ϑ_Vr²Σ_{b:Y_b=Y}Σ_{e∈J_col(Λ)}e^{−μ_xd(b,e)}, with IT-1 §3j(4)'s Ĉ^δ_bd and C^δ_bd := (1 + C_ω)C_δĈ^δ_bd;
  - (iii) the tilt threshold of §3e(3.12), a g₀-threshold, and the class-(β) factor 1 + (log g₀⁻²)^{(q₁−p₀)₊} of §3e(3.12) *Order*, since τ̄/ω_t ≍ (log g₀⁻²)^{q₁−p₀}.
  The mechanism is V4's for its marked terms (proof of lem:c5-5-6: "Take moduli; every factor other than the mark and the density is bounded by its supremum"): the fixed cutoffs are ≤ 1; the shells 𝟏_{S_b} are the real shells of m̂₀ (lem:c5-5-5, with reserve (1 − t|m̂₀,b|_σ/r)² ≥ 9/16); the tilted hosts obey §3e(3.12) *Bounds*; and the Gaussian payment at complex H_𝐔 is IT-1 §3e(3.4)'s, V4's lem:gaussian-payment(ii) at the kernel γ − s.
  In this bound the centre m̂₀ enters only through |m̂₀,b|_σ ≤ r/4 and the decay of h(m̂₀,b), both uniform in (U₀, ω₀,₀); the complex data enter only through sup bounds on 𝒫(U₀) and 𝒟^{sub}_{ω,0} (C_eϱ₁, C_Hϱ₁, ρ_ω, the primitives' majorants), uniform in the base point too. Hence the constants of the bound (ω̃′_t, ω̄̃′, τ̄, C_δ, C_ω, Ĉ^δ_bd, C^δ_bd, C_m, and V4's) are independent of the base point (U₀, ω₀,₀), which is the uniformity the carry needs (§2).

## 2. Analyticity Domains and Radii

*(a) What the consumers read.* The later steps use the carried factors of D(1)(iii) through (i) their values at real backgrounds; (ii) sup bounds on ω-polydiscs around real base points (map D2.2(6)(e); closure 5.0; closure 5.4: on 𝒟^{sub}_{k+1} the complex part of the background is bounded by the top-layer radius α_{1,k+1}L^{−(k+1)} per fine bond on every layer); (iii) Cauchy estimates for the touching (closure 5.1–5.2, (T2): on the (s,t)-polydisc of the group, with the factor 2/T_Y); and (iv) the exterior data at the complex points R(w_n(s,t))ω₀ of the current step (map #MD2.2.6.157, #MD2.2.6.196; (e) below).
Bałaban's analyticity spaces are of the same kind: U^c_j(X, α₀, α₁, γ₀) of RG1 p.262 (Definition, (1.11)–(1.14): 𝐔 = U′U with U′ = exp iξA′, |A′|, |∇A′| < α₁, a tube around the real configurations), with sup bounds there used through Cauchy's formula on circles whose radius is set by α₂ (RG2 p.7 (1.22); CRE p.277). No consumer uses a single analytic function on the union of all polydiscs.

*(b) Radii: every evaluation point of every step lies in the polydisc of every step's real background.* For a step n′ let U′ := 𝐔(U_{n′+1}) be the real background on the species' region at the step's fluctuation 0, a function of the step's external field U_{n′+1} alone. The region is the R_dep-neighbourhood of Z₁ = Λᶜ, R_dep = R_t + 6L + 2 ≤ 2ℓ (closure 6.2, 6.4), so it lies in 𝒩₁′, the 2ℓ-neighbourhood of Λᶜ, inside Ω₂ᶜ (closure 5.6).
On 𝒫(U′) the (F)-species at the centre tm̂(U′, ω₀,₀) are analytic (§1(c)) with the sup bound §1(d). At step n′ every evaluation point lies in D(n′)(iii)'s domain around the real U_{n′} (map #MD2.0.3.53 at n′ = 1), and so within 𝒫(U′) by the containment below, U′ and U_{n′} agreeing on the species' region up to the deformations bounded there.
  *The complex part.* At step n ≥ 2 the consumer evaluates on 𝒟^{sub}_n (closure 5.4), where the complex part of the background is ≤ α_{1,n}L^{−n} per fine bond, and the group's deformation over the (s,t)-polydisc adds ≤ β4^{−n}α_{1,n}L^{−n} per fine bond (closure 4.2 with T_Y ≥ 2). With α_{1,n} ≤ (1 + β₀)²(n − 1)^{1/2}α_{1,1} (CRE (2.6)–(2.7) at j = 1), α_{1,1} = Lϱ₁, and (n − 1)^{1/2} ≤ L^{n−2}, the complex part is ≤ (1 + β₀)²(1 + β/16)ϱ₁/L ≤ 3ϱ₁/8 for L ≥ 4(1 + β₀)² and β ≤ 1.
  *The real part.* The real backgrounds of two steps differ by the real deformations of the steps between them, U_m = (e^{iη_m𝐇_m(1,B′_m)}U_{m+1})^{g_m} (map #MOMGD.31–33, #MD2.2.4.32; g_m a real frame), of the size CRE p.277's 𝐇-decay gives (map #MOMGD.55): on the species' region η_m|𝐇_m| ≤ O(1)θ^{m−1}h_m/L per fine bond for m ≥ 2, with h_m ≤ α_{1,m}/2 (map #MD2.2.6.156).
  At m = 1 the region lies on the step's own layer, at distance ≥ (4L − 2)R₂ π₁-sides from Ω₂ (closure 5.6 *The bridge*), i.e. (4L − 2)MR₂ ≥ 3MR₁ ≥ R₁M₁ step-1 units (R₁ ≤ (L+1)R₂ by CRE (2.9), L ≥ 5, M ≥ M₁). That is one decay length of the walk-decay kernel behind CRE p.277's 𝐇-decay (VAR (190), as closure 4.1 uses it), so η₁|𝐇₁| ≤ O(1)θh₁ ≤ O(1)θLϱ₁/2.
  Closure 4.2's condition RM₁ ≥ 32log(4L·O(1))/δ₀ gives θ = e^{−δ₀RM₁/32} ≤ 1/(4L·O(1)) ≤ 1/(4L), so the m = 1 term is ≤ ϱ₁/8, and Σ_{k≥1}k^{1/2}θ^{k−1} ≤ (1 − θ)^{−2} ≤ 3/2 (θ ≤ 1/6 suffices). The sum over m ≥ 2 is ≤ (1 + β₀)²ϱ₁·(O(1)θ/2)Σ_{k≥1}k^{1/2}θ^{k−1} ≤ (1 + β₀)²ϱ₁·(3/2)/(8L) ≤ ϱ₁/16 for L ≥ 4(1 + β₀)². The phases add linearly up to Baker–Campbell–Hausdorff corrections of order ϱ₁² per bond, inside the margin below.
  Every evaluation point of every step is therefore within 3ϱ₁/8 + ϱ₁/8 + ϱ₁/16 = 9ϱ₁/16 < ϱ₁ per fine bond of the real background U′ = 𝐔(U_{n′+1}) of every step n′, inside 𝒫(U′), with the margin 7ϱ₁/16 to its boundary. The Cauchy estimates of closure 5.2 are taken on the (s,t)-polydisc with (T2)'s factor 2/T_Y, not on 𝒫(U₀), so no constant of closure 5.2 changes. L ≥ 4(1 + β₀)² is implied by the map's L ≥ L₀ = 5000 and β₀ ≤ 1/2.

*(c) The centre per step.* For a fixed real vector m with |m_b|_σ ≤ r/4 the (F)-construction at the centre tm is one expansion of the term with fixed members and lineages: the step-1 (F)-species at m, exact at every real point (§1(b)) and analytic in the background wherever the data are (§1(c)), and their descendants under the carry lemma (closure §5), which are analytic in every field, since the centre is a parameter and not a function of the fields.
For every such m the term is the sum of its m-members, and (T0)'s "fixed data, not vertices", (T1)'s anchoring, closure 4.5's "untouched members are evaluated at (U_{k+1}, ω)", and the hard core hold for the m-members at every step.
  For m = tm̂(U′, ω₀,₀) with U′ a real background on the species' region, §1(d) bounds the step-1 (F)-species at every 𝐔 ∈ 𝒫(U′) with constants independent of U′, and the carry lemma propagates the bounds to the descendants with the same constants as long as every evaluation point of every step lies in 𝒫(U′) (U′ is then *admissible* for the term). By (b) the real background 𝐔(U_{n+1}) of every step n on the region is admissible.
  At step n the term is read as the sum of its m^{(n)}-members, m^{(n)} := tm̂(𝐔(U_{n+1}), ω₀,₀), with U_{n+1} the step's external field (map #MOMGD.31–33: U_n = (e^{iη𝐇_n(B′)}U_{n+1})^g with U_{n+1} independent of B′). So m^{(n)} is independent of the step's fluctuation, the m^{(n)}-members are analytic in the step's (s,t)-deformation, their untouched values at t = 0 are the real species at the step's external data, and D(n)(iii)'s bounds hold for them on 𝒫(𝐔(U_{n+1})), which contains the step's evaluation points ((b)), with the constants of §1(d).
  The carry lemma at step n is applied to the m^{(n)}-members, and its output is the m^{(n)}-expansion of the term at step n+1; at step n+1 the term is read as the sum of its m^{(n+1)}-members, the same function. The bounds on the m^{(n+1)}-members at step n+1 come from the run of the carry lemma from step 1 with the centre fixed at m^{(n+1)}, with §1(d) and the containment (b) at each step. The two runs agree as functions because both are exact expansions of the same term, with the same labels, dependence sets R_dep = R_t + 6L + 2 (closure 6.4), decay rate μ_x (IT-1 §3j(5)), and constants (§1(d)).
  The centre must not depend on the fluctuation of the step at hand or of an earlier step: the step-1 real background on the region is (e^{iη𝐇₁(1,B′₁)}U₂)^{g₁}, a function of B′₁ and, through U₂, of every later fluctuation (map #MOMGD.33), so an untouched value at that centre would depend on B′₁, and (T0)–(T1) would fail.
  *Gauge frames.* At step n the term's variables on the region are read in the step's real gauge frame w, the background U^w and the exterior data R(w)ω₀. The (F)-construction is covariant: m̂(U^w, R(w)ω₀) = R(w)m̂(U, ω₀), and likewise every member with the centre R(w)m, because H_𝐔, the local inverses G_Δ, the parametrix, Σ_nK_𝐔ⁿ, the primitives, and the source are built by sitewise Ad-covariant operations and the rescaling m̂ = λm̃ uses a constant λ (closure 6.4 *Shift*). The complex rotation R(w_n(s,t)) of the exterior data is (e)'s.

*(d) The factor's continuation is unique.* The complex extension of a term at 𝐔 ∈ 𝒫(U₀) is defined by evaluating the step's construction at the complex data (H_𝐔, ℓ_𝐔, 𝔭_𝐔) with the real cutoffs (IT-1 §3e(4): "The indicators stay outside as factors"), which is (F). It is analytic on 𝒫(U₀) and agrees with the real factor on the real section, a totally real submanifold of maximal dimension of the connected 𝒫(U₀), so by the identity theorem it is the unique analytic function on 𝒫(U₀) with this property.
