# O4 Closure: The Carried t > 0 Species at Complex Backgrounds

Companion of WILSON_IT1_D26_CLOSURE_2026_10_24.md ("the closure") and of WILSON_IT1_LINE_DEFECT_2026_10_21.md ("IT-1"). It bounds the carried t > 0 species (Υ¹_W, the terms of 𝓑^Λ, and the marks) as analytic functions of the later steps' block field 𝐔. Sources: the closure; IT-1 (D1(iii)′, §3e(3.1), §3e(3.6), §3e(3.12), §3e(4), §3j); the map WILSON_IT1_D2_STEP_MAP_2026_10_22.md; the step-1 paper V4 (WILSON_FIRST_RG_STEP_V4.tex, cited by label); the transcriptions Balaban\tex\<paper>\parts\pNNN_corrected.tex of CRE pp.254–256 and 276–277, RG2 pp.6–7, and RG1 pp.262–263.

The carried t > 0 species, the leading word, and the lw slots of a term are the terms of IT-1 §3j(6)'s representation at a real centre tm̂(U′, ω₀,₀), with U′ a real background on the species' region and ω₀,₀ the term's real exterior data of step 1. For every fixed real centre this is one expansion of the term with fixed members and lineages, exact at every real point and analytic in every field (§1). At step n it is read at the centre of the step's own external background 𝐔(U_{n+1}), which is independent of the step's fluctuation, in the step's gauge frame (§2(c)).
Every evaluation point of every step lies in the polydisc 𝒫(𝐔(U_{n+1})) (§2(b)), so the step-n bounds hold with constants independent of the centre; the carry lemma reads fixed members at every step, and the change of centre between two steps is the change between two exact expansions of the same term. The bounds are IT-1 §3j(6)'s with the tilt constant (1 + C_ω)C_δ′ (§1(a), §2(e)) and no new per-bond factor: the per-bond costs of the complex tilt are inside closure 7.1's C_h and §3j(6)'s ω̂_t (§3).

## What the Objects Are and What That Forces

*The species are terms of one expansion of one integral.* On a labelled term with final region Λ, V4 integrates the young variable a ∈ 𝔤^{D(Λ)} against K^Λ_{ξ;t}(a) = 𝟏_{𝒟^Λ}(a)exp[−½⟨a,Ha⟩ − t⟨a,ℓ_Λ⟩ + P^Λ_t(a)] (V4 sec:c5-4), with H = H_{D(Λ)D(Λ)}, ℓ_Λ = H_{D(Λ)J(Λ)}A^{Λ,Ψ}, and the cutoff 𝟏_{𝒟^Λ}(a) = Π_{b∈D(Λ)}𝟏{|a_b|_σ < r} on the support of the exterior cutoffs (V4 sec:c5-4, the paragraph before eq:c5-4-1; the norm is |·|_op or |·|_HS, V4 eq:marginal-two-norms and eq:literal-cutoff).
Every cutoff is an indicator of one bond's a_b (V4 proof of lem:c5-4-1, property (i): "each χ_b a Borel function of a_b alone with values in [0,1]"). The translation B := a − tm (V4 sec:c5-4-2) centres the Gaussian: by eq:c5-4-2, ∫F(a)K^Λ_{ξ;t}(a) da = e^{½t²⟨m,Hm⟩}∫F(B+tm)Θ^t(B)exp[−½⟨B,HB⟩ + Σ_Y𝐖_t(Y,B)] dB with χ^t_b(B) = 𝟏{|B_b + tm_b|_σ < r}, Θ^t = Π_bχ^t_b, 𝐖_t(Y,B) = 𝐕^Λ_{ξ,t}(Y,B+tm), and the constant e^{½t²⟨m,Hm⟩} "is the same in the numerator and the denominator of every conditional expectation" (eq:c5-4-3) "and is never estimated".
The species are the terms of the polymer and source-insertion expansions of these normalised integrals (V4 lem:c5-4-1, lem:c5-4-2, sec:c5-5): cluster integrals of products of Mayer factors, cutoffs, cutoff differences (V4 lem:c5-5-6), and the source, against the conditioned Gaussian density V4 eq:fixed-kernel-density. The shift m is a parameter of the expansion, not of the integral: any real vector m with |m_b|_σ ≤ r/4 (eq:c5-4-1 for V4's m = −H^{−1}ℓ_Λ; IT-1 §3j(1) for the local m̂) gives a valid expansion of the same integral, and different shifts give different species. Closure 6.4 *The centre* uses this.

*What a complex background does.* At a complex 𝐔 in the ρ₁-polydisc around a real base point U₀ (‖a′‖_∞ < ρ₁ := α_{1,1}/L in fine units, IT-1 D1(iii)′ and §3e(4); CRE (2.39) at j = n = 1), the data H_𝐔, ℓ_𝐔, and the primitives are analytic in 𝐔 (IT-1 §3e(3.1), (3.6)). The real integration variable cannot be translated by a complex vector (IT-1 §3e(3.12) *The problem*).
The frozen-centre representation (F) translates by the real tm̂₀, m̂₀ := m̂(U₀), and puts the residual linear term −t⟨B,δℓ⟩, δℓ := H_𝐔m̂₀ + ℓ_𝐔, into the host potentials as a tilt (IT-1 §3e(3.12), §3j(6)); nothing but analytic data enters it (§1). The species built at U with the moving centre tm̂(U) are not used: V4's hosts carry their own cutoffs (eq:c-potential-definition), so the host-mark terms of lem:c5-5-6 contain lenses {|x|_σ ≥ r, |x + m_b|_σ < r}, whose integrals have a kink at m_b = 0.

*The carried factor.* (F) expands the same integral at every real point of the polydisc (the translation identity eq:c5-4-2 holds for every real vector; IT-1 §3e(3.12) *The tilt in the host potentials*: "the identity is exact"), is analytic on the polydisc, and is bounded there by IT-1 §3j(6) (with §3e(4)) at the frozen centre, with the complex tilt marks and the factor τ̄ (§1). At the base point it is the real step-1 species of D(1)(ii).
The later steps use the carried factors on polydiscs around real base points (map D2.2(6)(e); closure 5.0), through their values at real points, their bounds, and Cauchy estimates on the polydisc (closure 5.1–5.2); none uses a single analytic function on Bałaban's whole space U^c_j(X, α₀, α₁, γ₀) of RG1 p.262 (Definition, (1.11)–(1.14)). Their labels, dependence sets, and bounds are those of §3j(6), with constants independent of the centre (§1(d)), which is all the carry lemma reads.

*One centre per term.* The leading word and the lw slots are the expansion of the source f_ξ at B = 0 (IT-1 §3j(4) *Identity*; closure 6.5), so they are terms of the same expansion as the t > 0 species and use its centre: at step n the leading word is f_ξ(m^{(n)}) and the lw slots are Y_i at m^{(n)} (§2(c); IT-1 §3e(3.12) *What else uses m*). The touching of the lw slots acts through the stencil background only.

## 1. The Frozen-Centre Representation on a Polydisc

*(a) Definition.* Let U₀ be a real background in the support of a term's indicators, 𝒫(U₀) := {𝐔 = e^{iηa′}U₀ : ‖a′‖_∞ < ρ₁} its polydisc (IT-1 D1(iii)′), and m̂₀ := m̂(U₀) the real local shift m̂ = λm̃ of closure 6.4 *Shift*, with |m̂₀,b|_σ ≤ r/4 and h(m̂₀,b) ≤ 2C_mϑ_VrΣ_{e∈J₁(Λ)}e^{−μ_xd(b,e)} (IT-1 §3j(5)).
The latter follows from h(m̂_b) ≤ h(m_b) + ε_N𝟏{d(b,J₁(Λ)) ≤ R_N}, the bump bound 𝟏{d(b,J₁) ≤ R} ≤ e^{μ_xR}Σ_{e∈J₁}e^{−μ_xd(b,e)}, and q_s^{N+1}e^{μ_xR_N} ≤ e^{−μ_Hℓ_sN/4 + μ_Hρ_L/10}, since R_N = 5ℓ_s(N+2) + 2ρ_L, μ_x = μ_H/20, μ_xR_N = μ_Hℓ_s(N+2)/4 + μ_Hρ_L/10, and q_s^{N+1} ≤ e^{−μ_Hℓ_s(N+1)/2}. J₁(Λ) ⊆ J_col(Λ) (V4 sec:c5-4), so the same bound holds with J_col(Λ). For 𝐔 ∈ 𝒫(U₀) and B := a − tm̂₀ (a real translation),
  −½⟨a,H_𝐔a⟩ − t⟨a,ℓ_𝐔⟩ = −½⟨B,H_𝐔B⟩ − t⟨B,δℓ_𝐔⟩ − t²(½⟨m̂₀,H_𝐔m̂₀⟩ + ⟨m̂₀,ℓ_𝐔⟩),  δℓ_𝐔 := H_𝐔m̂₀ + ℓ_𝐔,
the identity of IT-1 §3e(3.12) with m̂₀ in place of m₀ (§3j(6): "At complex backgrounds (3.12)'s real shift becomes m̂₀ := m̂(U₁) … and the two tilts add").
The tilt splits as δℓ_𝐔 = τ̂_N(U₀) + (H_𝐔 − H_{U₀})m̂₀ + (H_𝐔 − H_{U₀})_{DJ}A^{Λ,Ψ}: the real tilt τ̂_N := H_{U₀}m̂₀ + ℓ_{U₀} of §3j(4) (of size C_shε_Nϑ_Vr for the local shift) and the complex tilt δℓ, bounded entrywise by h_ℂ(δℓ(b)) ≤ C_δ′ρ₁ϑ_VrΣ_{e∈J_col(Λ)}e^{−μ_xd(b,e)}, C_δ′ := C_ee^{μ_xρ_L}(4(2ρ_L+1)⁴·2C_m + 1).
Here C_e is the entrywise Cauchy constant of IT-1 §3e(3.1), ‖(H_𝐔 − H_{U₀})(b,b′)‖ ≤ C_eρ₁, the entries vanishing for d(b,b′) > ρ_L. The first term is bounded by the entry bound over the 4(2ρ_L+1)⁴ bonds b′ within ρ_L of b and h(m̂₀,b′) ≤ 2C_mϑ_Vre^{μ_xρ_L}Σ_{e∈J₁(Λ)}e^{−μ_xd(b,e)}; it is supported within R_t + ρ_L of J₁(Λ) (m̂₀ within R_t of J₁(Λ), §3j(6)).
The second is bounded by the direct count Σ_{e∈J_col(Λ): d(b,e)≤ρ_L}C_eρ₁ϑ_Vr ≤ C_eρ₁ϑ_Vre^{μ_xρ_L}Σ_{e∈J_col(Λ)}e^{−μ_xd(b,e)} (|A^{Λ,Ψ}(e)|_σ < ϑr on J_col(Λ), V4 lem:c5-3-1(b)); it vanishes beyond ρ_L of J_col(Λ). At complex exterior data the tilt gains a third term, bounded in §2(e) by the constant factor 1 + C_ω on C_δ′.
The (F)-species at 𝐔 are the terms of V4's expansions (lem:c5-4-1, lem:c5-4-2, sec:c5-5 with IT-1 §3j(4)(a)–(e)) for the data H_𝐔, the fixed real cutoffs χ^t_b(B) = 𝟏{|B_b + tm̂₀,b|_σ < r}, the hosts 𝐖_t(Y,B;𝐔) = Θ^t_{E(Y)}(B)𝐏_t(Y,B;𝐔) with 𝐏_t(Y,B;𝐔) = Σ_{𝔭 near, Y_𝔭=Y}𝔭_𝐔(B + tm̂₀ + tA^{Λ,Ψ}) (V4 proof of lem:c5-5-6 and eq:c-potential-definition), and the tilt put into the hosts, W̃_t(Y,B;𝐔) := 𝐖_t(Y,B;𝐔) − tΘ^t_{E(Y)}(B)Σ_{b:Y_b=Y}⟨B_b,δℓ_𝐔(b)⟩ (IT-1 §3e(3.12) *The tilt in the host potentials*, with the host assignment b ↦ Y_b of §3j(4)).
The constant e^{−t²(…)} is common to the numerator and the denominator of every ratio and is never estimated (V4 eq:c5-4-3).

*(b) Exactness at real points.* At every real U ∈ 𝒫(U₀), (a) is V4's identity eq:c5-4-2 for the integral at U with the real shift vector m̂₀ in place of m: eq:c5-4-2 is the change of variables a = B + tm for an arbitrary real vector m (its proof completes the square and translates), and the residual linear term −t⟨B,δℓ_U⟩ is what the completion of the square leaves when m̂₀ ≠ −H_U⁻¹ℓ_U. On Θ^t's support Θ^t_{E(Y)} = 1, so the tilt in the hosts is exact there (IT-1 §3e(3.12)).
So the (F)-species at a real U expand the same integral as any other real centre, with a different split between the reference Gaussian and the hosts; the integral, the conditional expectations (eq:c5-4-3), and Ξ^{f,t} are the same functions of U. The (F)-species at the base point are the real species of D(1)(ii) at U₀ in IT-1 §3j(4)'s form.

*(c) Analyticity on the polydisc.* Every (F)-species is a finite sum of integrals ∫_{box}Φ(B;𝐔) dB over the fixed real box {|B_b + tm̂₀,b|_σ < r, b ∈ E(Y₀)} × {|B_b|_σ ≥ 3r/4 or the shell, b ∈ 𝖯} (the supports of the fixed bond factors χ^t_b, 1 − χ^t_b, 𝟏_{S_b}; on the support |B_b|_σ < 5r/4, V4 proof of lem:c5-5-6). Φ(B;𝐔) is a product of the following factors.
  - The fixed indicators.
  - The Gaussian e^{−½⟨B,H_𝐔B⟩} and the conditioned density eq:fixed-kernel-density with C_{E₀}(𝐔) = (ι*H_𝐔ι)⁻¹, analytic in 𝐔: the entries of H_𝐔 are analytic, and the inverse is analytic where Re H_𝐔 ≥ γ − s > 0 (IT-1 §3e(3.1)).
  - The Mayer factors of W̃_t, analytic in 𝐔 because 𝔭_𝐔 is analytic in the background on the enlarged box (IT-1 §3e(3.6) and (★): "V3's majorants for the coefficient and its directional derivatives hold at complex backgrounds e^{iZ^c} … with the real shift m₀ and the tilt of (3.12)"), and the tilt ⟨B_b,δℓ_𝐔(b)⟩ is linear in (H_𝐔, ℓ_𝐔).
  - The source f_ξ, analytic in 𝐔 (IT-1 §3e(4)), and the Dirac tensor's pushforward, which is 𝐔-independent: the Dirac tensor is ⊗_{b∈𝖩}(δ_{B_b} − δ₀) ⊗ ⊗_{b∈𝖫_D∖𝖩}δ₀ (V4 eq:probe-selection-measure), a function of B and the label sets alone, and its integral against F is the finite difference eq:c4-4-1 of F at B.
  For fixed B the integrand is analytic in 𝐔 on 𝒫(U₀). On the closed box its modulus is bounded, uniformly for 𝐔 in a compact subset of 𝒫(U₀), by IT-1 §3e(3.1)'s complex-density bound (Re H_𝐔 ≥ γ − s, the modulus of the density bounded by the real density with the kernel γ − s), the host sup bounds of V4's proof of lem:c5-5-6, and ω̂_t for the tilted hosts ((d)). Differentiation under the integral sign gives: each (F)-species is analytic in 𝐔 on 𝒫(U₀).

*(d) The bound on the polydisc.* IT-1 §3j(6) with §3e(3.12) and (4): on 𝒫(U₀), the (F)-species obey V4's bounds (lem:c5-4-2, lem:c5-5-6, prop:c5-5-9, and the consumers of sec:c5-5R) with:
  - (i) the host bound ω_t replaced by ω̂_t := ω_t + e^{κ(B_h+4)}(τ̂-term of §3j(4) + τ̄), τ̄ := (5/4)C_σ(1 + C_ω)C_δ′N_lat(μ_x)·4(4M+2)⁴·ρ₁ϑ_Vr² (IT-1 §3e(3.12) *The tilt in the host potentials*, with (1 + C_ω)C_δ′ and μ_x for its C_δ and μ_H), the two tilts adding (§3j(6));
  - (ii) the host marks 𝔪^δ_Y ≤ C^δ_bdρ₁ϑ_Vr²Σ_{b:Y_b=Y}Σ_{e∈J_col(Λ)}e^{−μ_xd(b,e)} of §3e(3.12) *New host marks*, C^δ_bd := (5/4)C_σ(1 + C_ω)C_δ′·2e·e^{κ(B_h+4)}e^{1/8}/ω_*, added to V4's 𝔪_Y and to §3j(4)'s Ĉ^δ_bd-marks;
  - (iii) the tilt threshold of §3e(3.12), a g₀-threshold: τ̄/ω_t ≍ (log g₀⁻²)^{q₁−p₀} is paid by the class-(β) factor 1 + (log g₀⁻²)^{(q₁−p₀)₊} (§3j(6)).
  The mechanism is V4's for its marked terms (proof of lem:c5-5-6: "Take moduli; every factor other than the mark and the density is bounded by its supremum"; lem:c5-5-5; lem:gaussian-payment(ii)): the fixed cutoffs are ≤ 1; the shells 𝟏_{S_b} are the real shells of m̂₀ (lem:c5-5-5 at the real centre, with reserve (1 − t|m̂₀,b|_σ/r)² ≥ 9/16); the tilted hosts obey |W̃_t| ≤ |𝐖_t| + t·sup_{|B_b|_σ<5r/4}|⟨B_b,δℓ_𝐔(b)⟩|·#{b : Y_b = Y}, which is the τ̄ computation of §3e(3.12); and the Gaussian payment at complex H_𝐔 is IT-1 §3e(3.1) and (3.4)'s, V4's lem:gaussian-payment(ii) at the kernel γ − s.
  In this bound the centre m̂₀ enters only through |m̂₀,b|_σ ≤ r/4 and h(m̂₀,b)'s decay, both uniform in (U₀, ω₀,₀); the complex data enter only through sup bounds on 𝒫(U₀) and 𝒟^{sub}_{ω,0} (C_eρ₁, C_Hρ₁, ρ_ω, the primitives' majorants), uniform in the base point too. Hence the constants of the bound (ω̂_t, τ̄, C_δ′, C_ω, C^δ_bd, Ĉ^δ_bd, C_m, c_ℓ, and V4's) are independent of the base point (U₀, ω₀,₀), which is the uniformity the carry needs (§2).

*(e) The per-bond factor.* Relative to the real bound at U₀, the complex bound on 𝒫(U₀) costs (i) the host factor e^{κ(B_h+4)}τ̄ inside ω̂_t, a per-bond factor e^{O(1)N_hostρ₁ϑ_Vr²e^{−μ_xd(b,J₁(Λ))}} at the bonds within R_t + ρ_L of J₁(Λ), where δℓ is nonzero; (ii) the marks 𝔪^δ_Y; and (iii) the exterior-data term of §2(e), of the same form and size. Together: the factor e^{Cρ₁ϑ_Vr²e^{−μ_xd(b,J₁(Λ))}} per bond within R_t + ρ_L of J₁(Λ), and no factor elsewhere.

## 2. Analyticity Domains and Radii

*(a) What the consumers read.* The later steps use the carried factors of D(1)(iii) through (i) their values at real backgrounds; (ii) sup bounds on ω-polydiscs around real base points (map D2.2(6)(e); closure 5.0; closure 5.4: on 𝒟^{sub}_{k+1} the complex part of the background is bounded by the top-layer radius α_{1,k+1}L^{−(k+1)} per fine bond on every layer); (iii) Cauchy estimates for the touching (closure 5.1–5.2, (T2): on the (s,t)-polydisc of the group, with the factor 2/T_Y); and (iv) the exterior data at the complex points R(w_n(s,t))ω₀ of the current step (map #MD2.2.6.157, #MD2.2.6.196; (e) below).
Bałaban's analyticity spaces are of the same kind: U^c_j(X, α₀, α₁, γ₀) of RG1 p.262 (Definition, (1.11)–(1.14): 𝐔 = U′U with U′ = exp iξA′, |A′|, |∇A′| < α₁, a tube around the real configurations), with sup bounds there used through Cauchy's formula on circles whose radius is set by α₂ (RG2 p.7 (1.22); CRE p.277). No consumer uses a single analytic function on the union of all polydiscs.

*(b) Radii: every evaluation point of every step lies in the polydisc of every step's real background.* For a step n′ let U′ := 𝐔(U_{n′+1}) be the real background on the species' region at the step's fluctuation 0, a function of the step's external field U_{n′+1} alone. The region is the R_dep-neighbourhood of Z₁ = Λᶜ, R_dep = R_t + 6L + 2 ≤ 2ℓ (closure 6.2, 6.4), so it lies in 𝒩₁′, the 2ℓ-neighbourhood of Λᶜ, inside Ω₂ᶜ (closure 5.6).
On 𝒫(U′) the (F)-species at the centre tm̂(U′, ω₀,₀) are analytic (§1(c)) with the sup bound §1(d). At step n′ every evaluation point lies in D(n′)(iii)'s domain around the real U_{n′} (map #MD2.0.3.53 at n′ = 1), and so within 𝒫(U′) by the containment below, U′ and U_{n′} agreeing on the species' region up to the deformations bounded there.
  *The complex part.* At step n ≥ 2 the consumer evaluates on 𝒟^{sub}_n (closure 5.4), where the complex part of the background is ≤ α_{1,n}L^{−n} per fine bond, and the group's deformation over the (s,t)-polydisc adds ≤ β4^{−n}α_{1,n}L^{−n} per fine bond (closure 4.2 with T_Y ≥ 2). With α_{1,n} ≤ (1 + β₀)²(n − 1)^{1/2}α_{1,1} (CRE (2.6)–(2.7) at j = 1), α_{1,1} = Lρ₁, and (n − 1)^{1/2} ≤ L^{n−2}, the complex part is ≤ (1 + β₀)²(1 + β/16)ρ₁/L ≤ 3ρ₁/8 for L ≥ 4(1 + β₀)² and β ≤ 1.
  *The real part.* The real backgrounds of two steps differ by the real deformations of the steps between them, U_m = (e^{iη_m𝐇_m(1,B′_m)}U_{m+1})^{g_m} (map #MOMGD.31–33, #MD2.2.4.32; g_m a real frame), of the size CRE p.277's 𝐇-decay gives (map #MOMGD.55): on the species' region η_m|𝐇_m| ≤ O(1)θ^{m−1}h_m/L per fine bond for m ≥ 2, with h_m ≤ α_{1,m}/2 (map #MD2.2.6.156).
  At m = 1 the region lies on the step's own layer, at distance ≥ (4L − 2)R₂ π₁-sides from Ω₂ (closure 5.6 *The bridge*), i.e. (4L − 2)MR₂ ≥ 3MR₁ ≥ R₁M₁ step-1 units (R₁ ≤ (L+1)R₂ by CRE (2.9), L ≥ 5, M ≥ M₁). That is one decay length of the walk-decay kernel behind CRE p.277's 𝐇-decay (VAR (190), as closure 4.1 uses it), so η₁|𝐇₁| ≤ O(1)θh₁ ≤ O(1)θLρ₁/2.
  Closure 4.2's condition RM₁ ≥ 32log(4L·O(1))/δ₀ gives θ = e^{−δ₀RM₁/32} ≤ 1/(4L·O(1)) ≤ 1/(4L), so the m = 1 term is ≤ ρ₁/8, and Σ_{k≥1}k^{1/2}θ^{k−1} ≤ (1 − θ)^{−2} ≤ 3/2 (θ ≤ 1/6 suffices). The sum over m ≥ 2 is ≤ (1 + β₀)²ρ₁·(O(1)θ/2)Σ_{k≥1}k^{1/2}θ^{k−1} ≤ (1 + β₀)²ρ₁·(3/2)/(8L) ≤ ρ₁/16 for L ≥ 4(1 + β₀)². The phases add linearly up to Baker–Campbell–Hausdorff corrections of order ρ₁² per bond, inside the margin below.
  Every evaluation point of every step is therefore within 3ρ₁/8 + ρ₁/8 + ρ₁/16 = 9ρ₁/16 < ρ₁ per fine bond of the real background U′ = 𝐔(U_{n′+1}) of every step n′, inside 𝒫(U′), with the margin 7ρ₁/16 to its boundary. The Cauchy estimates of closure 5.2 are taken on the (s,t)-polydisc with (T2)'s factor 2/T_Y, not on 𝒫(U₀), so no constant of closure 5.2 changes. L ≥ 4(1 + β₀)² is implied by the map's L ≥ L₀ = 5000 and β₀ ≤ 1/2.

*(c) The centre per step.* For a fixed real vector m with |m_b|_σ ≤ r/4 the (F)-construction at the centre tm is one expansion of the term with fixed members and lineages: the step-1 (F)-species at m, exact at every real point (§1(b)) and analytic in the background wherever the data are (§1(c)), and their descendants under the carry lemma (closure §5), which are analytic in every field, since the centre is a parameter and not a function of the fields.
For every such m the term is the sum of its m-members, and (T0)'s "fixed data, not vertices", (T1)'s anchoring, closure 4.5's "untouched members are evaluated at (U_{k+1}, ω)", and the hard core hold for the m-members at every step.
  For m = tm̂(U′, ω₀,₀) with U′ a real background on the species' region, §1(d) bounds the step-1 (F)-species at every 𝐔 ∈ 𝒫(U′) with constants independent of U′, and the carry lemma propagates the bounds to the descendants with the same constants as long as every evaluation point of every step lies in 𝒫(U′) (U′ is then *admissible* for the term).
