## What the Objects Are and What That Forces

*The species are terms of one expansion of one integral.* On a labelled term with final region Λ, V4 integrates the young variable a ∈ 𝔤^{D(Λ)} against K^Λ_{ξ;t}(a) = 𝟏_{𝒟^Λ}(a)exp[−½⟨a,Ha⟩ − t⟨a,ℓ_Λ⟩ + P^Λ_t(a)] (V4 sec:c5-4), with H = H_{D(Λ)D(Λ)}, ℓ_Λ = H_{D(Λ)J(Λ)}A^{Λ,Ψ}, and the cutoff 𝟏_{𝒟^Λ}(a) = Π_{b∈D(Λ)}𝟏{|a_b|_σ < r} on the support of the exterior cutoffs (V4 sec:c5-4, the paragraph before eq:c5-4-1; the norm is |·|_op or |·|_HS, V4 eq:marginal-two-norms and eq:literal-cutoff). Every cutoff is an indicator of one bond's a_b (V4 l.2754, property (i): "each χ_b a Borel function of a_b alone with values in [0,1]"). The translation B := a − tm (V4 sec:c5-4-2) centres the Gaussian: by eq:c5-4-2, ∫F(a)K^Λ_{ξ;t}(a) da = e^{½t²⟨m,Hm⟩}∫F(B+tm)Θ^t(B)exp[−½⟨B,HB⟩ + Σ_Y𝐖_t(Y,B)] dB with χ^t_b(B) = 𝟏{|B_b + tm_b|_σ < r}, Θ^t = Π_bχ^t_b, 𝐖_t(Y,B) = 𝐕^Λ_{ξ,t}(Y,B+tm), and the constant e^{½t²⟨m,Hm⟩} "is the same in the numerator and the denominator of every conditional expectation" (eq:c5-4-3) "and is never estimated". The species are the terms of the polymer and source-insertion expansions of these normalised integrals (V4 lem:c5-4-1, lem:c5-4-2, sec:c5-5): cluster integrals of products of Mayer factors, cutoffs, cutoff differences (V4 lem:c5-5-6, *Marked Differences*), and the source, against the conditioned Gaussian density V4 eq:fixed-kernel-density. The shift m is a parameter of the expansion, not of the integral: any real vector m with |m_b|_σ ≤ r/4 (eq:c5-4-1 for V4's m = −H^{−1}ℓ_Λ; IT-1 §3j(1) for the local m̂) gives a valid expansion of the same integral, and different shifts give different species. This is the fact behind closure §6.4's remark that "the split between the reference Gaussian and the host tilts depends on the centre".

*What a complex background does.* At a complex 𝐔 in the ρ₁-polydisc around a real base point U₀ (‖a′‖_∞ < ρ₁ := α_{1,1}/L in V3's fine units, IT-1 D1(iii)′ l.902 and §3e(4) l.1131–1133; CRE (2.39) at j = n = 1), the data H_𝐔, ℓ_𝐔, and the primitives are analytic in 𝐔 (IT-1 §3e(3.1), (3.6)), and the local shift m̂(𝐔) = −λP_𝐔Σ_{n≤N}K_𝐔ⁿℓ_𝐔 is analytic too (closure 6.3 *Complex bound*, 6.4 *Shift*). The real integration variable cannot be translated by the complex vector tm̂(𝐔) (IT-1 (3.12) *The problem*). Two representations are available at 𝐔: (F) the frozen-centre representation, the translation by the real tm̂₀, m̂₀ := m̂(U₀), with the residual linear term −t⟨B,δℓ⟩, δℓ := H_𝐔m̂₀ + ℓ_𝐔, put into the host potentials as a tilt (IT-1 (3.12), §3j(6) l.1757); (M) the analytic continuation in 𝐔 of the real function U ↦ (species built at U with the centre tm̂(U)), which closure §6.4 takes as the carried factor. (F) is analytic in 𝐔 on the polydisc, since nothing but analytic data enters it (§2 below). (M) exists only if the real function is analytic in the background.

*Structural consequence 1: (M) does not exist for the marked species.* The source-insertion expansion telescopes the cutoffs and Mayer factors between t = 0 and t = 1 (V4 lem:c5-5-6; IT-1 §3j(4)(e), l.1730: "its measure is the t = 0 measure plus marked cutoff differences"). A marked term carries, for one bond b, the bond's own cutoff at one centre and a host's differenced cutoff at the other: V4 l.2974 bounds exactly such a product, "(1 − χ⁰_b)χ¹_b ≤ 𝟏_{S_b}(B_b)", S_b the shell r − |m_b|_σ ≤ |x|_σ < r + |m_b|_σ (V4 lem:c5-5-5). As a function of the shift m_b, the integral of an analytic integrand over {|B_b|_σ ≥ r, |B_b + m_b|_σ < r} is not analytic at m_b = 0 (§3 below: in one dimension its one-sided derivatives at 0 are −g(r) and g(−r)), and for the operator norm it is only piecewise analytic in the direction of m_b. The real species therefore cannot be asserted to be real-analytic in the background at a background where a marked bond has m̂_b = 0 (and are not whenever the remaining integrand is nonvanishing on the shell, §3(b)), and the ρ₁-polydisc around a base point may contain such backgrounds for the marked bonds b with |m̂_b(U₀)|_σ below the variation c_hC_Δρ₁ϑ_Vr C_μ of m̂_b over the polydisc (closure 6.3(d); §1 below): the far marks, whose weight ϖ_b ∝ |m̂_b|_σ is tiny but not zero. Whether a given base point has such a bond is not decided by the inductive hypothesis, which bounds |m̂_b|_σ from above only; what §3(b)–(c) show is that no radius of real-analyticity of the moving-centre species in the background beyond the distance of m̂_b(U₀) to the singular set of the lens integral can be asserted, so no radius uniform in the base point can be. So there is no analytic continuation of the marked species with the centre tm̂(𝐔) on polydiscs of a uniform radius, and O4 as posed ("the carried species' activities, as analytic functions of 𝐔") can only be answered for (F).

*Structural consequence 2: (F) at the base point of each polydisc is the carried factor.* (F) expands the same integral at every real point of the polydisc (the translation identity eq:c5-4-2 holds for every real vector; IT-1 (3.12) *The tilt in the host potentials*: "the identity is exact"), is analytic on the polydisc, and is bounded there by IT-1 §3j(6) (with §3e(4)) at the frozen centre, with the complex tilt marks and the factor τ̄ (§2). At the base point itself it is the real step-1 species of D(1)(ii). The later steps' consumers use the carried factors on polydiscs around real base points (map D2.2(6)(e); closure §5.0 "the att bounds over the ω-polydiscs"), through their values at real points, their bounds, and Cauchy estimates on the polydisc (closure §5.1–5.2); none uses a single analytic function on Bałaban's whole space U^c_j(X, α₀, α₁) of RG1 p.262 (Definition, (1.11)–(1.14)). So the carried t > 0 species of a term are the (F)-species at a real centre: for every fixed real centre they are one expansion of the term with fixed members and lineages, analytic in every field, which the carry lemma (closure §5) reads as such, and at step n the bounds are read at the centre m^{(n)} := tm̂(𝐔(U_{n+1}), ω₀,₀) of the step's own external background on the species' region, independent of the step's fluctuation, for which every evaluation point of every step lies inside 𝒫(𝐔(U_{n+1})) (§4(b)–(c)). Their labels, dependence sets, and bounds are those of §3j(6), with constants independent of the centre (§2(d)), which is all the carry lemma reads.

*Structural consequence 3: one centre per term.* The leading word and the lw slots are the expansion of the source f_ξ at B = 0 (IT-1 §3j(4) *Identity*; closure 6.5), so they are terms of the same expansion as the t > 0 species and must use its centre. Closure §6.4 gives them the moving centre m̂(𝐔) and the t > 0 species the open bound O4; with (F) the leading word on the polydisc is f_ξ(m̂₀) and the lw slots are Y_i at m̂₀, as IT-1 (3.12) *What else uses m* had it before the amendment of closure 6.4. Closure §6.1–6.4's bounds hold a fortiori (the complex sup bound 6.3(c) covers the real m̂₀; the Λ-dependence and the dependence sets are those of §3j(2) at U₀; the touching acts through the stencil background only). The amendment is for the user's review (§4(e)).

*Structural consequence 4: no new booking.* The per-bond costs of the complex background in (F) are the host tilt τ̄ (inside ω̂_t) and the complex tilt marks 𝔪^δ_Y ≤ C^δ_bdρ₁ϑ_Vr²Σ_{b:Y_b=Y}Σ_{e∈J_col(Λ)}e^{−μ_xd(b,e)} (IT-1 (3.12) *New host marks*, §3j(6) l.1757; with the constant C_δ′ of §2(a) and the constant factor 1 + C_ω of §4(f) for the exterior data), which closure 7.1 *Host marks* already carries inside C_h and hence inside C_{B,1} ⊂ C_A(1), with the remark "if O4 closes by its stated route (a host tilt at the centre tm̂(𝐔)), this term of C_h and K₁'s condition (7.2) are to be re-checked". ## 3. Why the Moving-Centre Continuation Does Not Exist

*(a) The host-mark terms carry cutoffs at two centres.* V4's hosts are defined with their own local-box cutoff: 𝐕_ξ(Y,a) = Σ_{𝔭 near, Y_𝔭=Y}𝔭(a) if |a(b)| < r for all b ∈ E(Y), and 0 otherwise (eq:c-potential-definition, l.701; l.990: "every selected host Y comes with the factors χ_b, b ∈ E(Y) ⊆ E(Y₀)"). Translated, 𝐖_t(Y,B) = Θ^t_{E(Y)}(B)𝐏_t(Y,B), Θ^t_{E(Y)} = Π_{b∈E(Y)}χ^t_b (proof of lem:c5-5-6, l.2958). The source-insertion telescoping between t = 0 and t = 1 ("Order these factors (bonds first, then hosts) and telescope … Each term has exactly one differenced factor, the mark") produces, at a host mark Y, a term whose bond factors are at t = 1 and whose differenced Mayer factor is
  M¹_Y − M⁰_Y = ∫₀¹[𝐏₁e^{t_Y𝐏₁} − 𝐏₀e^{t_Y𝐏₀}] dt_Y + (1 − Θ⁰_{E(Y)})∫₀¹𝐏₀e^{t_Y𝐏₀} dt_Y
(l.2962). The "cutoff part" (1 − Θ⁰_{E(Y)}(B))Π_{b∈E(Y₀)}χ¹_b(B) contains, for each b ∈ E(Y), the product χ¹_b(B_b)(1 − χ⁰_b(B_b)) = 𝟏{|B_b + m_b|_σ < r}𝟏{|B_b|_σ ≥ r}: cutoffs of the same bond at the two centres. (The differenced bond factors χ¹_b − χ⁰_b of the same telescoping are the differences 𝟏{|B_b + m_b|_σ < r, |B_b|_σ ≥ r} − 𝟏{|B_b|_σ < r, |B_b + m_b|_σ ≥ r} of the indicators of the two lenses, each of the same kind.) V4 bounds it by 𝟏_{S_b}(B_b) (l.2964, "on the support of χ¹_b, (1 − χ⁰_b)χ¹_b ≤ 𝟏_{S_b}(B_b)") and pays it with ϖ_b ≤ C_Vr^{d_G−1}|m_b|_σ (lem:c5-5-5); the second ϖ_b in 𝔪_b = 2ϖ_b is exactly this host-generated bond mark ("one for the cutoffs of the hosts containing b, paid by (c5-5-T)(iii)"). These terms are a genuine part of the expansion, not of its bound: the source-insertion bound (prop:c5-5-9) is the sum over them.

*(b) The one-sided lens is not analytic in the shift.* Fix such a term and the bond b, and substitute B_{b′} = y_{b′} − m_{b′} for the other bonds b′ ∈ E(Y₀) (their factors are at t = 1 and become fixed; the rest of the integrand, G say, is analytic in (B, m), since the Gaussian, the density, the undifferenced Mayer factors, and the source are analytic in B on the enlarged box, l.2958). The term's dependence on m_b is through
  I(m_b) := ∫_{|x|_σ ≥ r, |x + m_b|_σ < r} G(x; m) dx = ∫_{|y|_σ<r}𝟏{|y − m_b|_σ ≥ r}G(y − m_b; m) dy.
In one variable (d_G = 1, |·| the absolute value, G > 0 analytic and m-independent for the model): for 0 < m < 2r, I(m) = ∫_{−r−m}^{−r}G(x) dx, so I′(0⁺) = G(−r); for −2r < m < 0, I(m) = ∫_r^{r−m}G(x) dx, so I′(0⁻) = −G(r). The one-sided derivatives differ by G(−r) + G(r) > 0: I has a kink at m = 0 and is not analytic there. In general, for G nonvanishing on the shell (positive, after a choice of sign): I(0) = 0 and |I(m)| ≥ (min_{shell}|G|)·Leb{|x|_σ ≥ r, |x + m|_σ < r} ≥ c_r|m|_σ for small m (V4 lem:c5-5-5's ϖ_b is this volume's upper bound, of the same order), so |I(m) + I(−m)| ≥ 2c_r|m|_σ, whereas an analytic I with I(0) = 0 has I(m) + I(−m) = O(|m|²); for a G that changes sign on the shell nothing is asserted. The complementary lens {|x|_σ < r, |x + m_b|_σ ≥ r} gives the same conclusion with the two one-sided derivatives exchanged. For the operator norm the ball {|x|_op < r} has a non-smooth boundary (d_G ≥ 2), so I is only piecewise analytic in the direction of m_b even away from 0.

*(c) Consequence for the moving-centre species.* The real moving-centre species at the real background U is a sum of such terms composed with U ↦ m̂_b(U). For its real-analytic continuation in U, bond by bond, no radius (in the h_ℂ-metric of the shift) beyond the distance of m̂_b(U₀) to the singular set {m_b = 0} of I (and, for σ = op, to the non-smooth set) can be asserted, and none exists when the remaining integrand is nonvanishing on the shell ((b)). The inductive hypothesis bounds |m̂_b(U)|_σ from above (closure 6.3(c), IT-1 §3j(5)) and never from below; the displacement of m̂_b over 𝒫(U₀) is of the same order e^{−μ_xd(b,J_col(Λ))}ρ₁ϑ_Vr as the far bonds' |m̂_b(U₀)|_σ itself (§1), and at a base point with m̂_b(U₀) = 0 for a marked bond, if there is one, no positive radius can be asserted for that term. So no analytic continuation of the moving-centre species to 𝒫(U₀) with a radius uniform in U₀ can be asserted, and the object named in closure §6.4 *The step's shift convention* ("the analytic continuation of a carried t > 0 species has the centre tm̂(𝐔)") and in O4 is not available. What is available is (F) (§2), which is what IT-1 §3e(4) l.1131–1133 and §3j(6) l.1757 bound, and what §3e(4) names: "Υ¹_W, the words U_j and f_ξ(m₀) are analytic in a′", the leading word at the frozen m₀.

*(d) Where the obstruction would disappear (not derived here).* With hosts defined without their own cutoff (𝐏_t in place of 𝐖_t = Θ^t_{E(Y)}𝐏_t, bounded on the enlarged box {|a_b|_σ ≤ 2r} where V4 already has the primitives analytic with |𝔭| ≤ C_pg₀s̄³, l.2958 and rem:c5-4-5) every term of the telescoping is, after the substitution of (b), an integral of an analytic integrand over fixed sets or a plain difference χ¹_b − χ⁰_b, which integrated against an analytic G is ∫_{|y|<r}[G(y − m_b) − G(y)] dy, analytic in m_b. The moving-centre species would then be analytic in the background and O4 would close in its posed form. This is a change of V4's host definition (eq:c-potential-definition) with a re-check of every host bound that uses the local box (l.990: "only the values on the local boxes enter"); it is not needed, since (F) suffices for the consumers (§4), and it is recorded for the V3/V4 overhaul only.
