## 1. The Weight on ℓ₀ {#RWS1}

{#RW1.1} On ℓ₀, Ω₁ = Λ₁ = T, so the young carrier is I = Λ₁*, the retained carrier J = Ω₁*∖I is empty, and A_e ∈ 𝔤^J is the empty vector (V4 sec:branch-data; #MD2.3.4.54-9).
  {#RW1.1-1} *R₁ = S₁ = ∅.* CRE (1.20), p.251, gives Λ₁ = Ω₁^{∼−2} ∩ (R₁′^∼)^c, R₁′ being the union of the LMR₁-cubes meeting R₁, before the modification that replaces small components of Λ₁^c by parallelepipeds containing them; that modification only enlarges Λ₁^c. So Λ₁ = T forces R₁′ = ∅, hence R₁ = ∅. Ω₁ = T has no boundary on the torus, so Ω₁^{∼−1} = T and S₁ = (Ω₁∖Ω₁^{∼−1}) ∪ R₁ = ∅.
  {#RW1.1-2} *Ψ_ξ = id.* Ψ_ξ changes only the bonds of S₁* (V4 eq:native-source-exact-boundary-map), and S₁* = ∅.
  {#RW1.1-3} *No interpolation.* With A_e absent, j_ξ = 0 and P_{ξ,t} = P_{ξ,0}, so K_{ξ;t} does not depend on t (V4 eq:native-source-boundary-interpolation), and I_{ξ;1} = I_{ξ;0} = 𝔫_ξΞ^f_ξ with 𝔫_ξ = (2π)^{d_G|I|/2}det(H)^{−1/2} (V4 lem:polymer, eq:fixed-kernel-source-numerator).
  {#RW1.1-4} *c_ξ = 0.* By V4 eq:native-collar-boundary-constant, c_ξ = 𝓔_ξ(0) − ½⟨Ψ_ξ(A_e), H_ξΨ_ξ(A_e)⟩ + Σ_far 𝔭(Ψ_ξ(A_e)) + log|det DΨ_ξ(A_e)|. The quadratic term pairs empty vectors, and the Jacobian term vanishes as Ψ_ξ = id. A primitive is far when its free-input set misses I (V4, the paragraph after eq:native-source-jacobian-primitive).
  {#RW1.1-5} On ℓ₀ every bond is a free bond, in E_* = Ω₁* = I, or the designated bond b₀(c) of a row, and 𝓕_r = {r} for r ∈ E_*, 𝓕_r = 𝓔_c ∩ E_* for r = b₀(c) (V4 eq:regular-stencil-extension-support), nonempty since b₀(c) is the only designated bond of 𝓔_c (V4 lem:two-block). So every free-input set (⋃_{r⊂∂p}𝓕_r, 𝓔_y, 𝓕_b or 𝓔_c ∩ E_*) is a nonempty subset of I, no primitive is far, and c_ξ = 𝓔_ξ(0) = v(0) = 0 (V4 lem:primitive-sum).
  {#RW1.1-6} Hence, on ℓ₀, log 𝗐_ξ = (d_G|I|/2)log 2π − ½log det H + log Ξ^f_ξ.

## 2. The Determinant as a Sum of Local Closed-Walk Terms {#RWS2}

{#RW1.2} By V4 eq:fixed-kernel-inputs, γ ≤ H ≤ h₊ on 𝔤^I. With s_H = 2h₊, 𝖠_H = id − H/s_H and q_H = 1 − γ/s_H < 1 (V4 sec:fixed-first-kernel), the spectrum of 𝖠_H lies in [1/2, q_H], so ‖𝖠_H‖ ≤ q_H and log det H = d_G|I|log s_H − Σ_{b∈I}Σ_{m≥1}(1/m)tr_𝔤((𝖠_H^m)_{bb}), absolutely convergent.
  {#RW1.2-1} Put d_{b,m} := (1/(2m))tr_𝔤((𝖠_H^m)_{bb}), and let X_{b,m} be the set of bonds whose initial site is within distance (m+2)R of b₋. Then −½log det H = −(d_G|I|/2)log s_H + Σ_{b,m}d_{b,m}, and |d_{b,m}| ≤ d_Gq_H^m/(2m), since |tr_𝔤Y| ≤ d_G‖Y‖ and ‖(𝖠_H^m)_{bb}‖ ≤ q_H^m.
  {#RW1.2-2} A block of 𝖠_H^m depends only on the coefficients within distance (m+1)R of its first bond, and H(b′, c′) is determined by the background, Ω₁ and membership in I within distance R of b′₋ (V4 sec:fixed-first-kernel, eq:fixed-kernel-inputs). So d_{b,m} is one finite formula in the background on X_{b,m}, a sum over the closed walks of m steps of range R from b of products of entries of H. X_{b,m} has ℓ^∞ diameter at most 2(m+2)R + 1 ≤ 2(m+3)R.

## 3. The Vacuum Gas as a Sum of Local Clusters {#RWS3}

{#RW1.3} Apply V4 lem:vacuum-clusters with 𝒜 the set of all connected final domains, finite at fixed volume. Every connected final domain shares a cube with ∪𝒜 = T, so the only family compatible with ∪𝒜 is the empty one and (Ξ^f_ξ)^{[T]} = 1, and every cluster hits 𝒜. V4 eq:part1-joint-vacuum-exponential then gives Ξ^f_ξ = exp(Σ_𝔠Φ^v_ξ(𝔠)), the sum absolutely convergent, with Φ^v_ξ(𝔠) = (φ^T(𝔠)/m!)∏_jk^f_ξ(D_j) (V4 eq:part1-vacuum-cluster).
  {#RW1.3-1} The sum is real at real data, so it is log Ξ^f_ξ, Ξ^f_ξ > 0 (V4 eq:fixed-kernel-source-numerator). Each k^f_ξ(D) is a finite sum of selected integrals (V4 eq:c-vacuum-activity). In V4 eq:c-selected-integral the Cauchy integral (2πi)^{−1}∮(σ_Δ − s_Δ)^{−2}(·)dσ_Δ is the s_Δ-derivative at real s_Δ ∈ [0, 1] of the density, which is real at real σ and real data, and the potentials and cutoffs are real there. So k^f_ξ and Φ^v_ξ are real.
  {#RW1.3-2} *Locality.* The Ursell coefficient φ^T depends only on the incompatibility graph (a shared cube or wall), which is geometric. In a selected integral with final domain D every walk with w_ω ≠ 0 has D(ω) ⊂ int D (V4 lem:component-blocks), so Γ_{E₀}(σ) is formed from entries of H at bonds of D, and C_{E₀} from entries at bonds of E₀ ⊂ D (V4 eq:fixed-kernel-exact-blocks); the X-integral reads X on J_D only (V4, proof of lem:polymer).
  {#RW1.3-3} Each potential 𝐕_ξ(Y, ·) is a sum of primitives whose stencils lie in their host Y ⊂ D (V4 eq:part1-potential-host, eq:c-potential-definition), and the cutoffs χ_b do not involve the background. With V4 eq:fixed-kernel-inputs, k^f_ξ(D) is one expression in the background within distance R + 1 of D, and the dependence set of a cluster lies in the (R + 1)-neighbourhood of ∪_jD_j, inside its ρ_dep-neighbourhood (#RW1.0-4).

## 4. Embedding Terms, and the Tails at Real Data {#RWS4}

{#RW1.4} A term, a d_{b,m} or a Φ^v_ξ(𝔠), is *embedding* when its dependence set has ℓ^∞ diameter < Lι/4 fine bonds; this is *Embeds* (#MD2.3.4.35-2) at step 1, whose ρ_dep-neighbourhood contains the dependence set. Let U₁ on T and U₁′ on T′ be backgrounds that coincide, at a lift, on the Lι/4-neighbourhood of an embedding term's dependence set. That neighbourhood has diameter < 3Lι/4 ≤ 3LN_min^{(1)}/16, below half of every fine period, so the covering T′ → T is injective on it, and the partitions π₀, π₁ and the M₁-partition are lifted (#MD2.3.4.54-9).
  {#RW1.4-1} *(a) Embedding terms.* On such a neighbourhood the closed walks of §2 and the domains, selections, walks and hard core of §3 are lifted copies of their ℤ⁴ models, and each embedding term is the same finite formula in the same local entries on T and on T′. So the embedding terms agree at real data, term by term.
  {#RW1.4-2} At complex data both sides are analytic on 𝒫(U₁) (#RW1.5-1): d_{b,m} as a polynomial in the entries of H (#RW1.5a-3), Φ^v_ξ(𝔠) as a finite product of the k^f_ξ(D_j) (#RW1.5d-1). Each is a function of the A′(b), b in its dependence set, on the same product of balls on T and on T′, and the difference vanishes at the real points by (a). So it vanishes on 𝒫(U₁) (#RW1.5-1), and the terms agree there: the analytic-function form that (vac)_1 poses (#MD2.3.4.35-1).
  {#RW1.4-3} *(b) The determinant tail.* A non-embedding d_{b,m} has 2(m+3)R ≥ Lι/4, so m ≥ Lι/(8R) − 3. Per bond b, Σ_{m non-embedding}|d_{b,m}| ≤ (d_G/2)q_H^{Lι/(8R)−3}/(1 − q_H) = C_dete^{−(L|log q_H|/(8R))ι}, C_det := d_G/(2q_H³(1 − q_H)).
  {#RW1.4-4} The rate exceeds c. By V4 eq:fixed-kernel-grouping, V_{n_*}q_H^{n_*} ≤ 1/(4a_*) ≤ 1/4 (a_* = e^{81κ₁} ≥ 1, V_{n_*} ≥ 1), so R_* = n_*R ≥ R log 4/|log q_H|; by V4 eq:fixed-kernel-collar-size, M ≥ 4(ρ_w + R + R_* + 2) > 4R_*. So L|log q_H|/(8R) ≥ L log 4/(8R_*) > L log 4/(2M) ≥ 1/(32M).
  {#RW1.4-5} *(c) The vacuum-cluster tail.* Incompatible domains share a cube or a wall, so a cluster's ∪_jD_j is a wall-connected union of at most n(𝔠) := Σ_jN₁(D_j) LM-cubes, of diameter at most LM·n(𝔠). For a non-embedding cluster, LM·n(𝔠) + 2ρ_dep ≥ Lι/4 (§3), and 2ρ_dep ≤ Lι/8 (#RW1.0-4), so n(𝔠) ≥ ι/(8M).
  {#RW1.4-6} Fix an LM-cube x. {x} is a connected final domain (V4 sec:fixed-first-kernel), and every cluster containing x hits {x}. So V4 lem:vacuum-clusters with 𝒜 = {{x}} gives Σ_{𝔠∋x, non-embedding}|Φ^v_ξ(𝔠)| ≤ e^{−2θι/(8M)}Σ_{𝔠 hits {x}}|Φ^v_ξ(𝔠)|e^{2θn(𝔠)} ≤ (1/8)e^{−(θ/(4M))ι}.
  {#RW1.4-7} The rate exceeds c: θ = λ_*/(16C_g) and λ_* ≥ 2C_g log 128 (V4 eq:c2-mayer-constants, eq:c2-kappa-choice), so θ ≥ (log 128)/8 > 1/8 and θ/(4M) > 1/(32M).
  {#RW1.4-8} *(d) Per step-1 position.* Charge d_{b,m} to the LM-cube containing b₋, and a cluster to one LM-cube it contains (any fixed choice). At most 4(LM)⁴ bonds have b₋ in a given LM-cube, so by (b) and (c) the non-embedding terms charged to one LM-cube total at most (4(LM)⁴C_det + 1/8)e^{−cι}. Assign each LM-cube to one step-1 position meeting it. A step-1 position meets at most 3⁴ LM-cubes, so the non-embedding part of log 𝗐_ξ charged to it is at most C_we^{−cι}, C_w := 4(3LM)⁴C_det + 3⁴/8, which depends on L, M and the group only.
  {#RW1.4-9} So at real data on ℓ₀, log 𝗐_ξ = (d_G/2)log(2π/s_H)|I| + Σ(embedding terms) + Σ(non-embedding terms). The first is a field-independent multiple of |I| with the same coefficient on every torus, which cancels in the normalised state (#MD2.3.4.38-1); the second is the same on T and on T′ at the lift; the third is at most C_we^{−cι} per step-1 position. This is (vac)_1's form (#MD2.3.4.35-1) for 𝗐_ξ.
  {#RW1.4-10} *The consumer of the non-embedding terms* is the *Step* at k = 1 (#MD2.3.4.38), where its non-embedding terms are a further summand of (Z-d)'s non-embedding bound at k+1 = 1, C_S′ρ₁·pl·e^{−c^Zι} per root (#MD2.3.4.40-9, #MD2.3.4.49, #MD2.3.4.57-2). The T–T′ difference has such terms on T and on T′, which is why the step-1 constant is #MD2.3.4.49's C_Z^{(1)}. The bound of (d) lies under that summand, uniformly in K, as follows.
  {#RW1.4-11} With ρ₁ = C_ρg₁²(1 + r₁)⁴ (#MD2.0.3.35-2), C_w^∨ := max(C_w, C_w^c) (C_w^c of #RW1.5f) and C_lg := 1 + log₊(C_w^∨/(C_S′C_ρ)), log(C_w^∨/(C_S′ρ₁)) ≤ C_lg log g₁^{−2} once log g₁^{−2} ≥ 1, which holds as g₁ ≤ (1 + β₀)g_K (CRE (2.6), third line) and g_K is small. CRE (2.7), second line, with p = 1, m = 1, n = K, gives log g₁^{−2} ≤ (1 + β₀)(K − 1)^{β₀}log g_K^{−2} for K ≥ 2, and g₁ = g_K at K = 1. With g_K ≥ g_min, log(C_w^∨/(C_S′ρ₁)) ≤ C_lg(1 + β₀)max(1, (K − 1)^{β₀})log g_min^{−2}.
  {#RW1.4-12} By (TC), ι/(64M) ≥ max(1, (K − 1)^{β₀})(3/2)L⁴(L + 1)(log g_min^{−2})^r (#RW1.0-4), and r ≥ 2 (CRE p.245 l.34), so ι/(64M) ≥ log(C_w^∨/(C_S′ρ₁)) for g_min small, uniformly in K. Hence C_we^{−cι} ≤ C_w^∨e^{−cι} ≤ C_S′ρ₁e^{−ι/(64M)} ≤ C_S′ρ₁·pl·e^{−c^Zι}, since 1/(64M) ≥ c^Z = ν/(256A_dM) (ν ≤ 1/1000, #MD2.4.13.19-3; A_d ≥ 2, #MD2.3.5.2) and pl ≥ 1. At complex data the same holds by #RW1.5f.
  {#RW1.4-13} *The outer prefactor.* On ℓ₀ the prefactor κ_ξ of V4 eq:native-source-outer-prefactor has the background-dependent factors z^{(0)}(U₁), which is ∏_c|det_𝔤α_c(U₁)|^{−1} times a constant fixed by the volume, and exp(−g₀^{−2}S(U₁)), S the Wilson action. α_c(U₁) depends on U₁ on 𝓔_c, whose bonds are within 2L − 1 of each other (V4 lem:two-block; the paragraph after eq:native-source-jacobian-primitive), and a plaquette factor on one plaquette. Since Lι/4 ≥ 24L (ι ≥ 96, #RW1.0-4), every such factor embeds and is the same function on T and on T′ at the lift.
  {#RW1.4-14} The other factors of κ_ξ, including e^{−𝖤}, depend on the volume only and cancel in the normalised state.

## 5. (TT′)(c): The Representation at Complex Data {#RWS5}

{#RW1.5} The terms of §§2–4 extend analytically to D(1)(iii)'s complex domain, and §4's tail bounds hold there. The consumer is the *Step* at k = 1 (#MD2.3.4.38, through (vac)_1's analytic form, #MD2.3.4.35-1).
  {#RW1.5-1} *Domain.* At k = 1, D(1)(iii) (#MD2.0.3.53) asks for the extension to 𝐔 = e^{iη₁A′}U₁, η₁ = L^{−1}, for A′ ∈ 𝒫(U₁) := {A′ : |A′(b)| < α_{1,1} for every bond b}, at real U₁ in the support of the indicators, which stay factors evaluated at U₁. 𝒫(U₁) is a product of open norm balls, hence convex, and its real points form a nonempty open real set, so an analytic function on 𝒫(U₁) is determined by its real values. V4 (sec:sourced-effective-interaction) has h ≤ |·| ≤ ‖·‖_op on N × N matrices, with h(Y) = (N^{−1}tr Y*Y)^{1/2}, which on 𝔤_ℂ is the Ad-invariant h_ℂ of #T12.32; so h_ℂ(η₁A′(b)) < α_{1,1}/L.
  {#RW1.5-2} *Local gauges.* The background set of an entry f_{bb′} (V4 lem:c2-entry-range), of a fibre coefficient α_c (#RW1.4-13) and of a primitive (within 2L of its index, proof of V4 lem:hessian-gauge-locality) lies within ℓ^∞ distance 3L of one bond.   {#RW1.5-3} V4 lem:c2-local-gauge on 𝔓, whose condition 6c_hs_𝔓ε₁ ≤ r_G follows from V4's μ̂ = C_{L,R_c}ε₁ < τ_L/2 (lem:regular-bounds) and τ_L ≤ 2c_Gr_G (V4 eq:c2-tau-final), gives a site gauge u on 𝔓 with U₁^u(b) = e^{iζ_re(b)}, ζ_re(b) ∈ 𝔤, h(ζ_re(b)) ≤ μ̂.   {#RW1.5-4} *BCH.* There are ρ_bc ∈ (0, 1] and C_bc, depending on the group only, such that bch (#T12.24-0) extends, by the Baker–Campbell–Hausdorff series, to an analytic map on {(X, Y) ∈ 𝔤_ℂ × 𝔤 : h_ℂ(X), h(Y) ≤ ρ_bc} with values in 𝔤_ℂ, e^{iX}e^{iY} = e^{i·bch(X, Y)}, bch(0, Y) = Y and h_ℂ(bch(X, Y) − Y) ≤ C_bc·h_ℂ(X), C_bc bounding ‖D_X bch‖ on that compact set.   {#RW1.5-5} *Radii.* The entries f_{bb′} (V4 eq:c-entry-function) and the α_c are analytic near the real ball ‖ζ‖_∞ ≤ τ_L of the phases on their stencils (V4 lem:hessian-gauge-locality, lem:two-block).   {#RW1.5-6} *Entries.* With τ_w ≤ τ_ℂ and C_bcα_{1,1}/L ≤ τ_w (#RW1.5bp-13, -14), ζ ∈ 𝒵_ℂ on every rectangle.   {#RW1.5-7} *Size.* Ad_u is unitary for h_ℂ and the segment from ζ_re to ζ lies in 𝒵_ℂ, so ‖H(𝐔)(b, b′) − H(U₁)(b, b′)‖ ≤ sup_{𝒵_ℂ}‖Df_{bb′}‖·‖ζ − ζ_re‖_∞; Schur's test, with equal row and column sums by complex symmetry, gives ‖H(𝐔) − H(U₁)‖ ≤ c_H^ℂτ_w =: ε_ℂ in the operator norm on 𝔤_ℂ^I.
