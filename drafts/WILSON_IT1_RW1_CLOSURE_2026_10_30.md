## 1. The Weight on ℓ₀ Is c_ξ + log 𝔫_ξ + log Ξ^f_ξ, with c_ξ a Branch Constant

On ℓ₀, Ω₁ = Λ₁ = T, I = Λ₁* and J = Ω₁*∖I = ∅ (V3 L269; map l.1132).

- No interpolation. A_e ∈ 𝔤^∅ is absent, so K_{ξ;t} does not depend on t (V3 L600) and I_{ξ;1} = I_{ξ;0} = 𝔫_ξΞ^f_ξ (V3 eq:fixed-kernel-source-numerator, L837–838), with 𝔫_ξ = (2π)^{d_G|I|/2}det(H)^{−1/2}.

Every primitive vanishes at zero argument: the energy residuals because V3 eq:native-source-energy-residual subtracts only first- and second-order terms, so p_{ξ,α}(0) = E_{ξ,α}(0), and E_{ξ,α}(0) = 0 (q_U(0) = 0 for plaquettes, V3 L475–480; "vanishing at B = 0" for gauge terms, V3 L475), the Haar primitives because p^H_{ξ,b}(0) = log σ(0) − log σ(0), and the Jacobian primitives because Dφ_c(0) = 0 (V3 L475). The first two terms are fixed multiples of |Ω₁| and |I| (the bonds of Ω₁ and the free bonds), field-independent, with the same coefficients on every torus.

## 2. The Determinant as a Sum of Exactly Local Closed-Walk Terms

V3 L713 fixes γ ≤ H ≤ h₊ on 𝔤^I, s_H = 2h₊, 𝖠_H = id − H/s_H and q_H = 1 − γ/s_H < 1. The spectrum of 𝖠_H lies in [1/2, q_H], so ‖𝖠_H‖ ≤ q_H and

  log det H = d_G|I|·log s_H − Σ_{b∈I} Σ_{m≥1} (1/m)·tr_𝔤((𝖠_H^m)_{bb}),

absolutely convergent (finite dimension, ‖𝖠_H‖ < 1). V3 L717: a block of 𝖠_H^m has operator norm at most q_H^m and depends only on coefficients within distance (m+1)R of its first bond. V3 L715: H(b, c) is determined by the background, Ω₁ and membership in I within distance R of b. Define the closed-walk term and its support

  d_{b,m} := (1/(2m))·tr_𝔤((𝖠_H^m)_{bb}),  X_{b,m} := the fine ball of radius (m+2)R about b.

Then −½·log det H = −(d_G|I|/2)·log s_H + Σ_{b,m} d_{b,m}. Each d_{b,m} is a function of the background on X_{b,m}, given by one finite formula: a sum over closed walks of m steps of range R from b, with the entries of H on X_{b,m}. Moreover |d_{b,m}| ≤ d_G·q_H^m/(2m).

## 4. Exact Equality for Embedding Terms, and the Tails at Real Data

Let U₁ on T and U₁′ on T′ be real backgrounds that coincide, at a lift, on the Lι/4-neighbourhood of a term's dependence set. Call a term embedding when its dependence set has diameter < Lι/4 fine bonds, the *Embeds* condition of map l.1113 at step 1.

- (a) Embedding terms. Embedding d_{b,m} and Φ^v_ξ(𝔠) are the same function on T and on T′ at the lift. By *Embeds* (map l.1113) the covering T′ → T is injective on the neighbourhood of an embedding support (diameter < Lι/4 ≤ N_min/16 fine bonds, since ι ≤ N_min^{(1)}/4), so no closed walk of an embedding d_{b,m} winds around the torus, and the partitions are lifted (map l.1132 (4)). On an embedding support, the walks of §2 and the domains, selections and hard core of §3 are lifted copies of their ℤ⁴ models, and each term is the same finite formula in the same local entries. Equality holds term by term at real data. If each term has an analytic extension to a connected complex domain containing the real data, the extensions agree by the identity theorem. That extension exists on D(1)(iii)'s domain for d_{b,m}, a polynomial in the entries of H, which are analytic there (§5(a)), and for Φ^v_ξ(𝔠), a finite product of the k^f_ξ(D_j), analytic there (§5(d)); the domain is connected (§5, Setting). So the analytic-function form that (vac)_j poses (map l.1113) is delivered for both kinds of embedding terms, given §5.

- (b) The determinant tail. X_{b,m} has diameter 2(m+2)R, so a non-embedding d_{b,m} has m ≥ Lι/(8R) − 2. Per fine bond b,

  Σ_{m non-embedding} |d_{b,m}| ≤ (d_G/2)·q_H^{Lι/(8R)−2}/(1−q_H) = C_det·e^{−(L|log q_H|/(8R))ι},  C_det = d_G/(2q_H²(1−q_H)).

  The rate is at least c = 1/(32M), with no new condition on M. V3 L719 chooses n_* with V_{n_*}q_H^{n_*} ≤ 1/(4a_*) ≤ 1/4, so q_H^{n_*} ≤ 1/4 and R_* = n_*R ≥ R·log 4/|log q_H|. V3 L727 gives M ≥ 4(ρ_w + R + R_* + 2) > 4R_*. So L|log q_H|/(8R) ≥ L·log 4/(8R_*) > L·log 4/(2M) ≥ 1/(32M).

- (c) The vacuum-cluster tail. A cluster is connected under incompatibility, so ∪_jD_j is a connected union of π₁-cubes. Its diameter is at most LM·n(𝔠), with n(𝔠) = Σ_jN₁(D_j) the cube count (V3 L808: N₁ is the number of π₁-cubes). A non-embedding cluster has LM·n(𝔠) + 2ρ_dep ≥ Lι/4. Since R_D^{(0)} = ρ_dep fine bonds, i.e. ρ_dep/L step-1 bonds, and ρ_dep/L ≤ ι/16 under (TC) (map l.1120, l.1131, the condition (Z-f) uses; ρ_dep/L < MR₁/4), this gives n(𝔠) ≥ ι/(8M). By V3 eq:c2-pinned-vacuum (L3940–3941), summed over the connected final domains containing a fixed π₁-cube x, and by V3 eq:native-source-weighted-vacuum-criterion (L3925–3926) for the union Z = {x} (proved for every finite union of LM-cubes, L3943; D ∋ x gives D ≁ {x}, and 2θ + 1/8 ≤ 1 + 6θ),

  Σ_{𝔠 ∋ x, non-embedding} |Φ^v_ξ(𝔠)| ≤ e^{−2θι/(8M)}·Σ_{D∋x}|k^f_ξ(D)|e^{(2θ+1/8)N₁(D)} ≤ (1/8)·e^{−(θ/(4M))ι}.

  The rate is at least c: θ = λ_*/(16C_g) and λ_* ≥ 2C_g·log 128 (V3 eq:c2-kappa-choice, eq:c2-mayer-constants), so θ ≥ (log 128)/8 > 1/8 and θ/(4M) > 1/(32M).
