## 0. What the Objects Force (Derived) {#PFS0}

{#PF0.1} *The quantity is a covariance.* With the common stopping step K′ and κ_i := κ^{(K′)}_{C_i} (#366),
{#PF0.1-1} W^×_ren(ε) − W_ren(C₁; ε)W_ren(C₂; ε) = Cov_ε(W_{χ₁}(C₁), W_{χ₂}(C₂))/(κ₁κ₂),
{#PF0.1-2} since W^×_ren = E_ε[W_{χ₁}(C₁)W_{χ₂}(C₂)]/(κ₁κ₂) (#PC1.1) and W_ren(C_i) = E_ε[W_{χ_i}(C_i)]/κ_i, both at K′ (#941's normalisation). The left side converges as K → ∞: the pair's limit by #PC7.1, the single limits by the Cauchy note run with the common stopping index j in place of j(C_i), as #PC5.1-1 runs it (#ND.6.7-3). So every bound on the right side that is uniform in K holds for the limit.
{#PF0.2} *Physical units.* ℓ_i = p_iL^{j−q_i} step-K′ units is the side s_i := p_iL^{−q_i}u (#945, #PC7.2), and D step-K′ units is the physical ℓ^∞ distance R := DuL^{−j}. So ℓ_i/D = s_i/R, independent of j.
{#PF0.3} *On a fixed torus, D is large only when the loops are small against their distance.* The physical torus is fixed, so R ≤ T_ph/2 (#334, D ≤ N_s/2). A bound that decays in D at fixed s_i is therefore a bound that decays in R/s_i. A bound that decays in R uniformly in the torus size would be exponential clustering in infinite volume, a mass-gap statement. It is not the target here.
{#PF0.4} *The form of the target.* Nothing in the construction makes the covariance vanish at a fixed ratio s_i/R. So the target is a bound that is small in the coupling and decays as a power of s₁s₂/R².
{#PF0.5} *Target.* For C₁, C₂ as in #332–#332.1, with constants fixed before the coupling and uniform in K,
{#PF0.5-1} |W^×_ren − W_ren(C₁)W_ren(C₂)| ≤ C_F(ρ̄²s₁s₂/R² + 𝔞̄(ℓ₁+ℓ₂)e^{−c_FD}),
{#PF0.5-2} with ρ̄ := sup_k(ρ_k + ε_k), the activities' amplitude and CRE's small-field threshold over the scales (#946, map #MD2.0.2.2), and c_F > 0. The first term is §4's, the second §3's. The power 1 of s₁s₂/R² comes from the perimeter bound of (B2). A higher power is not addressed.
