# D2.6 Closure: Large Fields

Companion of WILSON_IT1_D2_STEP_MAP_2026_10_22.md ("the map"). Sources: the map; WILSON_IT1_SW_CLOSURE_2026_10_24.md ("SW closure"); RC_PAYMENT_PASS_2026_10_24.md; WILSON_IT1_LINE_DEFECT_2026_10_21.md ("IT-1 note"); the transcriptions Balaban\tex\<paper>\parts\pNNN_corrected.tex of CRE (pp.245, 255–278), LFR I (pp.177, 183), LFR II (p.380, pp.384–389), ER2 (p.59), RG1 (pp.253, 256–257, 266, 299), RG2 (pp.3–5, 9–13, 15), PBG (pp.416, 427–428), VAR (pp.277–281, 308), CMP98 (pp.23–24, 33, 42), REG (pp.79, 87); V4 (WILSON_FIRST_RG_STEP_V4.tex, cited by label and section); BETA_FUNCTION_WILSON_LOOPS.md (§2, the tube construction).

After the step k → k+1 the loop is a dressed loop of step k+1 (map D2.0(3)). Its insertions of attached species (att(j, Z), att(j, coll), lw(Z), wrap(j)) are carried without Bałaban's contraction L^{−1}: they are bounded once, at production, and charged to the fundamental factor exp(−κ_j(Z) − 2p₀(g_{j(Z)})) of the large-field component Z they are attached to (LFR II p.384).

D2.6 consists of three statements: (A) every insertion produced at step k+1 that is not dev has an attached species with a bound at the scale of its production step (§2–§4, and §6 for the step-1 slots lw(Z)); (B) carrying the attached insertions through later steps does not multiply their total by a per-step factor (§5); (C) the per-component total is paid by Z's fundamental factor over Z's lifetime, through Bałaban's induction pp.384–389 with an enlarged summand (§7). §8 supplies the band-cube input of the map's (T-dev).

## §1. Scope and the Objects

- *Steps.* k+1 ≤ N: no 𝐑-operation has acted (LFR I p.177 (ii), map #MD2.0.3.67). Every large-field component Z of Z_{k+1} = Λ_{k+1}ᶜ (2.0) carries, on each labelled term, the fundamental factor exp(−κ_{k+1}(Z) − 2p₀(g_{j(Z)})) of LFR II p.384, j(Z) the step at which the first large-field region inside Z was created.
- *Steps beyond N.* At k+1 > N the 𝐑-operation acts on the components that satisfy p.177 (i)–(ii); the observable's part of that is D2.7's. Every bound below is stated for k+1 ≤ N and is used by D2.7 unchanged for the components R does not touch. At k+1 > N the D2.7 note's 15.8 restates these bounds for every n ≤ K (S_R and c̄ over m ≤ K, g_* over m ≤ K+1) and re-enters the second-class components into 7.2's induction.
- *Units.* Step-(k+1) units: the π_{k+1}-cube has side M in the scale of T_{L^{−(k+1)}}, i.e. LM in the scale of T_{L^{−k}} (RG2 p.13); throughout, a π_n-side is M in step-n units and an LMR_{n+1}-cube of CRE's step-(n+1) construction is LR_{n+1} π_n-sides. The MR_{k+1}-cubes of LFR are the cubes of Ω_{k+1}'s construction (CRE p.256); the layers added on pp.264–265 are layers of LMR_{k+1}-cubes, LR_{k+1} π_k-sides each. M ≥ 2LM₁ + 2R₁M₁ is the SW closure's margin (C-M) (its §3), used only where cited; its R₁, M₁ are VAR's, fixed before the coupling (VAR p.277), so M is a constant.
- *The layers.* Ω_n∖Ω_{n+1}, n ≤ k, are the older layers; Ω_{k+1} the current small-field region; Λ_{k+1} ⊆ Ω_{k+1} the fluctuation region of the step (CRE p.269). The geometry:
  - (G1) CRE (3.5), p.265: Ω_{k+1} = (Q′_{k+1}^{∼2} ∪ (B^{k+1}(P¹_{k+1})ᶜ)^{∼3})ᶜ, and (B^{k+1}(P¹_{k+1}))ᶜ ⊇ P′_{k+1}^∼ ∪ Z_k′^{∼5}, where Z_k′ is the cover of Z_k by LMR_{k+1}-cubes and Z_k ⊇ Λ_kᶜ (p.265: the step starts from Z_k′^{∼4}). So dist(Ω_{k+1}, Λ_kᶜ) ≥ 8 layers of LMR_{k+1}-cubes ((Z_k′^{∼5})^{∼3} = Z_k′^{∼8}); 6 are used: every point within 6 such layers of Λ_kᶜ lies in Ω_{k+1}ᶜ. At the next step, with k+1 for k: dist(Ω_{k+2}, Λ_{k+1}ᶜ) ≥ 6 layers of LMR_{k+2}-cubes.
  - (G2) CRE p.256: "The domains Ω_j, Λ_j, which are determined by the j-th renormalization transformation" have boundaries at distance at least 2MR_j (the RG sequence; l.21–24), and the domains of the class 𝐁_j(Ω) satisfy dist(Ω_n, Ω_{n−1}ᶜ) ≥ L^nξM₁ (l.61); VAR (1), p.277: (L^mη)^{−1}dist(Ω_mᶜ, Ω_{m+1}) > RM₁ (map #MD2.2.6.160).
  - (G3) Ω_{k+2} ⊆ Λ_{k+1} (CRE pp.264–265; map #MD2.2.6.150-1). So the slot variable σ_{k+1} = A_{k+1} on Ω_{k+2}∩Λ_{k+2}ᶜ lies in the layer Ω_{k+2}∖Ω_{k+3}, and σ_k lives on Ω_{k+1}∩Λ_{k+1}ᶜ ⊆ Ω_{k+1}∖Ω_{k+2}.
- *The collar.* Coll_{k+1} := the π_{k+1}-cubes within d₀ = 6 layers of Λ_{k+1}ᶜ, those of Λ_{k+1}ᶜ included (SW closure §1(a3)). It contains S_{k+1} ⊆ Λ_{k+1}ᶜ (CRE p.254) and Ω_{k+1}ᶜ ⊆ Λ_{k+1}ᶜ. By (G1) at step k+2, Coll_{k+1} ∩ Ω_{k+2} = ∅ once a π_{k+1}-cube is not larger than an LMR_{k+2}-cube (a π_{k+1}-cube is one π_{k+1}-side, an LMR_{k+2}-cube is LR_{k+2} of them, so this holds since L, R ≥ 1): every collar cube of step k+1 lies in Ω_{k+2}ᶜ, hence in the layer Ω_{k+1}∖Ω_{k+2} or in Ω_{k+1}ᶜ, from step k+2 on.
- *What is carried, and what is bounded.* On a labelled term the loop's non-scalar parts near large-field data cannot be bounded in absolute value and summed over the contour (map D2.0(5)(f): the available bound of the summed absolute values grows with the contour). They are carried as insertions of attached species, bounded per large-field component by D(k)(ii′), and the per-component totals are paid by the fundamental factors (§7).
- *The non-scalar large-field parts* of the observable are, for k ≤ N, exactly these attached species: at step 1 they are §3a(4)'s att(1, Z) and wrap(1) parts and §3a(2)'s att(1, coll) parts (map #MD2.0.3.18-1–#MD2.0.3.18-2); at step k+1 they are the species of §2. Nothing else of the observable is attached to large fields: the scalar parts near large-field data are the thin terms E^∂ (§7.4), numbers given the labels.
- *Per-component statements.* All bounds are per component Z of Z_{k+1} = Λ_{k+1}ᶜ (species att(j, Z)) or per assigned mark/input (species att(j, coll), split by component through B_j(Z)), never global. This is the form D2.0(5)(f)'s payment needs and the form IT-1 note §3b, §3i use at step 1.

## §2. The Attached Species Made at Step k+1

*2.0 Components and the collar.* CRE (3.20), p.269, gives Λ_{k+1} = Ω_{k+1}^{∼−2} ∩ (R′_{k+1}^∼)ᶜ (the display's first form prints R′_{k+1} for R′^∼_{k+1}; the prose before it, "We surround the domain (Ω_{k+1}^c)^∼ ∪ R′_{k+1} by a layer of LMR_{k+1}-cubes", gives the second). A component of Λ_{k+1}ᶜ contained in a cube of size 100LMR_{k+1} is replaced by the smallest rectangular parallelepiped containing it (p.269).

*Large-field components.* The large-field region of step k+1 is Z_{k+1} = Λ_{k+1}ᶜ (CRE p.264, "a large field region Z_k=Λ_k^c", at k+1), "a union of connected components" (LFR I p.177), and LFR II p.384 factorises the large-field factors "in components of Z_j". So the components Z of Z_{k+1}, which carry the fundamental factors, are the components of Λ_{k+1}ᶜ, and every att(k+1, Z) and every reclassification attaches to one of them (map #MD2.0.3.21-1, #MD2.0.3.23).

*Counting cubes.* Every per-component bound below is first stated as C·#cubes_{k+1}(·), the number of MR_{k+1}-cubes. The components Z₀ ⊆ Z of Ω_{k+1}ᶜ are disjoint, so bounds C·#cubes(Z₀) add to C·#cubes(Z). The linear size d′(Z) is the length of a shortest tree contained in Z and meeting all the cubes of Z, in units of the MR_{k+1}-cube side (RG1 p.257: "tree graphs contained in X and intersecting all the cubes in X"; LFR II p.380 l.28).
  If d′(Z) ≠ 0, then #cubes(Z) ≤ 3·2^{d−1}d′(Z) (LFR II p.384 l.32); if d′(Z) = 0, the cubes of Z share a point, so #cubes(Z) ≤ 2^d. In both cases #cubes(Z) ≤ 3·2^{d−1}(d′(Z)+1). The payment of §7 is per component of Z_{k+1}, as LFR II p.384 needs.

*The collar.* Coll_{k+1} is §1's. "Meets Coll_{k+1}" is the single species test of 2.2–2.4. The collar marks are 𝔪̂^{(k+1)}_Δ := 1 for Δ ∈ Coll_{k+1} and 0 otherwise. Each Δ is assigned to the component of Λ_{k+1}ᶜ nearest to it, ties broken by D2.0(3)'s order. A π_{k+1}-cube has side M in T_{L^{−(k+1)}}, an MR_{k+1}-cube contains R_{k+1}^d of them, and 6 layers on each side give the factor 13^d, so #{Δ → Z} ≤ 13^dR_{k+1}^d·#cubes_{k+1}(Z).

*2.1 The rule.* A step-(k+1) group 𝒢 (D2.2(6)(d), after the three merging rules) is dev iff:
- (i) no polymer component and no member's domain of 𝒢 meets the one-layer neighbourhood of Λ_{k+1}ᶜ, that is, the π_{k+1}-cubes of Λ_{k+1}ᶜ and those within one layer of them (a subset of Coll_{k+1});
- (ii) every component of its polymers lies in Ω_k, and every traversal of its arc I(𝒢) (D2.2(6)(d) *Arcs*, length allowance included) lies in Ω_k;
- (iii) none of its polymers carries a boundary-type potential: a potential of the localised action CRE (3.27) whose localisation domain is not contained in Λ_{k+1}, or one of 𝐁_k, A(g_k^{−2}(·) − g_k^{−2}, ·), V^{(k)}(S_{k+1}) (§2.3). CRE p.276 (l.2–16, l.26–35) sends exactly the former to the boundary terms 𝐁^{(k+1)}, with the analyticity space (3.43); they include all the older-layer terms, those of 𝐄^{(j)} "with η replaced by L^{−n}" and of 𝐑_k ("treated in exactly the same way as 𝐄_k"), since Λ_{k+1} ⊆ Ω_{k+1} and Ω_n∖Ω_{n+1} ⊆ Ω_{k+1}ᶜ for n ≤ k;
- (iv) its comparison region lies in Ω_{k+1} (SW §5(e)).

*The reach of C and D̃.* C is linear and the identity off the bonds b₀(c), and (CB̃)(b₀(c)) solves (QB)(c) = 0 in the variable B(b₀(c)) (PBG p.428 l.11–13). (QB)(c) is linear in B and reads B only on B(c₋) ∪ B(c₊) ∪ B(c), since the contours of RG1 (0.4)'s average at c lie in the two blocks and the corridor. No b₀(c′) with c′ ≠ c lies in that set: b₀(c′) joins two distinct blocks, and the corridors B(c) = {b : b₋ ∈ B(c₋), b₊ ∈ B(c₊)} of distinct coarse bonds are disjoint (RG1 p.267 l.19–20). So (CB̃)(b₀(c)) reads B̃ only on B(c₋) ∪ B(c₊) ∪ B(c): C has reach one block.

D̃(B)(c) reads B only on B(c₋) ∪ B(c₊) ∪ B(c). Indeed Q̃ is defined by (2.4) (RG1 p.266) through the averages M(𝐔, c) of RG1 (0.4) (p.253), whose contours lie in the two blocks and the corridor, and C̃, the nonlinear part of LQ̃(B′) (p.267 l.26), has the same locality. Since h is supported on the bonds b₀(c′) (p.267 l.19), the defining equation LQ̃B − D̃(B) + C̃(B − hD̃(B)) = LQ̃B decouples per coarse bond. CRE uses the same operators on its {Ω_j} (CRE p.267, "only the symbols represent different operators").

A block is one step-(k+1) unit, so the composite range, one block for C and two for D̃, lies within one π_{k+1}-cube side M, as M ≥ 100 (map #MD2.4.13.19).
By 2.2 every channel through which ω′ enters is localised in domains meeting the one-layer neighbourhood of Λ_{k+1}ᶜ: the source term, the potentials at U_k(…C(σ_k, A)…), the 𝐁_k-terms, A(·), V^{(k)}, the touched members through B′ = g_kC(σ_k, A) − hD̃(g_kC(σ_k, A)) in 𝐇(s, tB′) (RG2 (1.19), localised on the touching component by D2.1(4)(b)), and the old insertions' ω-dependence. So a group passing (i) does not depend on ω′, and a group failing (i) has a cube of Coll_{k+1} in its domain. A group that is not dev is classified as follows, in the order of *Merged groups* below.
- (a) If (iv) alone fails, 𝒢 is att(k+1, Z), Z the component of Λ_{k+1}ᶜ containing the component of Ω_{k+1}ᶜ that the R_{t+1}^{(k+1)}-ball meets. The group is of kind (α)/(β) with ω′ = 0, so D2.4(10)–(11) with (f)'s parity give O(ρ_{k+1}) per group.   Two extents are used below: ext_{k+1}, the step's, inside (S-w); and the lineage extent ext^{coll}(𝒢) := max_{□∈Y_𝒢}dist(□, Coll_{k+1}) in π_{k+1}-sides, which is carried (§5.0).
  *The distance.* Let n′ be the traversal whose comparison region, the R_{t+1}^{(k+1)}-ball, meets Ω_{k+1}ᶜ; here R_t^{(k+1)} = C_boxM(t+1) in step-(k+1) units (map #MD2.0.3.12-1; not the tilt range R_t of §6), and t = t_{n′}(𝒢) is the least t with X(𝒢) ⊂ B_∞(n′, R_t^{(k+1)}) (map #MD2.4.4.5). - (b) If (ii) fails, 𝒢 is of kind (γ) and att(k+1, coll) (§4). It contains a collar cube, and the collar form needs no distance to a particular component (§4.3).
