## §1 Input (a): The Scope

*(a1) The scope.* (S-w) is proved in D2.4(10)–(13) for the step-(k+1) groups 𝒢 that pass the D2.6 closure 2.1's tests (i)–(iii): (i) no polymer component and no member's domain of 𝒢 meets the one-layer neighbourhood of Λ_{k+1}ᶜ; (ii) every component of its polymers and every traversal of its arc I(𝒢), length allowance included, lies in Ω_k; (iii) none of its polymers carries a boundary-type potential ((11)(f) *Scope*).
  Such a group is of kind (α) or (β), since the groups meeting Ω_kᶜ are of kind (γ) (D2.6 closure §4). It does not depend on ω′, since every channel of ω′ is localised in domains meeting the one-layer neighbourhood of Λ_{k+1}ᶜ (D2.6 closure 2.1, by its 2.2), so (11)(f)'s parity holds. The count of (10)–(13) uses no comparison region, so it proves (S-w) for every group passing (i)–(iii). Test (iv), the comparison region in Ω_{k+1} (§5(e)), is used only by the dev bound (§5).

*(a2) The groups outside the scope.* A step-(k+1) group takes its species from map #MD2.0.3.22–#MD2.0.3.25-1, tested in the order inherited, wrap(k+1), att(k+1, coll), att(k+1, Z), dev; the attached groups are bounded in the D2.6 closure §2–§4. A group whose set does not lift is wrap(k+1) (2.1(e)). A failure of (i) or (iii) is att(k+1, coll), of size O(b_k), which the collar term of D(k+1)(ii) carries (2.1(c), 2.2–2.3). A failure of (ii) is of kind (γ) and att(k+1, coll) (2.1(b), §4). A failure of (iv) alone is att(k+1, Z); it lies in the scope of (a1), with ω′ = 0 and O(ρ_{k+1}) per group (2.1(a)).

*(a3) The collar.* Coll_{k+1} := the π_{k+1}-cubes within d₀ := 6 layers of Λ_{k+1}ᶜ, those of Λ_{k+1}ᶜ included. It contains S_{k+1}, since S_j ⊂ Ω_j ∩ Λ_jᶜ (CRE p.254), and Ω_{k+1}ᶜ, since Λ_{k+1} ⊆ Ω_{k+1} (CRE (3.20), p.269). Test (i)'s one-layer neighbourhood lies in Coll_{k+1}, so every group failing (i) has a cube of Coll_{k+1} in its domain. The D2.6 closure uses Coll_{k+1} for the collar marks (2.0, where the 6 layers on each side give the factor 13^d), for the reference extraction (2.1(d)), and for the attached exposed segments (7.4(a)); Coll_{k+1} ∩ Ω_{k+2} = ∅ (its §1 *The collar*).

## §2 Input (c): The Prescription q₀ = q₁

*(c1) The freedom.* CRE (2.28), p.259: α_{0,j} = g_jC₀(log g_j^{−2})^{q₀}, α_{1,j} = g_jC₁(log g_j^{−2})^{q₁}, "where q₀, q₁ are integers greater than 1, C₀, C₁ are sufficiently large positive numbers". *(c2) The prescription.* Take q₀ = q₁ =: q, an integer with q > p₀, and C₀, C₁ large as CRE requires (C₁ = λ_RC₀, table #T2.24). Since ε_k = A₀g_k(log g_k^{−2})^{p₀}, α_{0,k+1}/ε_{k+1} = (C₀/A₀)(log g_{k+1}^{−2})^{q−p₀} → ∞ as g_{k+1} → 0, so the membership condition of (c1) holds for g_K small.
  Then pl = 1 + (log g_k^{−2})⁰ = 2; ᾱ_k := min(α_{0,k}, α_{1,k}) = g_k min(C₀, C₁)(log g_k^{−2})^q; (11)(e)'s condition 2q₀ ≥ p₀ + q₁ reads q ≥ p₀, so its polylog factor is absent; (12)(g)'s away-from-older-layers size is 2ρ_k; and the amplitude of D2.3(5)–(7) and (Z) is 𝔞_{k+1} = max(τ_k², 2ρ_{k+1}). The factor 1 + (log g_k^{−2})^{q−p₀} of ρ^c_{ph,k} is the IT-1 note's polylog item ((9)), outside (S-w).

*(c3) Its other uses.* The entailments of CRE p.277's margin and (12)(f)'s q₁ > p₀ ask that ε_k/α_{i,k} = (A₀/C_i)(log g_k^{−2})^{p₀−q} be small, which holds for g_K small, and D2.4(5) *Sizes on the support* uses ε_k/α_{i,k} ≤ A₀/C_i, which holds.
