## §5 Input (d): (LPG) derived (one reading, R-17)

*(e) Why the comparison region changes (derived; a counterexample to (LPG) as written).* D2.4(4) states (LPG) for every X ⊂ Ω_{k+1} with Dev_X = ℓ_X·max over X⁺. That statement is false for X with a hole: take X a closed ring of π_{k+1}-cubes around a region H ⊂ Ω_{k+1} whose plaquettes carry curvature while every plaquette of X⁺ is flat (δ_p = 0 on X⁺). Then Dev_X = 0, and (LPG) would give A = 0 on X, i.e. U_{k+1} pure gauge on X; but the holonomy of U_{k+1} around the ring is the product of the plaquette variables of a surface spanning it, which passes through H and need not be 1. So the comparison region must contain a surface for every loop of X: a contractible region.
