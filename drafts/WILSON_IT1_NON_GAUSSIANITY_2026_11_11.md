## 2 Gaussian Charge Models {#NGS2}

{#NG2.1} *Definition.* A Gaussian charge model is a triple (𝗀, 𝖷, κ): 𝗀 ∈ ℝ; 𝖷 = (𝖷₀, …, 𝖷₃) a real Gaussian vector of any mean and covariance; and κ a function on loops with values in ℂ∖{0} of κ-form, log|κ(C)| = Σ_vλ_vN_v(C) + Σ_{c∈𝒦(C)}ζ_{v_c,w_c}, with 𝒦(C) the corners of C and (v_c, w_c) the incoming and outgoing orientations at c (#GL1.1–#GL1.1-1), and real numbers λ_v, ζ_{v,w} not depending on C. The model is *stationary* if Cov(𝖷_a, 𝖷_b) = c^G_{|a−b|} for some real c^G_0, …, c^G_3.
{#NG2.1-0} For 𝔇 among the eleven diagrams put 𝖷(𝔇) := Σ_{a∈ind(𝔇)}𝖷_a (#NG1.4), so 𝖷(𝖨_a) = 𝖷_a, 𝖷(𝖱_n) = Σ_{a<n}𝖷_a, and 𝖷(𝖯_k) = 𝖷(𝖨₀) + 𝖷(𝖨_k); κ(𝔇) := ∏_{C∈𝔇}κ(C); and W(𝔇) := 𝔼[e^{i𝗀𝖷(𝔇)}]/κ(𝔇), which is 𝔼[∏_{C∈𝔇}e^{i𝗀𝖷(C)}]/κ(𝔇).
{#NG2.1-1} *Examples.* 𝖷_a := ∮_{𝖨_a}𝖡 for a real Gaussian random 1-form 𝖡 of any mean and covariance: on a lattice containing the eleven diagrams, or on the continuum torus when the line integrals over the sides of the 𝖨_a are L² limits of Riemann sums (L² limits of jointly Gaussian variables are jointly Gaussian). Then 𝖷(C) = ∮_C𝖡 for each of the nine rectangles C, as the interior sides are traversed once in each direction. If the law of 𝖡 is invariant under translation by te_μ, then Cov(𝖷_a, 𝖷_b) = Cov(𝖷₀, 𝖷_{b−a}) for a ≤ b, and the model is stationary.
{#NG2.1-2} This covers the U(1) lattice free fields at every spacing and charge, and fields with a mean. The gauge does not matter: by discrete Stokes the 𝖷(C) of the eleven diagrams are sums of the field strength, whose covariance a change of gauge leaves unchanged (#NG4.1-1).
{#NG2.2} *Proposition G.* 𝔜^sym(W) = 0 for every Gaussian charge model, and 𝔜(W) = 𝔜^sym(W) = 0 for every stationary one. If W_m are the functions of Gaussian charge models, each with its own (𝗀_m, 𝖷_m, κ_m), and W_m(𝔇) → W(𝔇) ≠ 0 for the eleven diagrams, then 𝔜^sym(W) = 0.
{#NG2.2-1} *Proof.* For a real Gaussian Z, |𝔼e^{iZ}| = e^{−Var Z/2}. So W(𝔇) ≠ 0 and log|W(𝔇)| = −(𝗀²/2)Var 𝖷(𝔇) − log|κ(𝔇)|, with Var 𝖷(𝔇) = Σ_{a,b∈ind(𝔇)}Cov(𝖷_a, 𝖷_b). Hence Σ_𝔇n^sym(𝔇)Var 𝖷(𝔇) = Σ_a𝗌(a, a)Var 𝖷_a + 2Σ_{a<b}𝗌(a, b)Cov(𝖷_a, 𝖷_b) = 0 (#NG1.4-3). log|κ(𝔇)| is linear in the N_v(𝔇) and in the corner counts, so #NG1.4-2 gives Σ_𝔇n^sym(𝔇)log|κ(𝔇)| = 0. Hence 𝔜^sym(W) = 0.
{#NG2.2-2} In a stationary model Var 𝖷(𝔇) = Σ_{a,b∈ind(𝔇)}c^G_{|a−b|} and log|κ(𝔇)| take the same values on 𝖨_a and 𝖨₀, and on 𝖱_n^{(1)} and 𝖱_n, so |W| does, and 𝔜(W) = 𝔜^sym(W) = 0 (#NG1.4-2). For the limit, 𝔜^sym(W) = lim_m𝔜^sym(W_m) = 0, as log|·| is continuous on ℂ∖{0}.
{#NG2.3} In a stationary Gaussian charge model Var 𝖷(𝖱_n) = nc^G_0 + 2Σ_{1≤k<n}(n − k)c^G_k and Var 𝖷(𝖯_k) = 2c^G_0 + 2c^G_k, so 𝔶_S(W) = 𝗀²(c^G_3 − c^G_2) and 𝔶_k(W) = −𝗀²c^G_k: the covariances of the separated rectangles that make the shape term also make the pair terms. So 𝔜^sym(W) ≠ 0 excludes every representation of W on the eleven diagrams by Gaussian charge models or their limits. These models are abelian.
{#NG2.3-1} *𝔤-valued Gaussian fields are not excluded.* Let B := Σ_aB^aT_a on the bonds, (T_a) a ⟨·,·⟩_A-orthonormal basis of 𝔤 (#O1C.1), the B^a(e) real, centred and jointly Gaussian with Cov(B^a(e), B^b(f)) = δ^{ab}G(e, f) invariant under translation by te_μ, U_e := e^{i𝗀B(e)}, W(𝔇) := 𝔼[∏_{C∈𝔇}χ(Hol_C)]/κ(𝔇) with κ of κ-form, and 𝖷^a(C) := Σ_{e∈C}B^a(e).
