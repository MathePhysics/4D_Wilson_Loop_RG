{#NG.1} **Result.** A real function 𝔜 of a Wilson-loop function on five diagrams (three rectangles and two pairs, §1) vanishes for every Gaussian charge model and every limit of such models, with any normalisation of κ-form (§2). For Yang–Mills, 𝔜 of the continuum function does not depend on the normalisation and is the limit of 𝔜 of the unnormalised lattice expectations (§3).
{#NG.1-1} The free field's shape defect is positive (§4). The pair terms of 𝔜 are O(ḡ⁴) up to powers of log ḡ^{−2}, because the trace of π vanishes on the Lie algebra (§5). The shape term equals a stopping-scale expectation up to a super-polynomially small error (§6). So 𝔜(W) > 0 for small ḡ, which is non-Gaussianity in the sense of §2, once (O1) is closed.

## 1 The Witness {#NGS1}

{#NG1.1} Fix q ∈ ℕ, a coordinate plane (μ, ν), t, s ∈ uL^{−q}ℕ with 3max(4t, s) < T_ph, and an irreducible unitary representation π of G with χ := n_π^{−1}tr π, n_π := dim π. Let I_a := o + [at, (a + 1)t]e_μ + [0, s]e_ν for a = 0, …, 3 and R_n := o + [0, nt]e_μ + [0, s]e_ν, as loops of bonds traversed counterclockwise in (μ, ν), each with χ, where o ∈ uL^{−q}ℤ⁴ has o₀ = uL^{−q}.
{#NG1.1-1} The five diagrams are R₁ = I₀, R₂, R₄ and the pairs P_k := {I₀, I_k}, k = 2, 3, whose loops are vertex-disjoint at ℓ^∞-distance (k − 1)t ≥ t. All five lie in 𝔊^{gl}_q (#GL9.2): 3diam_ph = 3max(4t, s) < T_ph, and the x₀-coordinates lie in (0, uL^{−q} + max(4t, s)] ⊂ (0, T_ph/2), as uL^{−q} ≤ t < T_ph/12.
{#NG1.2} For a function W on the five diagrams with nonzero values, 𝔜(W) := log|W(R₄)| + 8log|W(R₁)| − 3log|W(R₂)| − 2log|W(P₂)| − log|W(P₃)| = 𝔶_S(W) − 2𝔶_2(W) − 𝔶_3(W), with the shape term 𝔶_S(W) := log|W(R₄)W(R₁)²/W(R₂)³| and the pair terms 𝔶_k(W) := log|W(P_k)/W(R₁)²|.
{#NG1.3} *Counts.* Put n(R₄), n(R₁), n(R₂), n(P₂), n(P₃) := 1, 8, −3, −2, −1. Let |𝔇| be the number of loops of 𝔇 and N_v(𝔇) the total length of its segments of orientation v. Then N_{±μ}(R_n) = nt and N_{±ν}(R_n) = s, a pair adds the lengths of its loops, and every loop has exactly one corner of each type (+μ, +ν), (+ν, −μ), (−μ, −ν), (−ν, +μ).
{#NG1.3-1} So Σn(𝔇)|𝔇| = 1 + 8 − 3 − 4 − 2 = 0, Σn(𝔇)N_{±μ}(𝔇) = (4 + 8 − 6 − 4 − 2)t = 0 and Σn(𝔇)N_{±ν}(𝔇) = (1 + 8 − 3 − 4 − 2)s = 0, and each corner type occurs |𝔇| times, so its weighted count is 0. The same holds for the weights 1, 2, −3 on R₄, R₁, R₂ alone (4 + 2 − 6 = 0 and 1 + 2 − 3 = 0).

## 2 Gaussian Charge Models {#NGS2}

{#NG2.1} *Definition.* A Gaussian charge model is a triple (e, Φ, κ): e ∈ ℝ; Φ = (Φ₀, …, Φ₃) a real Gaussian vector of any mean with Cov(Φ_a, Φ_b) = c^G_{|a−b|}; and κ a function on loops with values in ℂ∖{0} of κ-form, log|κ(C)| = Σ_vλ_vN_v(C) + Σ_{c∈𝒦(C)}ζ_{v_c,w_c} with real numbers λ_v, ζ_{v,w} not depending on C. Put Φ(I_a) := Φ_a, Φ(R_n) := Σ_{a<n}Φ_a, κ(𝔇) := Π_{C∈𝔇}κ(C) and W(𝔇) := E[Π_{C∈𝔇}e^{ieΦ(C)}]/κ(𝔇).
{#NG2.1-1} *Examples.* Φ(C) := ∮_CA for a Gaussian random 1-form A whose law is invariant under translation by te_μ, on a lattice containing the five diagrams or with continuous paths (the line integrals are then almost-sure limits of Riemann sums, hence jointly Gaussian). Additivity holds because the interior edges of R_n are traversed once in each direction, and the invariance gives Cov(Φ_a, Φ_b) = Cov(Φ₀, Φ_{b−a}) for a ≤ b. This covers the U(1) free fields at every spacing and charge in any gauge, generalised free fields, and fields with a mean.
{#NG2.2} *Proposition G.* 𝔜(W) = 0 for every Gaussian charge model. If W_m are Gaussian charge models, each with its own (e_m, Φ_m, κ_m), and W_m(𝔇) → W(𝔇) ≠ 0 for the five diagrams, then 𝔜(W) = 0.
{#NG2.2-1} *Proof.* For a real Gaussian Z, |E e^{iZ}| = e^{−Var Z/2}. So W(𝔇) ≠ 0 and log|W(𝔇)| = −(e²/2)Var Φ(𝔇) − log|κ(𝔇)|, with Φ(𝔇) := Σ_{C∈𝔇}Φ(C). Here Var Φ(R₁) = c^G_0, Var Φ(R₂) = 2c^G_0 + 2c^G_1, Var Φ(R₄) = 4c^G_0 + 6c^G_1 + 4c^G_2 + 2c^G_3 and Var Φ(P_k) = 2c^G_0 + 2c^G_k.
{#NG2.2-2} With the weights of #NG1.3 the coefficient of c^G_0 is 4 + 8 − 6 − 4 − 2 = 0, that of c^G_1 is 6 − 6, that of c^G_2 is 4 − 4, and that of c^G_3 is 2 − 2. log|κ(𝔇)| is linear in the N_v(𝔇) and in the corner counts, so #NG1.3-1 gives Σn(𝔇)log|κ(𝔇)| = 0. Hence 𝔜(W) = 0, and 𝔜(W) = lim_m𝔜(W_m) = 0 as log|·| is continuous on ℂ∖{0}.
{#NG2.3} In a Gaussian charge model 𝔶_S(W) = −e²(2c^G_2 + c^G_3) and 𝔶_k(W) = −e²c^G_k: the covariances of the separated rectangles that make the shape term also make the pair terms. So 𝔜(W) ≠ 0 excludes every representation of W on the five diagrams by Gaussian charge models or their limits. These models are abelian; a 𝔤-valued Gaussian field with path-ordered holonomies is not one of them.

## 3 Yang–Mills: The Normalisations Drop Out {#NGS3}

{#NG3.1} For K with K′ = K − j_q ≥ 1 and the torus conditions of #GL9.2-1, let N_ε(𝔇) := E_ε[Π_{C∈𝔇}W_χ(C)]. Then W_ren(𝔇; ε) = N_ε(𝔇)/κ_𝔇 with κ_𝔇 := Π_{C∈𝔇}κ_{C,χ} ≠ 0 (#NV.9, read for 𝔊^{gl}_q by #GL9.7; #GL9.4).
{#NG3.2} *Proposition K.* (i) If N_ε(𝔇) ≠ 0 for the five diagrams, then 𝔜(W_ren(·; ε)) = 𝔜(N_ε). (ii) For ḡ below #GL9.8's thresholds for the five diagrams, W(𝔇) := lim_KW_ren(𝔇; ε_K) exists and is nonzero, N_{ε_K}(𝔇) ≠ 0 for K large, and 𝔜(W) = lim_K𝔜(N_{ε_K}).
{#NG3.2-1} *Proof of (i).* |κ_𝔇| = Π_{C∈𝔇}|κ_{C,χ}|, and log|κ_{C,χ}| = Σ_{k≤K′}Re(A^{li}_k(C) + A^{co}_k(C)) (#GL9.4, #GL9.5-3). A^{li}_k(C) is a sum over v of N_v^{(k−1)}(C), at k = 1 of the coarse count of the segments of orientation v, times a coefficient of (k, v); A^{co}_k(C) is a sum over 𝒦(C) of a coefficient of (k, v_c, w_c). The coefficients are numbers of the step, the same for every loop with the character χ (#GL9.4-1).
{#NG3.2-2} For the five diagrams N_v^{(k−1)} and the coarse counts are the N_v(·) of #NG1.3 divided by the length of a step-(k − 1) bond, or of a coarse bond uL^{−q}. So #NG1.3-1 gives Σn(𝔇)log|κ_𝔇| = 0, which is (i).
{#NG3.2-3} *Proof of (ii).* The five diagrams lie in 𝔊^{gl}_q (#NG1.1-1), so #GL9.8 gives the limits (#GL6.6-1 at level q) and W(𝔇) ≠ 0 below its thresholds. As W_ren(𝔇; ε_K) → W(𝔇) ≠ 0, W_ren(𝔇; ε_K) and N_{ε_K}(𝔇) are nonzero for K large, and log|·| is continuous on ℂ∖{0}.
{#NG3.3} *The prescription.* A change of normalisation of κ-form, such as a shift of a mass m_v or of a corner constant (#GL5.6-3), or a factor c^{|𝔇|}, leaves 𝔜 unchanged by #NG1.3-1. So 𝔜(W) does not depend on the renormalisation prescription.

## 4 The Free Field's Shape Defect {#NGS4}

{#NG4.1} *Setting.* Λ_a := (aℤ/Tℤ)⁴ with N_a := T/a ∈ ℕ, Λ*_a := (2π/T)ℤ⁴ mod 2π/a, p̂_α := (2/a)sin(ap_α/2) and p̂² := Σ_αp̂_α². The free field is the centred Gaussian 1-form A on the bonds with E[A_α(x)A_β(y)] = δ_{αβ}T^{−4}Σ_{p∈Λ*_a∖{0}}e^{ip·(x−y)}/p̂², A_α(x) living on the bond from x to x + ae_α. On the plaquette at x, F_{μν}(x) := ∂_μA_ν(x) − ∂_νA_μ(x) with ∂_αf(x) := a^{−1}(f(x + ae_α) − f(x)).
{#NG4.1-1} In Fourier, F̂_{μν}(p) = d_μ(p)Â_ν(p) − d_ν(p)Â_μ(p) with d_α(p) := (e^{iap_α} − 1)/a, which vanishes for Â(p) = d(p)φ̂(p). So a covariance changed by a term ξ(p)d_α(p)conj d_β(p), as by another gauge, gives the same law of F. For a rectangle I of plaquettes traversed counterclockwise in (μ, ν), Φ(I) := ∮_IA = a²Σ_{x∈I}F_{μν}(x) (discrete Stokes).
{#NG4.2} *Lemma F.* Let I be a rectangle of plaquettes of width t and height s in the plane (μ, ν), and I′ := I + de_μ with t ≤ d ≤ T − t, so that they have no plaquette in common. Then Cov(Φ(I), Φ(I′)) ≤ −(st)²/T⁴. In particular, for the rectangles of #NG1.1 on Λ_a with t, s ∈ aℕ, c^F_k := Cov(Φ(I₀), Φ(I_k)) ≤ −(st)²/T⁴ for k = 1, 2, 3, and D^F := −(2c^F_2 + c^F_3) ≥ 3(st)²/T⁴.
{#NG4.2-1} *Proof.* E[Â_μÂ_ν] = 0 for μ ≠ ν and |d_α|² = p̂_α², so E[F_{μν}(x)F_{μν}(y)] = T^{−4}Σ_{p≠0}e^{ip·(x−y)}(p̂_μ² + p̂_ν²)/p̂². Write (p̂_μ² + p̂_ν²)/p̂² = 1 − (p̂_ρ² + p̂_σ²)/p̂², {ρ, σ} the other two directions. As T^{−4}Σ_{p∈Λ*_a}e^{ip·z} = a^{−4}δ_{z,0}, the first part contributes −T^{−4} for x ≠ y.
{#NG4.2-2} In the second part fix p_⊥ := (p_α)_{α≠μ} with p̂_ρ² + p̂_σ² > 0, so that m² := Σ_{α≠μ}p̂_α² > 0. Then T^{−1}Σ_{p_μ}e^{ip_μz}/(p̂_μ² + m²) = h_m(z), the solution on aℤ/Tℤ of a^{−2}(2h(z) − h(z + a) − h(z − a)) + m²h(z) = a^{−1}δ_{z,0}, which is unique as the operator is positive definite.
{#NG4.2-3} h_m(z) = a cosh(θ(N_a/2 − z̄/a))/(2sinh θ sinh(θN_a/2)), with cosh θ = 1 + a²m²/2 and z̄ ∈ [0, T) the representative of z. For 0 < z̄ < T the equation is homogeneous and holds by cosh(u + θ) + cosh(u − θ) = 2cosh θ cosh u, as the formula takes the same value at z̄ = 0 and z̄ = T. At z̄ = 0, cosh(θ(N_a/2 + 1)) − cosh(θ(N_a/2 − 1)) = 2sinh θ sinh(θN_a/2) gives the right side a^{−1}. So h_m > 0.
{#NG4.2-4} For x ∈ I and y ∈ I′ the coordinates ρ and σ agree, so e^{ip_⊥·(x−y)_⊥} = e^{ip_ν(x_ν−y_ν)}, and I and I′ have the same rows. Hence a⁴Σ_{x∈I,y∈I′}E[F_{μν}(x)F_{μν}(y)] = −(st)²/T⁴ − T^{−3}Σ_{p_⊥}(p̂_ρ² + p̂_σ²)|aΣ_{x_ν}e^{ip_νx_ν}|²a²Σ_{x_μ,y_μ}h_m(x_μ − y_μ) ≤ −(st)²/T⁴, the sum over p_⊥ with p̂_ρ² + p̂_σ² > 0, x_ν over the rows, and x_μ, y_μ over the columns of I and I′. ∎
{#NG4.3} With Φ(C) := ∮_CA and charge e, the free field is a Gaussian charge model (#NG2.1-1) with c^G_k = c^F_k. So 𝔜 = 0 for it, while its shape term is e²D^F ≥ 3e²(st)²/T⁴ > 0 (#NG2.3).

## 5 The Pair Terms Are of Order ḡ⁴ {#NGS5}

{#NG5.1} *Lemma C.* Let G be compact, connected and semisimple, ϖ: G → U(n) a unitary representation and χ := n^{−1}tr ϖ. If h := ‖ϖ(U) − 1‖_op ≤ 2/n, then |χ(U) − 1| ≤ π²h²/8 ≤ (5/4)h², π here being the number.
{#NG5.1-1} *Proof.* det∘ϖ is a continuous homomorphism G → U(1) whose differential tr∘ϖ_* vanishes on [𝔤, 𝔤] = 𝔤, so it is 1 near the identity, hence on the connected G. Let e^{iθ_j}, |θ_j| ≤ π, be the eigenvalues of ϖ(U). Then 2|sin(θ_j/2)| ≤ h, and |sin y| ≥ 2|y|/π for |y| ≤ π/2 gives |θ_j| ≤ πh/2, so |Σ_jθ_j| ≤ nπh/2 ≤ π.
{#NG5.1-2} As e^{iΣ_jθ_j} = det ϖ(U) = 1, Σ_jθ_j = 0. So χ(U) − 1 = n^{−1}Σ_j(e^{iθ_j} − 1 − iθ_j), and |e^{iθ} − 1 − iθ| ≤ θ²/2 gives |χ(U) − 1| ≤ max_jθ_j²/2 ≤ π²h²/8. ∎ V4's G is compact, connected and simple (V4 (H1)), so Lemma C applies to ϖ = π.

{#NG5.2-3} *Size.* For the loops of the witness, r_{K′}(C) = min(t, s)/2 in step-K′ bonds (#GL2.7-2), which is at least L^{j_q−q}/2 ≥ L³MR_{K′}/2 (#GL5.2-6 at level q), with R_{K′} = R̄(j_q) ≥ 2(log ḡ^{−2})^r (#CP.13).
