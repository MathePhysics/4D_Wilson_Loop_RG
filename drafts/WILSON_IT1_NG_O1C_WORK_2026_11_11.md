## 2 The Flux Splitting

{#O1C.2} *Lemma FS (proved).* Let E, F, E′ be finite-dimensional real or complex inner product spaces, D: E → F and Q: E → E′ linear, Q onto. (i) There is a linear H: E′ → E with QH = 1 whose values minimise ‖DA‖ on {QA = B}, and for every such H, D^*DH(E′) ⊂ ran Q^*. (ii) ran D = D(ran H) ⊕ D(ker Q), orthogonally. (iii) Π_{ran D} = Π_{D ran H} + Π_{D ker Q}, and Π_{D ran H} = DH(H^*D^*DH)^+H^*D^*, ^+ the Moore–Penrose inverse.
{#O1C.2-1} *Proof.* (i) Let ι be the inclusion of ker Q. As Q is onto, QQ^+ = 1, and {QA = B} = Q^+B + ker Q. Put H := (1 − ι(Dι)^+D)Q^+, linear, with QH = QQ^+ = 1 as Qι = 0. DHB = (1 − Dι(Dι)^+)DQ^+B is DQ^+B minus its orthogonal projection onto D(ker Q) = ran Dι, so it is the point of least norm of DQ^+B + D(ker Q), and HB minimises ‖DA‖ on {QA = B}. At any minimiser HB, ⟨DHB, Dz⟩ = 0 for every z ∈ ker Q, so D^*DHB ∈ (ker Q)^⊥ = ran Q^*.
{#O1C.2-2} (ii) For z ∈ ker Q and B ∈ E′, ⟨DHB, Dz⟩ = ⟨D^*DHB, z⟩ = ⟨Q^*y, z⟩ = ⟨y, Qz⟩ = 0. Every A is HQA + (A − HQA) with Q(A − HQA) = QA − QA = 0. (iii) follows from (ii), and T(T^*T)^+T^* = Π_{ran T} for T := DH. ∎
{#O1C.2-3} *Reading.* E: the fine fluctuations B about a background U, F: the fine 2-forms with ε⁴Σ_x, D := D_U the linearised curvature, Q := Q_U Bałaban's linearised K′-step average (#T10.23), H := H_U his linearised minimiser (PRT1 (1.103) at U = 1). At a flat U the gauge directions ∂_Uω lie in ker D (D∂_U = 0, #O1C.3-5), so the fluxes ⟨χ, D·⟩ need no gauge fixing there; H is fixed only modulo ker D ∩ ker Q, which does not move DH.
{#O1C.2-4} *Identification with §4.* At a flat V₀ in the gauge of #O1C.1-3, in the component r, Π_{ran D} has the Fourier symbol of the projection onto {d(p) ∧ b}: in the plane (μ, ν) its diagonal entry is (p̂_μ² + p̂_ν²)/p̂², p = 0 removed at θ_r = 0, the kernel of #NG4.2-1. With χ_𝖨 := ε^{−2}1_𝖨 e_μ ∧ e_ν, ⟨χ_𝖨, F⟩ = ε²Σ_{x∈𝖨}F_{μν}(x) for a 2-form F, which is 𝖷(𝖨) for F = 𝖥 (#NG4.1-0, #NG4.1-1), so Re⟨χ_{𝖨₀}, Π_{ran D}χ_{𝖨_k}⟩ = c^{θ_r}_k.
{#O1C.2-5} *Gaussian reading.* Under the coarse Gaussian with density exp(−½⟨B, H^*D^*DHB⟩) on (ker DH)^⊥ the fluxes DHB have covariance Π_{D ran H}; the fine free field's fluxes have Π_{ran D}. Lemma FS(iii) is the statement that they differ by the fluxes' covariance given the averages, Π_{D ker Q}, the fluctuation part 𝔣_r := Re⟨χ_{𝖨₀}, Π_{D ker Q}(χ_{𝖨₃} − χ_{𝖨₂})⟩ in the component r.

## 4 The Exact Identity

{#O1C.4} *Lemma IBP (proved).* Let 𝔅 be the bonds of the step-K′ torus, dV the product of normalised Haar measures on G^𝔅, and for b ∈ 𝔅, Y ∈ 𝔤, (∂_{b,Y}F)(V) := (d/ds)F(V_s)|_{s=0}, V_s(b) := e^{isY}V(b), V_s = V off b. For v = Σ_b∂_{b,v_b} with v_b ∈ C¹(G^𝔅; 𝔤) and F ∈ C¹, ∫vF dV = −∫F div v dV, div v := Σ_bΣ_α∂_{b,T_α}⟨T_α, v_b⟩_A.
{#O1C.4-1} *Proof.* vF = Σ_bΣ_α⟨T_α, v_b⟩_A∂_{b,T_α}F, as ∂_{b,Y} is linear in Y. For h ∈ C¹, ∫∂_{b,Y}h dV = (d/ds)∫h(V_s)dV|_{s=0} = 0, the Haar measure on the b-th factor being invariant under left multiplication by e^{isY}, and the derivative passing under the integral on the compact G^𝔅. {#O1C.4-2} *With the density.* If supp F lies in the interior of {χ_{K′}(T) = 1} and 𝒜_{ℓ₀} is C¹ there, Lemma IBP for Fe^{𝒜_{ℓ₀}} gives 𝐄_{ℓ₀}[vF] = −𝐄_{ℓ₀}[F(div v + v𝒜_{ℓ₀})]. {#O1C.4-2.1} 𝒜_{ℓ₀} is real analytic on the interior of {χ_{K′}(T) = 1} (cited). There V is regular at every scale (CRE p.256 l.27–29), and U_{K′}(V) is analytic in V (VAR Proposition 9, p.309).
