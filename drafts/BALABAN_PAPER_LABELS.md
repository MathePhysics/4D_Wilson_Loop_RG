## Citation and Transcription Notes

- `REG`, `SPC` and `CMP99a` identify one publication. `PBG` / `CMP99b` is the other CMP 99 paper.
- `CMP98` names the averaging paper by its journal volume. It is not another title for either propagator paper.
- RG1's bibliography prints volume 89 for `HG3`; the publication is in volume **88**.
- Some older references give `CAR` as pp. 79–104. The publisher's chapter record gives **79–103**.
- `PHY` is frequently cited as pp. 79–90, including its printed header and RG1's bibliography. The publisher's article record gives **79–89**, which is used above.
- Local source pointers of the form `tex/LABEL/parts/pNNN.tex` mean the faithful transcription of printed page NNN of the paper identified by LABEL. Files ending in `_corrected.tex` contain editorial corrections and are distinct from the faithful transcription.

The dictionary was checked against the source transcriptions and their bibliographies, the V5 bibliography, and the linked publisher or author records. The labels are bibliographic identifiers, not statements about the status of any estimate cited from a paper.
