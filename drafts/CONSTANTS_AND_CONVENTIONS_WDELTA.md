## 1. Order of Choices {#TS1}

{#T1.10} Rule 1: no condition bounds α₀ or α₁ above by ε₁, by ε_k, or by ϱ.
{#T1.11} Rule 2: VAR's upper bound M_V ≤ M(ε₁) = R₁M₁a₁/ε₁ is read as an upper bound on ε₁ (stage 5), never as an upper bound on a stage-3 size.
{#T1.12} Rule 3: an O(1) belongs to the stage of the result it comes from; an O(1) that depends on L is written with its L-power whenever a condition compares two L-powers (c_u = O(1)L³, the L⁵ at the index j + 1, the L⁴ of the axial-gauge input, the L⁷ of the gradient at l.553).
{#T1.13} Rule 4: a constant that may grow with a size (C₀ and C_F carry R₁M₁ = M′; C_ι = O(1)LC_LC_F) never enters a lower bound on that size alone; either the condition is an upper bound on ε₁ (for example LC_ιε₁ ≤ O(1)M₁α₀), or the growing constants enter as a ratio bounded independently of the size (C_ι/C₀ ≤ O(1)L³C_LB₃ at l.1153, from VAR (8), whose space 𝔘_k(B₃ε₁) carries no cube factor; VAR (9)'s B₃M does, and is not used for the ratio).
{#T1.14} Rule 5: every condition the note uses on its constants is entered in W-δ §8's list when it is written, with its source; a condition stated only where it is derived is a defect. Bałaban's own conditions that the notes rely on (lower bounds on M, the RG I and RG II smallness conditions) are entered as well.
