## 1 𝔤-Valued Gaussian Models {#NAS1}

{#NA1.1} *Setting.* G is compact, connected and simple (V4 (H1)), T_1, …, T_{d_𝔤} a basis of 𝔤 (d_𝔤 := dim 𝔤, X ↦ e^{iX} the exponential) orthonormal for an Ad-invariant inner product, [T_a, T_b] = if_{abc}T_c with f real and totally antisymmetric, and Σ_{c,e}f_{ace}f_{bce} = C_Aδ_{ab}. For the irreducible π of #NG1.1 write T^π_a := π_*(T_a); then Σ_a(T^π_a)² = C₂(π)·1 (Schur), written C₂ below, tr T^π_a = 0 (#NG5.1-1), and n_π^{−1}tr T^π_aT^π_b = (C₂/d_𝔤)δ_{ab}, since an Ad-invariant form on simple 𝔤 is a multiple of the inner product and the trace of the sum is C₂.
{#NA1.1-1} Σ_aT^π_aT^π_cT^π_a = (C₂ − C_A/2)T^π_c: write T_aT_c = T_cT_a + if_{ace}T_e, so the sum is C₂T_c + if_{ace}T_eT_a = C₂T_c − (i/2)f_{ace}[T_a, T_e] = C₂T_c + (1/2)f_{ace}f_{aeg}T_g, and f_{ace}f_{aeg} = −f_{cea}f_{gea} = −C_Aδ_{cg}.
{#NA1.2} *Definition.* A 𝔤-valued Gaussian model on a set of oriented bonds is B = Σ_aB^aT_a with B(−e) = −B(e), the B^a(e) real, centred and jointly Gaussian with Cov(B^a(e), B^b(f)) = δ^{ab}G(e, f), a charge 𝗀 ∈ ℝ and κ of κ-form (#NG2.1). For a loop C = e_1⋯e_m put U_e := e^{i𝗀B(e)}, Hol_C := U_{e_m}⋯U_{e_1}, and W(𝔇) := 𝔼[∏_{C∈𝔇}χ(Hol_C)]/κ(𝔇).
{#NA1.2-1} For simple 𝔤 and a law invariant under B ↦ Ad_gB the covariance has this form by Schur's lemma, the adjoint representation being absolutely irreducible. This covers d_𝔤 independent copies of the U(1) free field of #NG4.1 at θ = 0 in any gauge.
{#NA1.2-2} *Path form.* Parametrise C by s ∈ [0, m), the bond e_j on [j − 1, j), and put 𝔟(s) := B(e_j) and 𝒢(s, s′) := G(e_j, e_l) for s ∈ [j − 1, j), s′ ∈ [l − 1, l). As 𝔟 is constant on each interval, Hol_C = Σ_{n≥0}(i𝗀)^n∫_{m>s_1>⋯>s_n>0}𝔟(s_1)⋯𝔟(s_n)ds, the series converging absolutely with the n-th term at most (Σ_j‖B(e_j)‖_op)^n|𝗀|^n/n!.
{#NA1.3} *Lemma NA1.* For a loop C, 𝗀 ↦ κ(C)W(C) is entire and even, and log(κ(C)W(C)) = −(𝗀²C₂/2)Q(C) − (𝗀⁴C₂C_A/2)J_×(C) + O(𝗀⁶), with Q(C) := Var 𝖷^a(C), 𝖷^a(C) := Σ_jB^a(e_j) (the same for each a), and J_×(C) := ∫_{m>s_1>s_2>s_3>s_4>0}𝒢(s_1, s_3)𝒢(s_2, s_4)ds ≥ 0 for G ≥ 0 pointwise.
{#NA1.3-1} *Proof.* By #NA1.2-2 and 𝔼e^{c‖B‖} < ∞ for Gaussian B, dominated convergence gives the expansion of 𝔼χ(Hol_C) term by term, with the n-th coefficient at most 𝔼(Σ_j‖B(e_j)‖)^n/n!, so it is entire. B ↦ −B preserves the law and maps Hol_C(𝗀) to Hol_C(−𝗀), so it is even.
{#NA1.3-2} Order 2: the coefficient is −∫_{s_1>s_2}Σ_{a,b}n_π^{−1}tr(T^π_aT^π_b)𝔼𝔟^a(s_1)𝔟^b(s_2) = −(C₂/d_𝔤)·d_𝔤∫_{s_1>s_2}𝒢 = −C₂Q/2, 𝒢 being symmetric and Q = ∫∫𝒢.
{#NA1.3-3} Order 4: Isserlis gives the pairings (12)(34), (14)(23) and (13)(24) of s_1 > ⋯ > s_4. Their colour factors Σn_π^{−1}tr(⋯) are C₂², C₂² and (C₂ − C_A/2)C₂ (#NA1.1-1). The sum of the three pairings of 𝒢 is symmetric in the four variables, so its integral over the simplex is (1/24)·3Q² = Q²/8. So the coefficient is C₂²Q²/8 − (C₂C_A/2)J_×.
{#NA1.3-4} log(1 + w_2𝗀² + w_4𝗀⁴ + O(𝗀⁶)) = w_2𝗀² + (w_4 − w_2²/2)𝗀⁴ + O(𝗀⁶), and w_2²/2 = C₂²Q²/8. ∎
{#NA1.4} *Lemma NA2.* For vertex-disjoint loops C, C′, log(W(C, C′)/(W(C)W(C′))) = 𝗀⁴C₂²c(C, C′)²/(2d_𝔤) + O(𝗀⁶), c(C, C′) := Cov(𝖷^a(C), 𝖷^a(C′)).
{#NA1.4-1} *Proof.* κ is multiplicative, so it cancels. The order-1 term of χ(Hol_C) is i𝗀n_π^{−1}tr T^π(𝖷(C)) = 0, and the order-2 term is 𝗀²ω_C with ω_C := −(2n_π)^{−1}tr T^π(𝖷(C))² = −(C₂/(2d_𝔤))Σ_a𝖷^a(C)², as the trace makes the ordered double integral half the square. The joint expectation is even in 𝗀 (#NA1.3-1), so Cov(χ(Hol_C), χ(Hol_{C′})) = 𝗀⁴Cov(ω_C, ω_{C′}) + O(𝗀⁶).
{#NA1.4-2} By independence over a and Cov(X², Y²) = 2Cov(X, Y)² for centred jointly Gaussian X, Y, Cov(ω_C, ω_{C′}) = (C₂/(2d_𝔤))²·2d_𝔤·c² = C₂²c²/(2d_𝔤). With 𝔼χ = 1 + O(𝗀²), log(1 + Cov/(𝔼χ𝔼χ′)) gives the claim. ∎
{#NA1.5} *Proposition NA3.* For the six diagrams of #NG1.1 in a 𝔤-valued Gaussian model whose law is invariant under translation by te_μ, with c_k := c(𝖨₀, 𝖨_k) and D^𝔤 := c_3 − c_2, 𝔜(W) = 𝗀²C₂D^𝔤 + 𝗀⁴[−(C₂C_A/2)Σ_𝖱n_S(𝖱)J_×(𝖱) + (C₂²/(2d_𝔤))(c_3² − c_2²)] + O(𝗀⁶), n_S(𝖱) the weights 1, −3, 3, −1 of #NG1.3.
{#NA1.5-1} *Proof.* 𝔜 = 𝔶_S − 𝔶_2 + 𝔶_3 (#NG1.2). By Lemma NA1, 𝔶_S = −(𝗀²C₂/2)Σn_S(𝖱)Q(𝖱) − (𝗀⁴C₂C_A/2)Σn_S(𝖱)J_×(𝖱) + O(𝗀⁶), κ dropping by #NG1.3-1, and Σn_S(𝖱)Q(𝖱) = 2c_2 − 2c_3 by #NG2.2-1's variances, 𝖷^a(𝖱_n) = Σ_{a′<n}𝖷^a(𝖨_{a′}) holding as there. W(𝖨_k) = W(𝖨₀) by the invariance, so 𝔶_k is Lemma NA2's logarithm, 𝗀⁴C₂²c_k²/(2d_𝔤) + O(𝗀⁶). ∎
{#NA1.5-2} For d_𝔤 copies of the free field at θ = 0, D^𝔤 = D^F ≥ s²t³/(112𝖳⁵) (#NG4.2-0), so 𝔜 > 0 for small 𝗀 at fixed spacing: 𝔜 does not vanish on 𝔤-valued Gaussian models. In the abelian model the pair terms −𝗀²c_k cancel the shape term (#NG2.3); here tr T^π_a = 0 removes them. J_× carries a logarithm of the spacing (#NA4.2), so the O(𝗀⁴) is not uniform in it.

## 2 What a Non-Abelian Witness Must Read {#NAS2}

{#NA2.1} Lemma H (#NG8.2) reduces Yang–Mills's holonomy part to the second moments Ψ_k of the 𝔤-valued fluxes, and (O1c) bounds Ψ_3 − Ψ_2 below by 3n_πc_Oḡ² (#NG8.3). That the Ψ_k are, to order ḡ², those of the twisted free covariances, as in Lemma NA1, is not proved; its consequence for the shape term is (Y1) (#NA5.2). its pair terms are O(ḡ⁴) up to logarithms (Theorem P), as in Lemma NA2.
{#NA2.1-1} So, given (Y1), Yang–Mills and the 𝔤-valued model with these covariances agree to order ḡ² on the shape and pair terms, and a functional of the log|W| that vanishes on 𝔤-valued Gaussian models sees Yang–Mills only through order-ḡ⁴ effects. The errors at one scale are O(ḡ³)·polylog (Proposition A, #NG7.1; Lemma H) and O(ḡ⁴)·polylog (#NG6.2).
{#NA2.2} The order-ḡ⁴ effect that is proved is the coupling flow. By #B0.2, g_k^{−2} − g_l^{−2} = Σ_{j=k+1}^lβ_j for k ≤ l, so items 1 and 5 of #B0.14 give |g_k^{−2} − g_l^{−2} − 2c(l − k)ln L| ≤ C + C_remΣ_{j=k}^{l−1}g_j². Over Δ := l − k steps, g² changes by about 2cΔ ln L·g⁴, against a per-scale error O(g³)·polylog; Δ ≫ g^{−1}·polylog resolves it.

## 3 The Running Rate and Screening {#NAS3}

{#NA3.1} *Definition.* For λ > 0 let 𝔶_S(W; λ) be the shape term (#NG1.2) of the rectangles of #NG1.1 scaled by λ about o. Put I_k := ∫_{𝖨₀}∫_{𝖨_k}|x − y|^{−4}d²x d²y for the unscaled rectangles, which depends on s/t only, D^F_∞ := (I_2 − I_3)/π², positive as in #NA3.3-2, g_W²(λ) := 𝔶_S(W; λ)/(C₂D^F_∞) when positive, and 𝔄_Δ(W; λ) := (g_W^{−2}(L^{−Δ}λ) − g_W^{−2}(λ))/(Δ ln L).
{#NA3.2} *Generalized free fields.* A Källén–Lehmann 1-form is a centred Gaussian random 1-form on ℝ⁴ with covariance Ĉ_{αβ}(k) = ∫dν(m²)(δ_{αβ} − k_αk_β/k²)/(k² + m²) + ϱ_L(k²)k_αk_β/k⁴, ν a finite positive Borel measure on [0, ∞) and ϱ_L ≥ 0. It includes the free massless and massive fields in every covariant gauge and their positive superpositions. W is a limit of such a model if W = lim_ηW_η, W_η the abelian charge model (#NG2.1) of the mollified field B * η_η with any κ-form normalisation, η_η a smooth mollifier supported in the ball of radius η.
{#NA3.2-1} *The flux correlation.* F := dB has 𝔼[F̂_{μν}(k)conj F̂_{μν}(k)] = (k_μ² + k_ν²)A(k²), A(k²) := ∫dν(m²)/(k² + m²); ϱ_L drops out, as in #NG4.1-1 (the k_αk_β/k² and ϱ_L terms cancel in k_μ²Ĉ_{νν} + k_ν²Ĉ_{μμ} − 2k_μk_νĈ_{μν}). So 𝔼[F_{μν}(x)F_{μν}(y)] = −(∂_μ² + ∂_ν²)Ǎ(x − y) for x ≠ y, Ǎ(z) = ∫dν(m²)G_m(|z|), G_m(r) := mK₁(mr)/(4π²r).
{#NA3.2-2} For z in the (μ, ν)-plane, (∂_μ² + ∂_ν²)f(|z|) = r^{−1}(rf′)′ at r = |z|. With f = G_m, x := mr and (K₁(x)/x)′ = −K₂(x)/x, −K₂′ = K₁ + 2K₂/x, r^{−1}(rG_m′)′ = (m⁴/(4π²))(K₁(x)/x + 2K₂(x)/x²). So r⁴𝔼[F_{μν}(z)F_{μν}(0)] = −(4π²)^{−1}∫dν(m²)φ(mr), φ(x) := x³K₁(x) + 2x²K₂(x).
{#NA3.2-3} φ′(x) = 3x²K₁ + x³K₁′ + 4xK₂ + 2x²K₂′ = −x³K₀(x) < 0 by K₁′ = −K₀ − K₁/x, and φ(0⁺) = 4, as x²K₂(x) → 2. At ν = δ_0 this is the massless r⁴𝔼FF = −1/π².
{#NA3.3} *Lemma S (screening).* If W is a limit of a Källén–Lehmann model with ν ≠ 0, then 𝔶_S(W; λ) = 𝗀²D_ν(λ) with D_ν(λ) := (4π²)^{−1}∫dν(m²)[∫_{𝖨₀}∫_{𝖨₂} − ∫_{𝖨₀}∫_{𝖨₃}]φ(mλ|x − y|)|x − y|^{−4}d²x d²y, positive and non-increasing in λ. Hence 𝔄_Δ(W; λ) ≤ 0 for every Δ ≥ 1.
{#NA3.3-1} *Proof.* For W_η, #NG2.3 gives 𝔶_S = 𝗀²(c^η_3 − c^η_2), c^η_k the flux covariances of the mollified field, and κ drops out (#NG1.3-1). 𝖨₀ and 𝖨_k, k ≥ 2, lie at distance ≥ λt; for η < λt/4 the mollified flux correlation converges to #NA3.2-2's uniformly on |z| ≥ λt/2, so c^η_k → c^ν_k := ∫_{𝖨₀}∫_{𝖨_k}𝔼[FF], the scaled rectangles written as λ times the unscaled ones and d²xd²y/|x − y|⁴ being invariant.
{#NA3.3-2} By Tonelli (the integrand has one sign), −c^ν_k = (4π²)^{−1}∫dν∫∫φ(mλ|x − y|)|x − y|^{−4} ≤ ν([0, ∞))I_k/π², finite, so c^ν_3 − c^ν_2 = −c^ν_2 − (−c^ν_3) is D_ν(λ). Pair y ∈ 𝖨₂ with y + te_μ ∈ 𝖨₃: for x ∈ 𝖨₀, y_μ − x_μ > 0, so r′ := |x − y − te_μ| > r := |x − y| ≥ t. With 𝖿_r(λ) := φ(mλr)r^{−4}, D_ν(λ) = (4π²)^{−1}∫dν∫_{𝖨₀}∫_{𝖨₂}(𝖿_r(λ) − 𝖿_{r′}(λ)).
{#NA3.3-3} φ > 0 is decreasing (#NA3.2-3), so 𝖿_r(λ) > 𝖿_{r′}(λ) and D_ν(λ) > 0 for ν ≠ 0. By φ′(x) = −x³K₀(x), (d/dλ)𝖿_r(λ) = −m⁴λ³K₀(mλr), and for λ < λ′, (𝖿_r − 𝖿_{r′})(λ′) − (𝖿_r − 𝖿_{r′})(λ) = −m⁴∫_λ^{λ′}μ³(K₀(mμr) − K₀(mμr′))dμ ≤ 0, K₀ being decreasing. Integrating, D_ν is non-increasing in λ. The same pairing with ν = δ_0, φ ≡ 4 gives I_2 > I_3. Then g_W^{−2}(L^{−Δ}λ) = C₂D^F_∞/(𝗀²D_ν(L^{−Δ}λ)) ≤ g_W^{−2}(λ). ∎
{#NA3.4} In words: for a positive spectral measure the shape-term coupling cannot grow with the scale. Yang–Mills's grows (§2), so 𝔄_Δ > 0 for it under (Y1)–(Y4), with limit the one-loop rate.

## 4 Path Ordering Runs {#NAS4}

{#NA4.1} *Homogeneous covariant models.* Ĉ = 𝖼_T(δ − kk/k²)/k² + 𝖼_Lkk/k⁴ with 𝖼_T > 0, 𝖼_L ≥ 0, regularised at spacing ε. In position space C_{αβ}(x) = (4π²|x|²)^{−1}(𝖼_δδ_{αβ} + 𝖼_xx̂_αx̂_β) with 𝖼_δ = (𝖼_T + 𝖼_L)/2, 𝖼_x = 𝖼_T − 𝖼_L: δ/k² is δ/(4π²|x|²), and kk/k⁴ is −∂∂ψ with ψ = −(8π²)^{−1}log|x| the inverse transform of k^{−4}, ∂_α∂_βlog|x| = (δ_{αβ} − 2x̂_αx̂_β)/|x|², so kk/k⁴ is (4π²|x|²)^{−1}(δ/2 − x̂x̂). Feynman's gauge is 𝖼_T = 𝖼_L = 1, Landau's 𝖼_L = 0.
{#NA4.2} *The logarithm of J_×.* With the cutoff of #NAG1.2, Σn_S(𝖱)J_×(λ𝖱) = −2𝔞_×𝖼_TD^F_∞log(λ/ε) + K₄ + O(ε/λ), 𝔞_× := (𝖼_δ + 𝖼_x)/(4π²) (Lemmas M and NAG3). The logarithm comes from a short chord enclosing one endpoint of a long one: per scale of the short chord, the enclosed positions weigh 𝔞_× on a straight side, and corners and nested chords give terms that n_S removes.
{#NA4.2-1} With Σn_S(𝖱)Q(λ𝖱) = −2𝖼_TD^F_∞ + O_N((ε/λ)^N) (Lemma NAG2) and the expansion of Lemma NAG1, 𝔶_S(W; λ) = 𝗀²𝖼_TC₂D^F_∞[1 + 𝗀²C_A𝔞_×log(λ/ε)] + 𝗀⁴𝒦₄ + O(𝗀²(ε/λ)^N + 𝗀⁴ε/λ + (𝗀λ/ε)⁶), 𝒦₄ := −C₂C_AK₄/2.
{#NA4.3} *The rate.* Then g_W^{−2}(λ) = (𝗀²𝖼_T)^{−1} − (C_A𝔞_×/𝖼_T)log(λ/ε) + const, up to the error below, and 𝔄_Δ = C_A𝔞_×/𝖼_T = (C_A/(4π²))(3/2 − 𝖼_L/(2𝖼_T)) ≤ (3/2)C_A/(4π²) up to O(L^Δε/λ + 𝗀^{−2}(L^Δε/λ)^N + 𝗀²ln(λ/ε)) (Theorem NAG12), which tends to 0 along 𝗀 = (ε/λ)^p, p > 0. For Yang–Mills in the normalisation in which the one-gluon term is −(g²C₂/2)Q with the free covariance, the rate is the one-loop (11/6)C_A/(4π²) (dg²/dln R = 2b₀g⁴ with b₀ = 11C_A/(48π²)). The margin is C_A/(12π²).
{#NA4.3-1} The proof writes W as a hard-core polymer gas of crossing-connected chord sets (#NAG6.2–#NAG6.3), whose cluster expansion converges for 𝗀²ln(λ/ε) small; perimeter and corner clusters cancel under n_S (Lemma NAG6), and a tree-graph bound shows that the derivative of the n_S-combination in the cutoff carries no logarithm (Lemma NAG11). The error tends to 0 whenever 𝗀²ln(λ/ε) → 0 with 𝗀 ≥ (ε/λ)^K for a fixed K.
{#NA4.4} *Constant parts.* A part of B concentrated below momentum 1/λ acts on the loops at scale λ as a nearly constant connection b. For constant b, BCH gives Hol_{𝖱_n} = e^Z with Z = nts[𝗀b_μ, 𝗀b_ν] + (terms of degree ≥ 3 in 𝗀b), and tr π_*(Z) = 0, so the degree-4 part of log W(𝖱_n) is ∝ n², which Σ_nn_S(𝖱_n)n² = 0 (#NG1.3-1) removes. The first surviving degree is 6, with n³ and n⁴ (Σn_Sn³ = −6, Σn_Sn⁴ = −60). Whether such parts can change 𝔄_Δ is part of (G3).
