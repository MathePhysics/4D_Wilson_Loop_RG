## 48. The Near Domains at the Boundaries of Minimisation Domains

The LF note's item at its l.19 concerns the near domains □₀ that meet the boundary of a configuration's minimisation domain: ∂Ω″^∼_{h+1} for U_1 and U_2; ∂Y₁, ∂Y₂, and ∂Y₃ for the representation configurations of (1.61)–(1.63); and ∂□₀ for the composites. "There c^A is not the local minimiser of its own data on □₀, so it is not in 𝒮_z and (5.1c) gives no age factor." This section states which configuration such a term reads and what is known about it. The bounds are derived in §57 (the glued member), §62 (the term at the composite, controlled by the configuration's own conditions on □₀), §§63–64 (the points where (N) fails, and the endpoints), 68.4–68.5 (complex points), and §71 (the top layers).

### 48.1 The Configuration the Term Reads

Let 𝐔 be a configuration of the class at which a near-domain term 𝐄^{(j)}(X, ·, z), X ⊂ □^{∼2}, is read. By R-data (§44.1, RG I p.275 l.1–5), "the configuration U_{k+1} is replaced by the configuration U_{k+1}(□₀, M^·(𝐔)), extended as equal to 𝐔 outside □₀". So the term reads the glued configuration: P_n𝐔 := U_n(□₀, M^·(𝐔)) on □₀, and 𝐔 outside, with the composite's current.
- The data of U_n(□₀, ·) are the cube data: the averages M^p(𝐔), p ≤ n, on the cube layers of □₀, and on the outer shell □₀ ∖ □₁ the bond values of 𝐔 themselves (RG I (1.15), p.262 l.41–50, with REG (1.5)). The outer shell is a layer of width more than RM₁ (REG (1.4)), not a single layer of bonds.
- P_n𝐔 exists and is analytic in 𝐔 on RG I's space U′^c_{k+1}(□₀, (1 + 2β)α₀, (1 + 2β)α₁): the conditions (i)–(iii) on the whole lattice and (iv) on □₀ (RG I p.275 l.19–21; p.273 l.39–40, "We take the function U_j constructed for the cube □₀ = □̃⁵ as in the condition (iv) in the definition of spaces U_j^c"). RG I Lemma 4 ((3.53), p.280), at 𝐀 = 0 and B′ = 0, places the pair (P_n𝐔, J_n(□₀, M^·(𝐔))) restricted to X in U^c_j(X, α₀, α₁). This is membership on X, not class membership of the glued configuration on the whole lattice.
- The term is not the term at the global minimiser W_𝐔 of 𝐔's determining-set data. On the outer shell P_n𝐔 = 𝐔 up to gauge, while W_𝐔 is a minimiser there, so the two differ whenever 𝐔 is not minimal on □₀ ∖ □₁.
- Bałaban's statement on this configuration is qualitative: "U_{k+1}(□₀, M^·(𝐔)) has approximately the same regularity properties as 𝐔, hence all necessary theorems are valid for operations with this configuration" (RG I p.275 l.6–7). It does not by itself give the age factor of (5.1c).

### 57.2 Ŵ_z(t) and 𝒮_z

𝒮_z is the set of pairs of the class 𝒞_k whose restriction to □₀ is the local minimiser of its own cube data (the Cauchy note's l.124). Ŵ_z(t) has this property by construction. Its membership of 𝒞_k needs (a) on the near domains and (b), the bounds (2.34)–(2.39) of CRE p.261, on the whole lattice at the reduced radii.
- Outside □₀. Ŵ_z(t) = c(t), the entry, with the membership moves of the LF note's l.567–569 (derived except the current, on three premises) on the disc of its l.528.
- On □̃³. P_nc^B = c^B for the members that are global minimisers on a domain containing □₀ ((I.3.18) at the layer, CRE p.280 l.14–16); it fails for the minimisers whose domain boundary crosses □₀ (57.3). The cube data of c(t) are e^{iB′(t)} times those of c^B with |B′(t)| ≤ K_M|t|σ_c (44.7). RG I (3.50) writes the Landau-gauge field of the composite at shifted data as that at the base data plus 𝐀₂, and p.280 l.1–5 bounds |𝐀₂|, |∇^ξ𝐀₂| ≤ B₃|B′| < B₃α₃, combined with the inequality of (3.37) on □̃³. Lemma 4 gives (i)–(ii) on □̃³, hence on X, for c^B in its input space, and the Cauchy note derives that input membership under (N) (its l.140, l.144). So on □̃³, under (N), the conditions hold at Ŵ_z(t) on the disc |t|σ_c ≤ c′_ρα_m with c′_ρ := c_ρ/max{1, B₃K_M} (B₃ ≥ 1).
- On □₀ ∖ □̃³. Not derived. Bałaban prints the 𝐀₂ bound without a domain and uses it only on □̃³, and states the regularity of the local-minimiser field on □̃⁴ ((3.27), p.276 l.43). On the shells of □₀ ∖ □̃⁴ the data are averages at scales finer than the layer's, and first differences of 𝐀₂ at the layer's scale need the gradient part a_p²|∇𝐒_c| of the data shift, not only its sup norm. The composites at the shifted and the base data are related by e^{iξ𝐀₂} only after the data-dependent gauge change w(t) = (u_jv)(0)^{−1}(u_jv)(t) of (3.26) and (3.37), and CRE's class is invariant only under G-valued gauge transformations (CRE p.261 l.42–43), so the gluing at ∂□₀ also needs w(t) − w(0) small with its differences.
- The localised minimisers. (2.35) and (2.37) read the configuration only through M^·(𝐔) (CRE p.261 l.10), at scales no finer than the layer's; these averages of Ŵ_z(t) are M-images of its cube data, which are c(t)'s, given that the cube shells are unions of blocks of the corresponding scales. So (2.35) and (2.37) are the entries' conditions.
- The current (2.36) on □₀ ∖ □̃³. Not covered: the current needs second differences of 𝐀₂, and Bałaban's second-order bound ("proved in the same way", p.280 l.21) is on □̃³.
- The class of (a). For the run-B configuration Ŵ_z(t), (a) of 𝒞_k also contains the lift condition on ι_X𝐔 and the current bound s₀/C_{Q*} (the Cauchy note's l.110, l.119); Lemma 4 gives the single-family condition only.

So Ŵ_z(t) ∈ 𝒮_z is not derived: it holds on □̃³ under (N), and on □₀ ∖ □̃³ the fields, the gauge change, and the current are open, as is the lift part of (a). (5.1c), which holds for U ∈ 𝒮_z whose □₀ satisfies (N) (the Cauchy note's l.128), is therefore not applied at Ŵ_z(t). §44 does not use it: 64.5 bounds irr_j at the entry c(t) itself, and §64 records its own premises.
