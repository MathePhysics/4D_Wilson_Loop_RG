## What the Objects Are

- **The stopping lattice is fixed.** ε = uL^{−K}, u fixed, C a square of physical side pL^{−q}u, torus periods T_ph fixed multiples of u. The stopping scale is a := uL^{−j(C)} with j(C) the least j ≥ q + q̄(j), q̄(j) := ⌈log_L(L³M(1+γ²β′(j+1))^{β₀}(log g_min^{−2})^r)⌉ (map l.193, l.1093; g_min := inf_K g_K > 0, γ := sup_K g_K). Under the coupling prescription j(C) depends on q, L, M, β₀, β′, r, g_min, γ only, so for every K the step K′ := K − j(C) integrates over the field V_{K′} on the lattice T^{(K′)} of spacing a with N_s := (T_ph/u)L^{j(C)} sites per side, |T^{(K′)}| = N_s⁴ sites, and C is the fixed closed contour C^{(K′)} of P_{K′}(C) = 4ℓ_C bonds, ℓ_C = pL^{j(C)−q}. None of a, N_s, ℓ_C, or C^{(K′)} depends on K.
- **The run depends on K.** The density ρ_{K′} = ρ^{[K]}_{K′} on T^{(K′)} is the output of K′ = K − j(C) renormalisation steps from the ε-lattice, with the bare coupling g_0(ε, g) of RG I Theorem 2. Its K-dependence passes through four channels and no other: (i) the effective coupling g_{K′} and the sequence g_1, …, g_{K′} (CRE (2.24), p.259, with RG I Theorem 2's g_0(ε, g)); (ii) the ε-lattice minimiser U_{K′}(V_{K′}) (CRE (2.12), p.256: the minimal orbit of A^η on {U regular, M_𝔹(U) = V}, a field on T_η with η = L^{−K′} in step-K′ units, i.e. on the ε-lattice), through which every function of V_{K′} in the action is defined (CRE p.258: A_k "depends on the gauge field variable V … through the background field U_k(V)"), and the fluctuation covariances built from it (RG1 (1.4), p.260: Z^{(j)}(U_k) = ∫dB δ(Q̃B)exp[−½⟨B,Δ^{(j)}(U_k)B⟩]); (iii) the number of steps summed, Σ_{j=1}^{K′}, in 𝐄_{K′} (CRE (2.25), p.259), 𝐑_{K′} (CRE (2.30), p.260), 𝐁_{K′} (CRE (2.40), p.261), E_{K′} (p.262), and in the dressing's κ^{(K′)}, 𝓔_{K′}, and insertions (D2.3(8), map l.1190–1200); (iv) the label set of the terms, the sequences {Ω_j}, {Λ_j}, {S_j} of K′ steps (CRE (2.18), p.257), with the pending 𝐓-integrations over the old variables on the lattices of spacing uL^{j−K} inside the surviving large-field regions (CRE (2.19)–(2.22)) and, for K′ > N, the R-histories of the renormalised regions (LFR II p.390: "The new large field region is equal to Z_k ∪ ⋃Y_i", so a kept Y_i persists as a large-field region with its pending V_k).
- **The identity is a convex combination.** On every labelled term ℓ of ρ_{K′} on which D(K′) holds, N^C_ℓ := ∫𝒲_ℓρ_ℓ dV (the numerator identity, map l.326, D2.2(2)), Z_ℓ := ∫ρ_ℓ dV ≥ 0 (LFR1 p.176: "We will prove that the densities are positive"), Z := Σ_ℓZ_ℓ, w_ℓ := Z_ℓ/Z ∈ [0,1], Σ_ℓw_ℓ = 1, and E_ε[W(C)] = Σ_ℓw_ℓ⟨𝒲_ℓ⟩_ℓ with ⟨F⟩_ℓ := ∫Fρ_ℓdV/Z_ℓ a probability expectation on the term's variables (for Z_ℓ > 0; a term with Z_ℓ = 0 contributes nothing). The dressing is 𝒲_ℓ = κ^{(K′)}e^{𝓔_{K′}(ℓ)}[f_{K′} + I_{K′}(ℓ)] (D(K′)(i), (v); map l.261), with the one number κ^{(K′)} in front of every term.

## What That Forces

- On ℓ₀ the integration variable is K-independent and the leading function can be made K-independent: by the background closure §3.4, on the terms whose scale-K′ blocks of C lie in Ω_{K′}, which on ℓ₀ is every term, f_{K′} = W_χ(C;U_{K′}(V_{K′})) = W_χ(C^{(K′)};V_{K′}) + β^{(K′)}_{K′}(V_{K′}) with |β^{(K′)}_{K′}| ≤ Lip_χ(e^{8a_dc_hB₃ℓ_Cε_{K′}} − 1) pointwise (the map's (7)(b) at k = K′, with the constraint M^{K′}U_{K′}(V_{K′}) = V_{K′} on every bond of ℓ₀, CRE (2.12)). W_χ(C^{(K′)};·) is one fixed continuous function on the fixed compact space G^{bonds(T^{(K′)})}. So the objects whose convergence is in question are the measures μ^{[K]}_ℓ := ρ^{[K]}_ℓdV/Z^{[K]} (sub-probability measures, total mass w_ℓ), the numbers E_const,K′, and the K-dependent bounded functions β^{(K′)}_{K′}, I_{K′}, E^∂_{K′}(ℓ) and the large-field dressings, integrated against those measures. A limit of W_ren(ε) is a statement about the whole sum (6.3): Bałaban's effective measure at a fixed physical scale tested against the fixed coarse loop, together with the remainder r_K, whose parts are K-dependent functions (through channels (i)–(ii)) integrated against K-dependent measures; weak convergence of the measures alone would not give it, since the test functions in r_K move with K.
- Every printed result of Bałaban's at the stopping scale is a bound uniform in K (CRE Theorem 2's E₁ and R₁ "independent of j, k, Ω, {Ω_j}, {Λ_j}, T", p.263; Corollary 3's E_± "independent of η and T", p.264, but dependent on g_k; LFR II (0.1)'s E_± "independent of k, T_η, U_k", p.356), and the family of all these K-dependent functions is uniformly bounded on the fixed compact space. Uniform bounds give compactness (every sequence of sub-probability measures on a compact metric space has a weakly convergent subsequence; the bounded sequence W_ren(ε_K) has convergent subsequences), never convergence of the full sequence; convergence needs a Cauchy statement across K, which compares two different runs and which neither Bałaban's step (one run, k → k+1) nor D2's step map (the same) contains.
- The large-field terms are not small by counting (CRE p.244, LFR1 p.175: the per-plaquette factor "does not give any positive power of ε", against ≍ (T_ph/ε)⁴ fine plaquettes); for K′ ≤ N, where no R-operation has acted, the weight w_{ℓ₀} of the small-field term is not shown to be bounded below as K → ∞ by any available estimate (item (4.1); a lower bound of the same order as the diverging count is expected and not proved, map l.376), so on the available bounds the ℓ₀-term alone is not the limit object. With the R-operation (K′ > N, i.e. for every large K) the surviving large-field regions carry their fundamental factors and the renormalised regions created at step K′ leave functions of V_{K′} only (LFR2 pp.391–392), but the total relative weight of the terms other than ℓ₀ is not bounded by any printed statement (item (4.3): the inequality Z_ℓ ≤ ∏_c e^{−(3/2)p₀(g_{K′})}Z_{ℓ₀} is what would bound it, and it is not derived). The cancellation of the far large-field terms against the partition function is performed by the R-operation itself, conditionally on D2.7 (item (4.4)); the near ones are D2.7's observable part.
- κ^{(K′)} is a number that is not shown to converge: its perimeter part is (P_phys/ε) times a running mass that is at most O(1/log(1/ε)) in modulus, and its corner part is bounded by exp(O((log log(1/ε))^{1+6p₀})) (item (6)). Dividing it out is the exact form of the perimeter and corner renormalisation, and W_ren(ε) is K-uniformly bounded given D(K′) on every labelled term and the relative-weight inequality of (4.3).

### (1) The stopping lattice and the coupling window (derived)

- *Status: derived from the map's l.1093–1094 and CRE (2.6)–(2.8).* With ε = uL^{−K} the torus of physical period T_ph has T_ph/ε = (T_ph/u)L^K fine sites per side and N_s = (T_ph/u)L^{K−K′} = (T_ph/u)L^{j(C)} sites per side at step K′; C has side ℓ_C = pL^{−q}u/a = pL^{j(C)−q} step-K′ bonds. Both are fixed once j(C) is, and j(C) is fixed by l.1093 under the prescription. The torus condition (TC) and the admissible-sides condition hold at K′ by l.1094 (checked there, reviewed in round 45).
- *The coupling at the stopping scale.* CRE (2.6), p.255, with n = K, m = K′ (n − m = j(C)): g_{K′} ≤ (1+β₀)g_K and g_K ≤ (1+g_K²β′j(C))^{1/2}g_{K′}, so g_{K′} ∈ [g_K(1+γ²β′j(C))^{−1/2}, (1+β₀)g_K], a window fixed by the prescription; CRE (2.8), p.256: ε_{K′} ≤ (1+β₀)(1+g_K²β′j(C))^{β₀}ε_K; CRE (2.9): R_{K′} ≤ L(1+g_K²β′j(C))^{β₀}R_K.   - the Wilson term (1/g_{K′}²)A(U_{K′}(V)) is the minimal ε-lattice action under the block constraint, divided by g_{K′}²: channels (i) and (ii);
  - 𝐄_{K′}(U_{K′}) = Σ_{j=1}^{K′}[𝐄^{(j)}(T,g_{j−1},U_{K′}) − 𝐄^{(j)}(T,g_{j−1},1) − β_j(g_{j−1})A(φ_j,U_{K′})] (CRE (2.25) with Λ_j = T): channels (i)–(iii). - *(3.5) The limit object, and exactly what prevents one (derived).* By (2), W_ren(ε) = e^{E_const,K′}μ^{[K]}_{ℓ₀}(W_χ(C^{(K′)};·)) + r_K. The sequence of sub-probability measures μ^{[K]}_{ℓ₀} on the compact space G^{bonds(T^{(K′)})} has weakly convergent subsequences (the space of sub-probability measures on a compact metric space is weakly compact), the sequence E_const,K′ is bounded, and |r_K| is bounded uniformly in K (items (4)–(5), given the relative-weight inequality of (4.3)); so along a subsequence K_i → ∞ every term converges, and lim_iW_ren(ε_{K_i}) = e^{Ē}μ̄(W_χ(C^{(K′)};·)) + r̄ exists, with μ̄ a sub-probability measure on the fixed space. This is the limit object available without further input: a stopping-scale effective measure μ̄ on G^{bonds(T^{(K′)})} (together with the limits of the recent large-field terms, item (4)) integrated against the fixed coarse loop, plus a bounded remainder. What prevents the limit along the full sequence: there is no Cauchy estimate |W_ren(ε_{K+1}) − W_ren(ε_K)| ≤ c_K with Σ_Kc_K < ∞, and within it no estimate comparing μ^{[K+1]}_{ℓ₀}, which is the output of K′ + 1 steps from the lattice ε/L, with μ^{[K]}_{ℓ₀}, the output of K′ steps from the lattice ε, on the common space, nor one comparing the remainders r_{K+1} and r_K, whose test functions move with K; by channel (ii) the comparison is between the K-th and the (K+1)-th lattice approximations of the same constrained variational problem (CRE (2.12)) and of the fluctuation integrals built on them, under the coupling matching of RG I Theorem 2 (which fixes g_K = g and hence the window of (1) at every K). The step map of D2 and Bałaban's step compare k with k+1 inside one run and never two runs; so nothing in D2, in the closures, or in the prints yields the Cauchy estimate. Its ingredients, in the project's record: the coupling matching (in hand, Cor. 8.2), a Lipschitz dependence of a step's output on its input's fine structure, and the loop remainders (the project's Cauchy-route inputs). This is the item of map l.16, now with its statement fixed: it is the Cauchy property of the whole renormalised expectation, the effective measures tested against the fixed coarse loop together with the loop remainders r_K.

### (4) Item (b): the large-field terms on ε-dependent lattices and their normalisation against the partition function

- *(4.1) Counting does not control them (checked against the print).* CRE p.244 and LFR1 p.175 (parallel passages, the displays differ): for d = 4 "the bare coupling constant behaves asymptotically as (a + b log ε^{−1})^{−1/2}, for ε → 0, … hence the bound does not give any positive power of ε. It is still small for ε small, and it controls a large number of steps, but this number is a small fraction of the total number of steps." The per-cube large-field factor at step 1 is at best exp(−½p₀²(g_0)) (p.244's display, with p₀(g_0) = A₀(log g_0^{−2})^{p₀}), and the fixed torus has (T_ph/ε)⁴/(LM₂R_0)⁴ cubes of CRE (1.1), so the available bound on the total weight of the terms with a large-field region at step 1, cubes × factor, is ≍ (T_ph/ε)⁴(LM₂R_0)^{−4}exp(−½A₀²(log g_0^{−2})^{2p₀}) with g_0^{−2} ≍ log ε^{−1}, which diverges as ε → 0.
