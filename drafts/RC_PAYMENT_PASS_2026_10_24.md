# The k = 1 Construction Check at Λ: The Payment of (β) and (γ)

{#RP.0} **Purpose.** The k = 1 construction check (IT-1 note §3c(4″)) says that CRE's step-1 construction uses no property of Λ₁ beyond (P1)–(P4) and (α)–(δ) (RC_PROOF_PASS_2026_10_23.md). Two of these need a payment at Λ = Λ₁^fin: (β), the 𝐁^{(1)}-volume near the new boundary of Λ, and (γ), the new holes of Λ.
{#RP.0-1} Both are paid by the j = 1 factor of LFR II (1.79). IT-1 note §3f(2) R1 (D2) bounds this factor at a labelled configuration, as a bound on the integrand of T′_k(X) for a step k at which the 𝐑-operation acts on X.
{#RP.0-2} That bound concerns a printed object once two facts are proved. (a) LFR I Sect. 1 builds the operation T′_k as printed when Z₁ = Λᶜ is among the step-1 data. (b) CRE's steps 2, …, h and the 𝐑-operations before step k accept the step-1 data (Ω₁, Λ₁, Λ) for the large-field objects and the 𝐑-terms; the small-field objects are map #MD2.2.3.2–#MD2.2.3.23.
{#RP.0-3} Section 1 states the payment chain as printed. Section 2 proves (a), Section 3 proves (b), and Section 4 treats the components on which the basic step never acts. Section 5 assembles the payment, Section 6 lists the results of Bałaban that are used, and Section 7 proves the j = 1 factor for every T-form.

## Notation and Three Geometric Facts

{#RP.N1} Z is the union of the class components of Z_k = Λ_kᶜ (LFR I p.177), and inside LFR I Sect. 1, Z_j stands for Z_j ∩ Z. h := k − N, k₀ := k − N₀ with N > N₀ (p.179). For a class component X, 𝒳 := Z₁ ∩ X.

{#RP.N2} Lengths are in fine units, in which the step-j lattice T_{L^{−j}} has spacing L^j. ℓ_j := L^jMR_j is the side of the MR_j-cubes of the step-j lattice, and ℓ₁ = ℓ. Ω_j and Λ_j are unions of ℓ_j-cubes (CRE p.256), and Z_j′, the union of the LMR_{j+1}-cubes of the step-j lattice meeting Z_j, is a union of ℓ_{j+1}-cubes (p.264).
{#RP.N2-1} On these sets a superscript ∼n adds n layers of cubes of their own scale, and ∼−n removes n layers (CRE p.255: Ω_j∖Ω_j^{∼−1} = Ω_j ∩ (Ω_jᶜ)^∼). The cubes □ of LFR I (1.3) at step j are the LM₂R_j-cubes of the step-j lattice, of side L^{j+1}M₂R_j (p.178), and □^{∼n} adds n layers of these cubes.

{#RP.N5} *(G3) The step-1 region.* (P2) gives Λ ⊆ Λ₁ ⊆ Ω₁^{∼−2}, so two layers of ℓ-cubes separate Λ from Ω₁ᶜ, and Ω₁ᶜ ⊆ Λᶜ = Z₁ (RC_PROOF_PASS, (P1)–(P4) for Λ).
