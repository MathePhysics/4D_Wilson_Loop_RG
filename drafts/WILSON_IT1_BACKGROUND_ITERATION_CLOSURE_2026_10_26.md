## What the Objects Are

- **Two chains, one measure.** Bałaban's step k → k+1 acts on the integrand of a labelled term by the seven operations of the map's D2.2(2) *Linearity* (decompositions of unity, pointwise identities on supports, a change of variables, regrouping, splitting the integral, the fluctuation substitution V_k = e^{iB′}V^{(k)}, gauge fixing). Every one of them is linear in the observable.
  So the step map 𝒯_{k+1}, which sends a function 𝒲_k of the step-k variables to the function 𝒲_{k+1} of the step-(k+1) variables with Σ_ℓ∫𝒲_k ρ_k^ℓ dV_k = Σ_ℓ∫𝒲_{k+1} ρ_{k+1}^ℓ dV_{k+1} (the numerator identity of D2.2(2)), is linear, and it is the same map for every observable.
  - The *fine chain* F starts from the bare loop W_χ(C;V₀) and has the D(j) form 𝒲^f_j = κ_f^{(j)}e^{𝓔^f_j}[f_j + I^f_j], f_j = W_χ(C;U_j(V_j)) the leading word, I^f_j the insertion sum (D2.3(8)).
  - The *coarse chain* G_k starts at step k from the coarse loop g_k^{(k)} := W_χ(C^{(k)};U^{[k]}_k), defined on every label (§1.6). Here U^{[k]}_j(c) := (M^kU_j)(c) on the *inner* bonds c of C^{(k)}, those whose two scale-k blocks lay in Ω_k at step k, and U^{[k]}_j(c) := T_c(U_j), the transport of U_j along c (map #MOMST.82), on the others; U_j = U_j(V_j) is the minimiser of CRE (2.12).
  - On an inner bond (M^kU_k)(c) = V_k(c) by the constraint of CRE (2.11)–(2.12). So g_k^{(k)} = W_χ(C^{(k)};V_k) on the terms on which every bond of C^{(k)} is inner, which include those whose scale-k blocks of C lie in Ω_k; the other terms are (iv)'s.
  - Given (iii) and (iv-a)'s attached species, the coarse chain has the D(j) form 𝒲^{g_k}_j = κ^{g_k,(j)}e^{𝓔^{g_k}_j}[g_k^{(j)} + I^{g_k}_j] for j ≥ k, with κ^{g_k,(k)} = 1, 𝓔^{g_k}_k = 0, I^{g_k}_k = 0 and the leading word g_k^{(j)} = W_χ(C^{(k)};U^{[k]}_j), which is W_χ(C^{(k)};M^kU_j(V_j)) on those terms: the coarse chain of the map's (7)(e).
- **What that forces.** The background term β_k^{(j)} = f_j − g_k^{(j)} (§1.4) is the difference of the two chains' leading words, and the word w_j shed at the step j → j+1 is exactly the difference of the two chains' slot-insertion words at that step (§1.4).
  The shed words are therefore never separate objects: each is averaged at step j+1 as part of the two chains' step maps, whose outputs are the two D(j+1) forms.
  Their first-order parts are the two chains' class-(i) activities at step j+1, even in the coupling by the map's (5) run at step j+1 (§2.2); their flat second-order parts are the two chains' own δm_{j+1}, ζ_{j+1}, E°_{j+1} (§2.3); their deviation parts are the two chains' Y^d insertions (§2.4).
  The "separate perimeter and corner absorption for the coarse chain and its normalisation" is the coarse chain's own D2.3, and the normalisation it induces is κ^{g_k,(K)}; the ratio of the two normalisations over the steps k+1, …, K is ∏_jr_j, the explicit factor of N_k in §3.1.
- **The identity.** E[W(C)] − N_k⟨g_k^{(k)}⟩_k is κ_f^{(K)} times the final-scale expectation of three differences over the labels where both chains have their D(K) forms, plus the whole-label remainder R_bad over the other labels (§3.1). The three differences are the background value β_k^{(K)}, the exponent difference, and the insertion difference. The β_k of different k enter one identity only through the telescoping of the coarse loops across scales (§3.3), where they appear under the single normalisation κ_f^{(K)}; the map's (7)(d) sum is the pointwise bound for that telescoping.
  At k = K the chain is trivial, and E[W(C)] − κ_f^{(K)}⟨e^{𝓔^f_K}g_K^{(K)}⟩_K = κ_f^{(K)}⟨e^{𝓔^f_K}(β_K^{(K)} + I^f_K)⟩_K with |β_K^{(K)}| ≤ Lip_χ(e^{8a_dc_hB₃ℓ_Cε_K} − 1) on the terms whose scale-K blocks of C lie in Ω_K (§3.4). On the other labels, ℒ_K, the fine part is bounded on D2's fixed torus by (iv-e); (iv-b)'s per-cube weight is needed only for a volume-uniform form. This is the statement the construction uses at the stopping scale, and it needs neither (iii) nor the sum over k.
