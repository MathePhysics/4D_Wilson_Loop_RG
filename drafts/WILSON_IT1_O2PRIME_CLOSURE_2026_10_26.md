Companion of WILSON_IT1_D26_CLOSURE_2026_10_24.md ("the closure") and of WILSON_IT1_D2_STEP_MAP_2026_10_22.md ("the map"). It closes the closure's open item (O2′) (closure §6.5, §8 l.161): the acceptance by the later RG steps of §6.5's merged contour-anchored activities together with the later-step carry of the retreat slots. Sources: the IT-1 note WILSON_IT1_LINE_DEFECT_2026_10_21.md ("IT-1"), the map, the closure, and the transcriptions Balaban\tex\<paper>\parts\pNNN_corrected.tex of CRE (pp.255–258, 264–265), LFR II (pp.380, 384–386), and LFR I (p.177), at the lines cited.

**Open after this work:** nothing of O2′ within D2.6 (k+1 ≤ N): the retreat slots and the retreat-anchored masses are species attached to components of Z₁ = Λᶜ and are carried by closure §5 and paid by closure §7 under the inputs those sections already list (O3 at n = 1, O6's closure given R-m and (R-m′) for the ω-dependent members of step ≥ 2, O4 for the t > 0 species, (T0), the smallness conditions, and IT-1's per-component factor R-d″(ii) of §3f(2) (D2), derived there and not reviewed, whose use at Λ is tied to O2); what lies outside D2.6 is unchanged: O2 (the pair (Λ₁, Λ)) and D2.7's joint linking expansion of the label sum at the R-operation (closure 7.4(b)(ii)), whose links now include the lw slots' Λ-dependence within 2ℓ of Z₁ (an existing D2.7 route, not a new item).

## What the Objects Are and What That Forces

- Z₁ is the large-field region of the step-1 term for the later steps. CRE defines Z_j := Λ_jᶜ ((2.3), p.255 l.24–26), and the step k+1 starts from "a large field region Z_k = Λ_kᶜ" (p.264 l.52). At the defect step the step-1 sequence of domains is (Ω₁, Λ₁, Λ), with the retreated Λ = Λ₁^fin ⊆ Λ₁ playing CRE's Λ₁ for the next step (IT-1 l.740: "Λ plays the role of CRE's Λ₁ for the next step (Z₁ = Λᶜ)"; map D2.2(3)(b) l.595, l.603, l.609, reviewed). So Z₁ = Λᶜ, and it contains the retreat region: Λᶜ = Λ₁ᶜ ∪ (Λ₁∖Λ₁^{(0)}) ∪ Ret(Λ), Ret(Λ) := Λ₁^{(0)}∖Λ (closure 6.2(c)). The closure's §6.2 and §7.2 write Z₁ = Λ₁ᶜ instead (l.103, l.139); that is the inconsistency, and everything below follows from correcting it (§1).
- Every free bond of the step-1 term lies in Z₁. J(Λ) = J ∪ (I∖D(Λ)) is the set of free bonds without an endpoint in Λ (V3 L2451, L2978, L2336 as quoted in closure 6.1–6.2), so J(Λ) ⊆ E(Λᶜ) = E(Z₁): the constant c_J = 100ℓ + 2 of closure 6.2, the distance of J(Λ) from Λ₁ᶜ ∪ Ret(Λ), becomes 0 when the distance is taken from Z₁ (§2). Hence every lw slot with Y_i ≠ 0 lies within R_t + 2L + 1 ≤ ℓ of Z₁, the near/retreat split of closure 6.2 is void, and every step-1 anchor of closure 7.1 lies in Z₁ or within R_t of it. There are no retreat slots and no retreat-anchored masses: every such object is an insertion of an attached species of a component X of Z₁ = Λᶜ.
- The step-2 small-field region avoids all of Z₁ by eight layers. CRE's step-2 construction starts from the cover Z₁′ of Z₁ = Λᶜ by LMR₂-cubes (p.264 l.52–56, with Z₁ = Λᶜ by map l.603), and Ω₂ = (Q₂′^{∼2} ∪ (B²(P₂¹)ᶜ)^{∼3})ᶜ with (B²(P₂¹))ᶜ ⊇ Z₁′^{∼5} ((3.5), p.265 l.15–19, l.43), so Ω₂ ⊆ (Z₁′^{∼8})ᶜ. Ret(Λ) ⊆ Z₁ is therefore at least eight layers of LMR₂-cubes from Ω₂: "where Ret(Λ) meets Ω₂" (closure §6.5, §8) is empty, and closure §5's bridge applies at every step from n = 1 on (§3).
- The components of Z₁ = Λᶜ are the label polymers. IT-1 §3c(4⁗)(d) l.741 takes the regions of CRE's factorisation (2.19) to be the ∗-components X of Λᶜ, IT-1 §3j(6) l.1760 attributes LFR II's factor e^{−κ₁(X)−2p₀(g₁)} to them, and closure §7.2 runs its ledger per X. With Z₁ = Λᶜ the attachment units of closure §2.0 (components of Z_{k+1} = Λ_{k+1}ᶜ at k+1 = 1) and the payment units coincide, and §6.5's merge of label polymers into contour-anchored activities has nothing left to merge: on each labelled term the subset expansion of f_ξ(m̂) is the multilinear expansion in the lw(X) insertions, which the later steps see as they see att(1, X) (§5).

## 1. Z₁ at Step 1 Is Λᶜ

*The two statements.* The closure writes "Z₁ = Λ₁ᶜ (CRE (2.3))" (6.2 *Units*, l.103) and "Every component Z of Z₁ = Λ₁ᶜ lies in one label polymer X. Λ ⊆ Λ₁^{(0)} ⊆ Λ₁ (6.2), so Λ₁ᶜ ⊆ Λᶜ" (7.2 *Ledger at j = 1*, l.139). The map writes, in the reviewed item D2.2(3)(b): "Λ = Λ₁^fin ⊆ Λ₁ is made by V3's retreat and plays CRE's Λ₁ at the later steps, with Z₁ = Λᶜ (IT-1 note §3c(4⁗), §3f(2))" (l.595); "Z_k = Λ_kᶜ, pp.264–265. It is the input of Z_k′ … The construction takes any union of cubes, so at k = 1 it applies to Z₁ = Λᶜ" (l.603); "(2.19)–(2.22): T_k(Z_k)'s factorisation and C^{(0)}(Λ), with Z₁ = Λᶜ" (l.609).

*Decision.* CRE (2.3) is Z_j = Λ_jᶜ for the domain Λ_j of the sequence {Λ_j} that labels the term (p.255 l.24–26; p.257 (2.18): the density is a sum over the admissible sequences of domains, and p.264 l.52: "The term has a large field region Z_k = Λ_kᶜ"). At the defect step the domain that the later steps use in Λ₁'s place is Λ: the renormalisation in (1.26)–(1.30) is at Λ (map l.599, item 2), the subtraction E₁ is at Λ (map l.600), the determining set 𝐁₁(Λ)|_{Ω₂ᶜ} of (2.14) uses Λ (map l.604, item 7), and the step-2 restrictions are built around the cover of Λᶜ (map l.603, item 6). The original Λ₁ is an outer parameter of T₁^{Λ₁,Λ} (IT-1 l.738, l.740: "The original Λ₁ is a parameter of T₁^{Λ₁,Λ}, as Ω₁ and Λ₁ are of CRE's T₁ and E₁"). So CRE (2.3) at step 1 of the defect construction reads Z₁ = Λᶜ. IT-1 uses it so throughout: the modified (1.76) is "Z₁ = Λᶜ ⊂ ⋃_G G^{∼107}" (§3f(2) Minor 4, l.1309), the label polymers are "the components of Λᶜ" carrying "LFR II's factor e^{−κ₁(Z)−2p₀(g₁)}" (§3j(6), l.1760), and §3g(2) l.1468 has "The components of Λᶜ are the polymers". The closure's l.103 and l.139 apply (2.3) with CRE's Λ₁ in a construction that has replaced it; they are wrong, and l.139's own attribution of the fundamental factor to the components X of Λᶜ (correct) shows the inconsistency inside §7.2.

*Consequence for closure §6.2.* With Z₁ = Λᶜ, Λ₁ᶜ ∪ Ret(Λ) ⊆ Z₁ and also Λ₁∖Λ₁^{(0)} ⊆ Z₁, so the distance of a slot or an anchor from Z₁ is at most its distance from Λ₁ᶜ ∪ Ret(Λ); §2 shows that it is in fact 0 for every free bond. The split of §6.2 into near and retreat slots, which was by distance to Λ₁ᶜ, has no retreat class. The replacement text is in the section *Proposed Correction of D2.6 Closure §6.2*.

*Which components.* CRE (2.19) factorises T_k over any representation of Z_k "as a union of disjoint regions, e.g. as a union of connected components" (p.258 l.3–9), and LFR II states its factors per component of Z_j (p.384 l.4–6). The components of Z₁ used below are the ∗-components X of Λᶜ (ℓ-cubes sharing a point), as in IT-1 l.741 and closure 7.2: a ∗-component is a disjoint union of face-connected components, so (2.19) factorises over the X, and the per-component factor of §4 is derived for X directly (its geometry joins every point of X inside Λᶜ to a generator, whichever adjacency is used). The closure's finer bookkeeping (components Z of Λ₁ᶜ inside a label polymer X, with the constant C_X) is then superfluous; it stays valid, since a single Z = X is a case of it with C_X ≥ 1.

## 2. Every Free Bond Lies in Z₁; Every Slot Is a Near Slot

*The free bonds.* E_{Ω,*} = I ⊔ J with I = D(Λ₁) (V3 L2223, L2336, as quoted in closure 6.1 class (III)), D(Λ) = {b ∈ I : b has an endpoint in Λ} (IT-1 l.1462), and J(Λ) = J ∪ (I∖D(Λ)) (V3 L2978, closure 6.1). J = E_{Ω,*}∖D(Λ₁) has no endpoint in Λ₁ (V3 L2336, closure 6.2(a)), hence none in Λ ⊆ Λ₁; a bond of I∖D(Λ) has no endpoint in Λ by the definition of D(Λ) (V3 L2451: "a free bond outside D(Λ) has no endpoint in Λ", closure 6.2(a)). So every bond of J(Λ) has both endpoints in Λᶜ = Z₁:

  J(Λ) ⊆ E(Z₁), and likewise J_col(Λ) ⊆ J(Λ) ⊆ E(Z₁) and J₁(Λ) ⊆ J_col(Λ) ⊆ E(Z₁) (V3 L2336, closure 6.2).

The closure's c_J = 100ℓ + 2 is the distance of J(Λ) from the smaller set Λ₁ᶜ ∪ Ret(Λ) (6.2(a)–(c): J within 0 of Λ₁ᶜ, D∖D^{(0)} within 100ℓ + 1 of Λ₁ᶜ through Λ₁∖Λ₁^{(0)}, D^{(0)}∖D(Λ) within 1 of Ret(Λ)); measured from Z₁ ⊇ Λ₁ᶜ ∪ (Λ₁∖Λ₁^{(0)}) ∪ Ret(Λ) the three distances are 0. The constant 100ℓ of 6.2(b) (V3 Lemma c5-3-0(b)) and the 102ℓ, 103ℓ of 6.2(c) and IT-1 l.868 (Lemma c5-3-1(c), with 𝒬⁺) are not needed here; they stay where IT-1 uses them (the modified (1.76), §4).

*The slots.* By closure 6.2 *Where Y_i can be nonzero*, a class-(I) slot i has Y_i ≠ 0 only if an input bond of its row is nonzero: m̂_b ≠ 0 only for d(b, J₁(Λ)) ≤ R_t (IT-1 §3j(2), (6) *Range*), a_old and A_e live on J(Λ), and the input set 𝓕_{r_i} lies within 2L + 1 of r_i (V3 L2598). So Y_i ≠ 0 forces d(i, J(Λ)) ≤ R_t + 2L + 1, hence

  d(i, Z₁) ≤ R_lw′ := R_t + 2L + 1 ≤ ℓ,

the last inequality being closure 6.2's g₀-threshold R_t + 2L + 3 ≤ ℓ (closure §8 l.160). A class-(III) slot lies within 2L of Λ₁ᶜ ⊆ Z₁ (closure 6.1). Class (II) has Y_i = 0. So every slot with Y_i ≠ 0 is a near slot in the sense of closure 6.2 with Z₁ = Λᶜ and R_lw′ in place of R_lw = R_t + c_J + 2L + 1: its species is lw(X), X the component of Z₁ nearest to i (ties by the closure's fixed order), and X is at distance ≤ ℓ, i.e. i lies in an ℓ-cube within one layer of X's cubes. The retreat class is empty, and §6.5 *Retreat slots* has no slots to pay.

*The step-1 anchors of closure 7.1.* The four retreat-anchored masses of 7.1 (l.126) are anchored at: (a) target bonds b_W ∈ E(B_ξ) ∪ (I∖D(Λ)) with b_W ∈ D^{(0)}∖D(Λ) (IT-1 §3d(4), case D2(d), l.867–869); (b) the E^∂ cluster terms meeting D(Λ)ᶜ only in Ret(Λ); (c) the inputs b with h(m̂_b) ≠ 0 within R_t of J_col(Λ) only near Ret(Λ); (d) the step-1 marks anchored in D^{(0)}∖D(Λ). In every case the anchor is a bond of J(Λ) ⊆ E(Z₁) or lies within R_t ≤ ℓ of one (c), or is a cluster meeting Ret(Λ) ⊆ Z₁ (b). So 7.1's booking rule becomes one rule: every anchor is assigned to the component X of Z₁ nearest to it (ties by the fixed order; an anchor with an endpoint in Z₁ goes to that endpoint's component), and the mass joins A₁(X) (att(1, X), first bullet), B₁(X) (the marks, second bullet), the inputs term (fourth bullet), or 7.4(a)'s step-1 exposed-segment term, respectively. The set 𝔅_X of the bonds assigned to X (7.1 l.135) becomes {b : d(b, Z₁) ≤ R_t and X nearest to b} ∪ E(X), which lies in the ℓ-cubes within one layer of X, at most 3^d #cubes₁(X) of them, in place of the 205^d #cubes₁(Z) of the 101ℓ-neighbourhood. The counts of 7.1 and 6.3 (205^d, 102 layers, 101ℓ) remain valid upper bounds and need not be changed; 3^d and 5^d are the sharp ones (6.3: slots within ℓ of X lie in the ℓ-cubes within two layers of X, at most 5^d #cubes₁(X)).

*What this removes.* The factor 2 per ℓ-cube of Ret(Λ) for the retreat slots (closure 6.5 *Retreat slots*), the second factor 2 per ℓ-cube for the retreat-anchored masses (7.1 l.135), the resulting 4^{#ℓ-cubes(X)} per label polymer and the share 3·2^d log 2·(d₁′(X)+1) carved out of κ₁(X) (6.5 *Absorption*, 7.2 *Ledger at j = 1*) are no longer needed: the masses they paid are now inside A₁(X), B₁(X), the inputs term, and the exposed-segment term of X, which the ledger pays through ε_A(1)(d₁′(X)+1) ≤ (K₁/8)(d₁′(X)+1). The conditions K₁ ≥ 4(1 + C_X)B₁ + 12·2^d log 2 and ε_A(1) ≤ K₁/(8C_X) of closure §8 remain sufficient (the share is simply not spent), and the condition 4C_I″g₀(r+1)·205^dc_CM_VR₁ ≤ log 2 (§8 l.160, the retreat slots) is no longer used.

## 3. The Bridge Holds at Every Step from n = 1 On

*What closure §5 needs.* (T3) (closure l.92) needs dist(Ω_{n+1}, A) ≥ 5R_{n+1} π_n-sides for the set A from which the lineage extent of the species is measured, so that a touched or merged descendant pays e^{−(a/2)·5R_{n+1}} per step (δ_n of 5.0, 5.3). For the step-1 species the closure measures the extent from 𝒩₁, the 102ℓ-neighbourhood of Λ₁ᶜ (5.6), and gets the bridge only from the step n_B on, with the constant C_lin = 2(1 + C_δc̄)^{n_B} for the bridgeless steps. The worry recorded in §6.5 and §8, "§5's bridge argument does not apply where Ret(Λ) meets Ω₂", is the case of a retreat chain reaching into Λ₁ as far as Ω₂; with Z₁ = Λᶜ it does not occur.

*Units (closure §1 l.32, 6.2).* A π_n-side is M step-n units; an LMR_{n+1}-cube of CRE's step-(n+1) construction is LR_{n+1} π_n-sides; ℓ = LM_VR₁ fine bonds = R₁ π₁-sides (M_V = M, closure 7.1 *Which M*; a π₁-side is LM fine bonds).

*The set.* Put 𝒩₁′ := the 2ℓ-neighbourhood of Z₁ = Λᶜ (fine bonds), i.e. the points within 2R₁ π₁-sides of Z₁. Every anchor of a step-1 species lies in it: the stencil part of an lw slot within R_lw′ + R_loc ≤ ℓ + 2L + 2 ≤ 2ℓ of Z₁ (§2; R_loc = 2L + 2, closure 6.4); the target bonds b_W ∈ E(B_ξ) ∪ (I∖D(Λ)) ⊆ E(Z₁) (§2); the marks' anchors in J_col(Λ) ⊆ E(Z₁) or at inputs in J(Λ) ⊆ E(Z₁) (closure 7.1 *Step-1 marks*); the inputs b within R_t ≤ ℓ of J_col(Λ) (§2). So 5.6 *Production* holds with 𝒩₁′: ext^{𝒩′,(1)}(W) ≤ 2ext₁(W) + 1 for every member anchored in 𝒩₁′, and the stencil part of an lw piece Y_{i,X} lies in 𝒩₁′, so 6.4(b)'s ext^𝒩 ≤ 2(|X| + N_adj + 1) holds with 𝒩₁′.

*n = 1 (the step 1 → 2).* By CRE p.264 l.52–56 and p.265 (3.5), with Z₁ = Λᶜ (map l.603): Ω₂ ⊆ (Z₁′^{∼8})ᶜ, Z₁′ the cover of Λᶜ by LMR₂-cubes, so every point within 8 layers of LMR₂-cubes of Λᶜ lies in Ω₂ᶜ; this is closure (G1) at step 2 with Λ as the step-1 Λ₁ (closure l.34 states (G1) for Z_k ⊇ Λ_kᶜ and uses 6 of the 8 layers). In π₁-sides: dist(Ω₂, Z₁) ≥ 6LR₂. The set 𝒩₁′ reaches 2R₁ ≤ 2(L+1)R₂ π₁-sides from Z₁ (CRE (2.9), p.256 l.14–15: R_m ≤ L(1 + g_n²β′(n−m))^{β₀}R_n ≤ (L+1)(n−m)^{β₀}R_n at n − m = 1). Hence

  dist(Ω₂, 𝒩₁′) ≥ 6LR₂ − 2(L+1)R₂ = (4L − 2)R₂ ≥ 5R₂ π₁-sides for L ≥ 2.

In particular Ret(Λ) ⊆ Z₁ is at distance ≥ 6LR₂ π₁-sides from Ω₂: Ret(Λ) ∩ Ω₂ = ∅.

*n ≥ 2.* 2R₁ ≤ 2(L+1)R₂ ≤ 6LR₂ for L ≥ 1, so 𝒩₁′ ⊆ Ω₂ᶜ ⊆ Λ₂ᶜ ⊆ Λ_nᶜ for every n ≥ 2 (Λ_{m+1} ⊆ Ω_{m+1} ⊆ Λ_m: CRE p.265 (3.5), p.269 (3.20), as closure (T3) cites them). By (G1) at step n+1, dist(Ω_{n+1}, Λ_nᶜ) ≥ 6LR_{n+1} ≥ 5R_{n+1} π_n-sides, so dist(Ω_{n+1}, 𝒩₁′) ≥ 5R_{n+1} π_n-sides.

*Conclusion.* With the extent of every step-1 species measured from 𝒩₁′, (T3) and 5.5 run verbatim at every step n ≥ 1: the descendants made at step n+1 have weighted mass ≤ δ_nM_n, and 5.3's product is ≤ e^{C_δc̄S_R} ≤ 2 under 5.0's conditions (C_δc̄S_R ≤ log 2). There are no bridgeless steps, n_B and C_lin are not needed, and the carried total of every step-1 species (lw(X), att(1, X), the inputs term, att(1, coll)) is ≤ 2 times its production total, as for every later species. The closure's C_lin ≥ 2 stays a valid upper bound wherever it is written (6.4(a), 7.1–7.2). **Derived** from CRE (2.9), (3.5), p.264 l.52–56, p.269 (3.20), map l.603, and closure (T3), 5.5, 5.6 with their inputs (unchanged: (T0), R-dec, R-m, R-adm, the sub-domain amendment, O3 at n = 1 for the members whose evaluation domain meets Ω₁∖Ω₂, the smallness conditions of 5.0); L ≥ 2 is implied by the map's L ≥ L₀.

*The lw slots at Ω₂.* The dependence set of an lw piece lies within R_dep = R_t + 6L + 2 of its bond i (closure 6.4), and i within R_lw′ of Z₁, so the whole dependence set lies within 2R_t + 8L + 3 ≤ 2ℓ of Z₁ (the g₀-threshold R_t + 4L + 2 ≤ ℓ, closure §8 l.160, next to R_t + 2L + 3 ≤ ℓ), inside 𝒩₁′ ⊆ Ω₂ᶜ. (T1)'s touch at the step 1 → 2 therefore needs a polymer from the dependence set to Ω₂, of length ≥ (4L − 2)R₂ π₁-sides: this is the bridge of the display above, and it is the whole content of "the later-step carry of the retreat slots" (closure §8 l.161) once the retreat slots are lw slots.
