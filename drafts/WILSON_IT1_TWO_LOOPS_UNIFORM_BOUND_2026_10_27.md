## {#304} What the Objects Are

- {#306} The pair observable W_{χ₁}(C₁;U)W_{χ₂}(C₂;U) is the normalised trace of π₁(Hol_UC₁) ⊗ π₂(Hol_UC₂) on V_{π₁} ⊗ V_{π₂} (V4 eq:two-loop-observable). It is a product of two closed words, not the trace of one holonomy.

## {#313} What That Forces

- {#315} **The normalisation factorises exactly.** The coefficients δm_{k+1} and ζ_{k+1} are ℤ⁴ quantities of a straight line and of a bent path, for one character (D2.3(5) *Independence*). Perimeters add, P_k(C₁ ⊔ C₂) = P_k(C₁) + P_k(C₂), and the pair has eight corners. So the pair's κ^{(K′)} is κ^{(K′)}_{C₁,χ₁}κ^{(K′)}_{C₂,χ₂}, with no cross factor ((5)).
  {#315-1} The cross constant E^× of (5) is the only cross term that the step map extracts. The loops stay coupled through the clusters of Q_k, which may hit insertions on both loops, through the cross insertions of (6), and through the common measure.
- {#316} **A cross group has no scalar flat value.** A group with arcs on both loops has a flat value in End(V_{π₁}) ⊗ End(V_{π₂}) (more tensor factors when its arc set on one loop has several intervals). This value commutes with π₁(u) ⊗ π₂(u) for u ∈ G, but it is not a scalar in general. So D2.3(3)'s subtraction c_𝒢T is not available for it. Cross groups get the flat value 0, as in V4 (a_W := 0, V4 L3590), and they are not contracted.
- {#317} **Cross groups are made with a doubly exponential decay in the distance to the stopping step.**
  - {#318} A cross group made at step k spans at least DL^{K′−k} step-k units.
  - {#319} The extent weight of (S-w) has the same rate at every step.
  - {#320} So the cross mass made at step k is ≤ C𝔞̄(ℓ₁+ℓ₂)L^{K′−k}e^{−ν_×DL^{K′−k}/M}, on every labelled term.
- {#321} **So the cross groups are carried, not taken out.**
  - {#322} They form their own species cross(j), last in D2.0(3)'s order. They are never contracted, so no step needs their flat value.
  - {#323} The D2.6 closure's carry lemma carries them without a bridge. Every later step can touch them, since the loops lie in the small-field region on most terms. So each step multiplies their mass by at most 1 + C_δc̄ ((6)), an 𝐑-step by (1 + C_δc̄)(1 + y^R_k) ((6B)).
  - {#324} The doubly exponential decay pays this geometric growth: the carried cross total A^× of (7) is uniform in K.
  - {#325} So the class carries everything to the stopping step, and the large-field terms are paid there, by B^×_LF, as for one loop. No step below K′ needs a large-field bound for the cross species. The attached species are carried by D2.6 as for one loop.
  - {#326} Taking the cross words out at the step that makes them doesn't work: on the large-field terms that step has no bound uniform in K.
