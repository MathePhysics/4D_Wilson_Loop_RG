## What That Forces

- **The normalisation factorises exactly.** The coefficients δm_{k+1} and ζ_{k+1} are ℤ⁴ quantities of a straight line and of a bent path, for one character (D2.3(5) *Independence*). Perimeters add, P_k(C₁ ⊔ C₂) = P_k(C₁) + P_k(C₂), and the pair has eight corners. So the pair's κ^{(K′)} is κ^{(K′)}_{C₁,χ₁}κ^{(K′)}_{C₂,χ₂}, with no cross factor ((5)). The cross constant E^× of (5) is the only cross term that the step map extracts. The loops stay coupled through the clusters of Q_k, which may hit insertions on both loops, through the cross insertions of (6), and through the common measure.
- **A cross group has no scalar flat value.** A group with arcs on both loops has a flat value in End(V_{π₁}) ⊗ End(V_{π₂}) (more tensor factors when its arc set on one loop has several intervals). This value commutes with π₁(u) ⊗ π₂(u) for u ∈ G, but it is not a scalar in general. So D2.3(3)'s subtraction c_𝒢T is not available for it. Cross groups get the flat value 0, as in V4 (a_W := 0, V4 L3590), and they are not contracted.
- **Cross groups are made with a doubly exponential decay in the distance to the stopping step.**
  - A cross group made at step k spans at least DL^{K′−k} step-k units.
  - The extent weight of (S-w) has the same rate at every step.
  - So the cross mass made at step k is ≤ C𝔞̄(ℓ₁+ℓ₂)L^{K′−k}e^{−ν_×DL^{K′−k}/M}, on every labelled term.
- **So the cross groups are carried, not taken out.**

  - The D2.6 closure's carry lemma carries them without a bridge. Every later step can touch them, since the loops lie in the small-field region on most terms. So each step multiplies their mass by at most 1 + C_δc̄ ((6)).
  - The doubly exponential decay pays this geometric growth: the carried cross total A^× of (7) is uniform in K.

### (4) Locality for the Pair (Derived by the Induction of D2.3(4), for Short Arcs)

(L×) Take any step k and a non-cross object of the pair on C_i: an insertion, a group with its occurrences, a cluster of the reference gas, or its weight, all of whose groups have short arcs on C_i (fewer than P_k(C_i)/2 traversals). Suppose its network has no point within r₀ of C_{3−i}.
- Then it is the object of the single-loop class of C_i with the same constituents, and it has the same value.
- Conversely, a single-loop object of C_i whose network has no point within r₀ of C_{3−i} is a non-cross object of the pair.
- The network of a group contains the domains of its old members and of the members of its merged occurrences (map l.1144: the supports, the arc hulls, and the merged occurrences' domains). Inductively, it therefore contains the domains of the group's whole lineage.

*Step 1.*
- V4 Lemma lem:two-loop-grouping(c): the C_i-groups of the pair are the single-square groups of C_i none of whose roots has a contact on C_{3−i}. They have the same interval, weight, footprint, and compatibility.
- a_W = a^{(i)}_W (V4 L3590).
- The paragraph *Cancellation* in the proof of Lemma lem:two-loop-cross: tuples of C_i-groups have the same Ursell coefficient and the same activities.
- A constituent with a contact on C_{3−i} lies within r₀ of C_{3−i}.
- At the retreated carrier D(Λ) these transfer: by the proof of Lemma lem:two-loop-retreated, part (i), the grouping lemma does not involve the carrier (V4 L3737).

*Step k → k+1.*
- A step-(k+1) object is built from its constituents by the composition, the joint cluster expansion, the merging rules, the flat value, and the hard core.
- For short arcs these use the contour only through the slots, arcs, and contact sets inside the object's network (D2.3(4) *Contour locality*, map l.1083; its proof is per object). A group with a short arc has only members with short arcs, since its arc contains theirs.
- The constituents are step-k objects whose networks lie inside the new network, so the hypothesis applies to them. The vacuum activities and occurrences do not involve the contour.
- Consider an old occurrence with arcs on both loops, that is, a cluster containing a C₁-group and a C₂-group. It puts arcs on both loops into the group it merges into, since the third merging rule takes the hull over J_Γ. That group is then a cross group.
- So no non-cross object contains such an occurrence or a cross insertion. Cross groups have activity 0 in the reference gas; in the words they are the cross insertions of (6).
- Groups long on C_i are not covered. D2.3(4)'s contour locality is for short arcs (map l.1083, l.1086), and the long-ness and the membership of a long group are properties of the whole term. The step-(k+1) analogue of V4 Lemma lem:two-loop-grouping(a) and (c) for long groups is not derived. (5) pays the clusters with long groups by (T′) instead.

### (7) The Count Check over the Steps (Derived)

- *A sum lemma.* For L ≥ 2 and a > 0: Σ_{m≥0}L^me^{−aL^m} ≤ e^{−a}(1 + 6/a), and Σ_{m≥0}L^{2m}e^{−aL^m} ≤ e^{−a}(1 + 12/a²).
