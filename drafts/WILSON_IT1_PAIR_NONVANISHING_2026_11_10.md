- {#NV.10} *Proposition.* If 𝔇 has a doubling tree, then |E_ε[F_𝔇]| ≥ Π_B|E_ε[F_B]| over its blocks B, and |W_ren(𝔇; ε)| ≥ Π_B|W_ren(B; ε)| for every K.
  {#NV.10-1} *Proof.* By induction on the tree. For a block it is an equality. For a double, #NV.8-1 and #NV.9-1 give |E_ε[F_{𝔇∪θ̂𝔇}]| = E_ε[F_𝔇·ΘF_𝔇] ≥ |E_ε[F_𝔇]|·|E_ε[F_{θ̂𝔇}]|. Here |E_ε[F_{θ̂𝔇}]| = |E_ε[F_𝔇]| ≥ Π_B|E_ε[F_B]| = Π_B|E_ε[F_{θ̂B}]|, by the induction hypothesis and |E_ε[ΘF]| = |E_ε[F]| (#RP.4-1). The κ-product of a configuration is the product over its blocks, so dividing by its modulus gives the second claim.
- {#NV.11} *Theorem (arbitrarily many loops).* Let 𝔇 have a doubling tree, and let ḡ lie below the thresholds of #NV.4 and #NV.5 for the data of its blocks (#NV.9-2). Then lim inf_K|W_ren(𝔇; ε_K)| ≥ Π_Bc_B > 0, with c_B = e^{−B_E}/4 for a single loop and e^{−E*}/4 for a pair, B_E and E* those of the block.
  {#NV.11-1} *Proof.* #NV.10 and, for each block, #NV.4 or #NV.5, whose bounds hold for every large K.
  {#NV.11-2} *How many.* Let h := L^{−q}u and start from one square of side h. If the configuration lies in [b, b + e] in direction μ, with e + h < T_ph/2, the plane a := b − h gives the image [b − 2h − e, b − 2h], at distance 2h, and a double of extent 2e + 2h in direction μ; the extents in the other directions do not change. So the extent after k_μ doublings in direction μ is at most 2^{k_μ}·3h − 2h.
  {#NV.11-2.1} Hence ⌊log₂(T_ph/(8h))⌋ doublings fit in each of the four directions, and n = 2^k loops with k = 4⌊log₂(T_phL^q/(8u))⌋, which grows without bound with q. The block data are the same for all of them, so one threshold serves every k at fixed q.
  {#NV.11-3} *The limit, and what #NV.11 adds to #NV.5.* The loops of 𝔇 form a family (#NV.1), each two of them as in #RP.6-3, so W_ren(𝔇; ε_K) converges by #NL10.7, and #NV.5 bounds the limit below, once the construction's L, ν and η also meet #NL12.1 for N_C = n: L ≥ (n/2)²L₀^×, ν ≤ 1/(500n) and 1000ηA_s < ϖ/(4n). #NV.11 needs only the blocks' conditions, at the pair's L ≥ L₀^×, ν ≤ 1/1000 and 1000ηA_s < ϖ/8, with one coupling threshold for every k at fixed q (#NV.11-2.1). So at fixed L, ν and η it gives the lim inf bound for every n, and the limit for the n that L, ν and η admit.
