# Bałaban Proof: PBG's Series (3.130) and (3.138) at Complex Configurations

PBG's series (3.130) for G and (3.138) for G₁, and the same series with an added perturbation without derivatives, converge at the complex configurations of BP_PBG_COMPLEX.md in the norms of Theorem 3.3, with walk expansions, and are analytic.

## Proof

- {#BG.4} *Lemma C (composition).* Let K₁, …, K_n have types (s_m, r, κ_m), σ_m := s₁ + ⋯ + s_m and σ* ≥ max_{m<n}|σ_n − σ_m|. Let 0 < s₀ < r with ¼s₀RM ≥ 2d log c₀ + 1 + σ* log L, c₀ := Σ_{z∈ℤ}e^{−½s₀|z|}. Then K₁⋯K_n has type (σ_n, r − s₀, (12L^{σ*}c₀^d)^{n−1}κ₁⋯κ_n), and the same holds for L²-types.
  {#BG.4-1} *Proof.* Insert Σ_{y_m}1_{Δ(y_m)} after K_m for m < n, with y₀ := y and y_n := y′. A sequence (y_m) contributes at most Π_mκ_m(L^{j_{m−1}}η)^{s_m}e^{−rd(y_{m−1},y_m)}|λ|, j_m the scale of y_m. Abel summation gives Π_m(L^{j_{m−1}}η)^{s_m} = (L^{j₀}η)^{σ_n}Π_{i=1}^{n−1}L^{(j_i−j_{i−1})(σ_n−σ_i)}. By the triangle inequality (#BQ.4-1), Σ_mrd(y_{m−1}, y_m) ≥ (r − s₀)d(y, y′) + s₀Σ_md(y_{m−1}, y_m).
  {#BG.4-2} Drop e^{−s₀d(y_{n−1},y′)} ≤ 1 and sum over y_{n−1}, then y_{n−2}, …, y₁. Each sum Σ_{y_i}L^{σ*|j_i−j_{i−1}|}e^{−s₀d(y_{i−1},y_i)} is at most 12L^{σ*}c₀^d by Lemma A (#BQ.5), with s₀ for r and p = σ*. For L²-types the same argument runs with ‖1_{Δ(y_m)}·‖ for |·|_{y_m}. ∎
- {#BG.5} *Lemma H (Hölder norms from derivatives).* Let y ∈ Λ_j, ζ ∈ C₀^∞(Δ̃(y)) and ξ := L^{−j}, and let |f| ≤ a and L^jη|∇f| ≤ a on the bonds within distance 2dL^jη of Δ̃(y), with ∇ at U or at 𝐔. Then ‖ζf‖_β ≤ 4d(L^jη)^{−β}(‖ζ‖^ξ_β + |ζ|)a, and ‖ζf‖^ξ_β ≤ 4d(‖ζ‖^ξ_β + |ζ|)a.
  {#BG.5-1} *Proof.* Distances on the ξ-lattice are those on the η-lattice times ξ/η = (L^jη)^{−1}, as ηA = ξ(L^jηA) (p.401 l.34). For j ≥ 1, the bonds within distance 3dL^jη of Δ(y) lie in Ω_{j−1} by CMP96 (2.2) (dist(Ω_{j−1}^c, Ω_j) > RML^{j−1}η) once RM ≥ 3dL, so |A′| < α₁L(L^jη)^{−1} there by (3.37); for j = 0 the fields live on Ω₀, where (3.37) gives |A′| < α₁η^{−1}. Hence the covariant derivatives at U and at 𝐔 differ there by |R(U′_b) − 1||f|/η ≤ 2c_GLα₁(L^jη)^{−1}|f| ≤ a/(L^jη) once 2c_GLα₁ ≤ 1, and L^jη|∇_Uf| ≤ 2a.
  {#BG.5-2} For |x − x′| ≤ L^jη, summing η(D_Uf) along Γ_{x,x′}, of length at most d|x − x′|, with R(U(·)) orthogonal, gives |R(U(Γ_{x,x′}))f(x′) − f(x)| ≤ 2d|x − x′|a/(L^jη). Also |ζ(x′) − ζ(x)| ≤ ‖ζ‖^ξ_β(|x − x′|/L^jη)^β. So the difference quotient of ζf is at most (L^jη)^{−β}(‖ζ‖^ξ_β + 2d|ζ|)a.
  {#BG.5-3} For |x − x′| > L^jη the quotient is at most 2|ζ|a(L^jη)^{−β}, as R(U(Γ)) is orthogonal. The ξ-norm takes only the pairs with |x − x′| ≤ L^jη, each with (L^jη)^β times the η-quotient. ∎
