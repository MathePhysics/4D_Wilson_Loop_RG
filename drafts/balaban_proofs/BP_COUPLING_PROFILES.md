# Bałaban Proof: The Profiles of the Coupling Function, of ζ₀, and of ζ₁

Bałaban proof: a choice of the smooth profiles that CRE (2.24), LFR I (1.100), and LFR II (1.70) leave unspecified, and the check that their gradients stay inside the right layers. Used by the LF note (WILSON_IT1_LARGE_FIELD_DEPTH_2026_10_27.md, #4.41-1–#4.41-1-1-1 and #4.109) for the constants c_{2.24} and c_ζ. Tags continue the LF's numbering; a cited #4.x not in this file is in the LF or in another file of this folder.

- {#4.41-1-2} *The profiles.* CRE prints only φ_j ∈ C₀^∞(Λ_j) with φ_j = 1 on Λ_j^{∼−1} (p.259 l.20–21), and LFR II only ζ₁'s layer (p.360 l.34–35). They are taken as mollified indicators, a choice within these printed conditions. Fix ψ ≥ 0 smooth, of integral 1, supported in the unit ball, and put ψ_r(z) := r^{−d}ψ(z/r).
  {#4.41-1-3} Let s^φ_j be the least side of the partition cubes of Λ_j: MR_ja_j for the step domains (CRE p.256 l.21–23, p.259 l.31), and Ma_j where Λ_j has cubes of the top N₀ layers of an R-operation (LFR I p.179 l.19–24). Put φ_j := 1_{S^φ_j} ∗ ψ_{s^φ_j/4}, S^φ_j the points of Λ_j at ℓ∞-distance ≥ s^φ_j/2 from Λ_j^c: φ_j = 1 on Λ_j^{∼−1}, whose points are at ℓ∞-distance ≥ s^φ_j from Λ_j^c, and abs(∇φ_j) ≤ 4‖∇ψ‖₁/s^φ_j, so c_{2.24} = 4‖∇ψ‖₁. ∇φ_j vanishes off the strip of the points of Λ_j at ℓ∞-distance between s^φ_j/4 and 3s^φ_j/4 from Λ_j^c.
    {#4.41-1-3-1} For j ≤ k − 1 the strip lies in the layer j once dist(Ω_{j+1}, Λ_j^c) ≥ s^φ_j (CRE (2.1): Ω_j ⊃ Λ_j ⊃ Ω_{j+1}), and there s^φ_j ≥ Ma_j ≥ a_{j(x)}; at j = k it lies in Λ_k ⊂ Ω_k. For the step domains dist(Ω_{j+1}, Λ_j^c) ≥ 8MR_{j+1}a_{j+1} = 8LMR_{j+1}a_j (#4.18-1) ≥ MR_ja_j, since R_j ≤ (L + 1)R_{j+1} (CRE (2.9), p.256 l.15) and the R_j are powers of L (CRE (2.5), p.255 l.36–39), which forces R_j ≤ LR_{j+1} (L ≥ 2).
    {#4.41-1-3-2} *After an R-operation.* CRE (2.3) sets Z_j = Λ_j^c (p.255 l.25), and an R-operation on Z replaces Z_j by LFR I's Z″_j for h + 1 ≤ j ≤ k, Z_j standing for Z_j ∩ Z (p.177 l.33–35). So Λ″_j = Ω″_j on Z for h + 1 ≤ j ≤ k and Λ″_j = Λ_j otherwise, the case CRE names ("Such a situation may arise as a result of an R-operation", p.255 l.2–3).
      {#4.41-1-3-2-1} On Z, dist(Ω″_{j+1}, (Ω″_j)^c) ≥ s^φ_j for h + 1 ≤ j ≤ k − 1: it is ≥ 3LMR_{j+1}a_j, and LMR_{j+1}a_j at j = k − N₀, both ≥ MR_ja_j (#4.18-1, R_j ≤ LR_{j+1}), and in the top N₀ layers one M-cube of the scale j + 1, LMa_j (#4.17-1). At j = h, Λ″_h = Λ_h, the steps h, …, k − 1 on Z are steps (#4.18-0–#4.18-0-1), and step h puts Ω_{h+1} at ≥ 8MR_{h+1}a_{h+1} from Λ_h^c (#4.18-1), so dist(Ω″_{h+1} ∩ Z, Λ_h^c ∩ Z) ≥ 3LMR_{h+1}a_h ≥ MR_ha_h. For j ≤ h − 1 the operation changes nothing (LFR I (1.12)).
      {#4.41-1-3-3} At ∂Z, for h ≤ j ≤ k − 1, both parts of Λ″_j^c = (Λ″_j^c ∩ Z) ∪ (Λ_j^c ∩ Z^c) lie in Ω_k^c (CRE (2.1); the operation only enlarges, #4.18-2-1), and Ω_k^c ∩ Z is ≥ 2MR_ka_k from Z^c and Ω_k^c ∩ Z^c from Z (#4.18-2-1-1), with 2MR_ka_k ≥ MR_ja_j ≥ s^φ_j (R_j ≤ L^{k−j}R_k). So the cross pairs are ≥ s^φ_j apart; the pairs within Z or Z^c are the above and, by induction over the times, the same claim at the earlier operations, whose regions keep their cubes and separations. So dist(Ω″_{j+1}, Λ″_j^c) ≥ s^φ_j, and at j = k the strip lies in Λ″_k ⊂ Ω″_k.
  {#4.41-1-4} Likewise ζ₁ := 1_{S′} ∗ ψ_{a_h/2}, S′ the points at distance ≥ a_h from (Z″_{h+1})^c, changes only at the distances between a_h/2 and 3a_h/2 from (Z″_{h+1})^c, inside the layer of the width 2a_h at the boundary, with abs(∇ζ₁) ≤ 2‖∇ψ‖₁/a_h, and a_{j(x)} ≤ a_h there, so c_ζ = 2‖∇ψ‖₁. Both are constants of d, uniform in j and h.
  {#4.41-1-5} LFR I prints only ζ₀ ∈ C₀^∞(Z), changing from 0 to 1 in a neighbourhood of ∂Z^{∼−2} (p.201 l.28–29, ζ_i for the component X_i = Z), and the LF takes ζ₀ = 1 on Z^{∼−2}, 0 ≤ ζ₀ ≤ 1 (#4.48-3). Put ζ₀ := 1_{S₀} ∗ ψ_{a_k/2}, S₀ the points at ℓ∞-distance ≤ a_k/2 from Z^{∼−2}. Then ζ₀ = 1 on Z^{∼−2}, ζ₀ = 0 at ℓ∞-distance > a_k from Z^{∼−2}, and abs(∇ζ₀) ≤ 2‖∇ψ‖₁/a_k = c_ζ/a_k, so a_{j(x)}abs(∇ζ₀(x)) ≤ c_ζ wherever a_{j(x)} ≤ a_k.
    {#4.41-1-5-1} Z^{∼−2} ⊂ Z^{∼−1}, the union of the MR_k-cubes of Z with no point in common with Z^c (BP_DERIVED_CONFIGS.md #BC.2-2-1, cubes of LFR I p.179 l.8 as in #4.48-3.0.0), lies at ℓ∞-distance ≥ MR_ka_k ≥ 2a_k from Z^c. So supp ζ₀ lies at ℓ∞-distance ≥ a_k from Z^c, ζ₀ ∈ C₀^∞(Z), and ζ₀ changes only in Z ∖ Z^{∼−2}.
