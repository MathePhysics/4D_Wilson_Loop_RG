# Bałaban Proof: The Profiles of the Coupling Function, of ζ₀, and of ζ₁

The profiles φ_j of CRE (2.24), ζ₀ of LFR I (1.100), and ζ₁ of LFR II (1.70) are taken as mollified indicators. Then a_{j(x)}|∇φ_j(x)| ≤ c_{2.24} := 4‖∇𝔧‖₁ and a_{j(x)}|∇ζ₀(x)|, a_{j(x)}|∇ζ₁(x)| ≤ c_ζ := 2‖∇𝔧‖₁ (a_j and j(x) as in #T2.3), constants of d only, and ∇φ_j vanishes off the layer j for j ≤ k − 1, also after R-operations.

- {#4.41-1-2} *The profiles.* CRE requires only φ_j ∈ C₀^∞(Λ_j) and φ_j = 1 on Λ_j^{∼−1} (p.259 l.20–21); LFR I only ζ₀ ∈ C₀^∞(Z) changing from 0 to 1 in a neighbourhood of ∂Z^{∼−2} (p.201 l.28–29, ζ_i for the component X_i = Z); LFR II only ζ₁ ∈ C₀^∞(Z″_{h+1}) changing from 0 to 1 "on a layer of the width 2 at the boundary, in L^{−h}-scale", of the width 2a_h (p.360 l.34–35). Fix 𝔧 ≥ 0 smooth, of integral 1, supported in the Euclidean unit ball, and put 𝔧_r(z) := r^{−d}𝔧(z/r) and ‖∇𝔧‖₁ := ∫|∇𝔧|.
  {#4.41-1-2-1} For F ⊂ ℝ^d and r > 0, f := 1_F ∗ 𝔧_r is smooth, 0 ≤ f ≤ 1, and |∇f| ≤ ‖∇𝔧_r‖₁ = ‖∇𝔧‖₁/r. As the Euclidean r-ball about x lies in the ℓ∞ one, f(x) = 1 if the ℓ∞ r-ball about x lies in F, and f(x) = 0 if it misses F. ∇f vanishes wherever f is 0 or 1, its extreme values. The ℓ∞-distance to a set is 1-Lipschitz for the ℓ∞-norm, which locates these balls below.
- {#4.41-1-3} *The coupling profiles.* Let s^φ_j be the least side of the partition cubes of Λ_j: MR_ja_j for the step domains (CRE p.256 l.21–23, p.259 l.31), and Ma_j where Λ_j has cubes of the top N₀ layers of an R-operation (LFR I p.179 l.19–24). Let S^φ_j be the set of the points at ℓ∞-distance ≥ s^φ_j/2 from Λ_j^c, and φ_j := 1_{S^φ_j} ∗ 𝔧_{s^φ_j/4}. By #4.41-1-2-1, φ_j = 0 at ℓ∞-distance < s^φ_j/4 from Λ_j^c, so φ_j ∈ C₀^∞(Λ_j), and φ_j = 1 at ℓ∞-distance ≥ 3s^φ_j/4.
  {#4.41-1-3-0} This includes Λ_j^{∼−1}: a point of Λ_j at ℓ∞-distance < s^φ_j from Λ_j^c lies in a cube touching Λ_j^c, the partitions being compatible with sides ≥ s^φ_j. Further |∇φ_j| ≤ 4‖∇𝔧‖₁/s^φ_j, and ∇φ_j vanishes off the strip of the points of Λ_j at ℓ∞-distance between s^φ_j/4 and 3s^φ_j/4 from Λ_j^c.
    {#4.41-1-3-1} For j ≤ k − 1 the strip lies in the layer j, Ω_j ∖ Ω_{j+1}, if dist(Ω_{j+1}, Λ_j^c) ≥ s^φ_j, since Λ_j ⊂ Ω_j (CRE (2.1)); at j = k it lies in Λ_k ⊂ Ω_k. In both cases a_{j(x)} = a_j ≤ s^φ_j/M on the strip, so a_{j(x)}|∇φ_j(x)| ≤ c_{2.24}. The bound dist(Ω_{j+1}, Λ_j^c) ≥ s^φ_j holds for every sequence of domains of the expansion, by induction over the times, with R_j ≤ LR_{j+1}: R_j ≤ (L + 1)R_{j+1} (CRE (2.9), p.256 l.15), the R_j are powers of L (CRE (2.5), p.255 l.36–39), and L + 1 < L² for L ≥ 2.
      {#4.41-1-3-1-1} So MR_ja_j ≤ MR_{j+1}a_{j+1} and R_j ≤ L^{k−j}R_k. A renormalisation step at the time j adds Ω_{j+1} and Λ_{j+1} only (CRE (3.5), (3.20)) and puts Ω_{j+1} at ≥ 8MR_{j+1}a_{j+1} ≥ s^φ_j from Λ_j^c (BP_LAYER_WIDTHS.md #4.18-1). An R-operation is treated in #4.41-1-3-2–#4.41-1-3-3-1.
    {#4.41-1-3-2} *An R-operation on Z at the time k, h = k − N.* LFR I replaces Z_j by Z″_j for h + 1 ≤ j ≤ k ((1.10)–(1.11), p.179; Z_j stands for Z_j ∩ Z, p.177 l.33–35), keeps Z_j otherwise, and puts Ω″_j = (Z″_j)^c on Z for these j and Ω″_j = Ω_j otherwise ((1.12)). As Z_j = Λ_j^c (CRE (2.3), p.255 l.25), Λ″_j = Ω″_j on Z for h + 1 ≤ j ≤ k and Λ″_j = Λ_j otherwise, the case CRE admits ("Such a situation may arise as a result of an R-operation", p.255 l.2–3).
      {#4.41-1-3-2-1} *Pairs within Z.* For h + 1 ≤ j ≤ k − 1, on Z, dist(Ω″_{j+1}, (Ω″_j)^c) is ≥ 3LMR_{j+1}a_j for j ≤ k − N₀ − 1 and ≥ LMR_{j+1}a_j at j = k − N₀ (BP_LAYER_WIDTHS.md #4.18-1-0), and ≥ LMa_j above (BP_LAYER_WIDTHS.md #4.17-1); each is ≥ s^φ_j. At j = h, Λ″_h = Λ_h, Ω″_{h+1} ∩ Z = Ω^{∼5}_{h+1} ∩ Z, and Ω_{h+1} is ≥ 8MR_{h+1}a_{h+1} from Λ_h^c (BP_LAYER_WIDTHS.md #4.18-1), so dist(Ω″_{h+1} ∩ Z, Λ_h^c ∩ Z) ≥ 3LMR_{h+1}a_h ≥ s^φ_h. For j ≤ h − 1 nothing changes ((1.12)).
      {#4.41-1-3-3} *Pairs across ∂Z.* For h ≤ j ≤ k − 1, Λ″_j ⊇ Ω″_{j+1} ⊇ Ω″_k ⊇ Ω_k (#4.41-1-3-2–#4.41-1-3-2-1; the operation only enlarges, BP_LAYER_WIDTHS.md #4.18-2-1), so Λ″_j^c ⊂ Ω_k^c. A point of Ω_k^c ∩ Z^c is ≥ 2MR_ka_k from Z (BP_LAYER_WIDTHS.md #4.18-2-1-1), a point of Ω_k^c ∩ Z is ≥ 2MR_ka_k from Z^c (BP_LAYER_WIDTHS.md #4.18-2-1-2), and 2MR_ka_k ≥ s^φ_j, by R_j ≤ L^{k−j}R_k. So Ω″_{j+1} ∩ Z is ≥ s^φ_j from Λ″_j^c ∩ Z^c, and Ω″_{j+1} ∩ Z^c from Λ″_j^c ∩ Z.
        {#4.41-1-3-3-1} The pairs within Z^c are pairs of the sequence before the operation, for which the bound holds by the induction. s^φ_j does not increase: off Z and for j ≤ h the cubes are kept, and on Z, for j ≥ h + 1, step cubes of the side MR_ja_j (BP_LAYER_WIDTHS.md #4.18-0-1) give way to cubes of the side MR_ja_j or Ma_j. So dist(Ω″_{j+1}, Λ″_j^c) ≥ s^φ_j for j ≤ k − 1, and at j = k the strip lies in Λ″_k ⊂ Ω″_k.
- {#4.41-1-4} *ζ₁.* Let S^{ζ₁} be the set of the points at ℓ∞-distance ≥ a_h from (Z″_{h+1})^c, and ζ₁ := 1_{S^{ζ₁}} ∗ 𝔧_{a_h/2}. By #4.41-1-2-1, ζ₁ = 0 at ℓ∞-distance < a_h/2 from (Z″_{h+1})^c, so ζ₁ ∈ C₀^∞(Z″_{h+1}), and ζ₁ = 1 at ℓ∞-distance ≥ 3a_h/2, so ζ₁ changes from 0 to 1 on the layer of the width 2a_h at ∂Z″_{h+1}, with |∇ζ₁| ≤ 2‖∇𝔧‖₁/a_h. On Z, Ω″_{h+1} = (Z″_{h+1})^c (LFR I (1.12)), so a_{j(x)} ≤ a_h on Z″_{h+1} and a_{j(x)}|∇ζ₁(x)| ≤ c_ζ.
- {#4.41-1-5} *ζ₀.* Let S^{ζ₀} be the set of the points at ℓ∞-distance ≤ a_k/2 from Z^{∼−2}, and ζ₀ := 1_{S^{ζ₀}} ∗ 𝔧_{a_k/2}. By #4.41-1-2-1, ζ₀ = 1 on Z^{∼−2}, ζ₀ = 0 at ℓ∞-distance > a_k from Z^{∼−2}, 0 ≤ ζ₀ ≤ 1, and |∇ζ₀| ≤ 2‖∇𝔧‖₁/a_k, so a_{j(x)}|∇ζ₀(x)| ≤ c_ζ, since a_{j(x)} ≤ a_k.
    {#4.41-1-5-1} Z is a union of MR_ka_k-cubes, since Λ_k is (CRE p.256 l.21–23). Z^{∼−2} ⊂ Z^{∼−1} = ((Z^c)^∼)^c (CRE p.247 l.49), the union of the cubes of Z touching no cube of Z^c (CRE p.245 l.41–43); as Z and Z^c are unions of cubes of one partition, it lies at ℓ∞-distance ≥ MR_ka_k ≥ 2a_k from Z^c. So supp ζ₀ lies at ℓ∞-distance ≥ a_k from Z^c, ζ₀ ∈ C₀^∞(Z), and ζ₀ changes from 0 to 1 in Z ∖ Z^{∼−2}, within a_k of ∂Z^{∼−2}.
