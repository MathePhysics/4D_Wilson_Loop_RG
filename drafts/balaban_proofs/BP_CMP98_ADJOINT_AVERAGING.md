W is a real configuration on T_η, with κ_W, W̄, and Q₁^{pl}(W) as in #C10.3.17, and ν is a bond field on T_{Lη}.

{#C10.3.21} With ⟨A, B⟩ = η^dΣ_b and ⟨ν, μ⟩ = (Lη)^dΣ_c, the linearisation Ā_c = L^{−(d+1)}Σ_{x∈B(c₋)}A(γ̄_{c,x}) of (0.4) at W = 1 (#C9.8.1) gives (Q₁^{pl}(1)*ν)(b) = L^{−1}Σ±|𝐆(c₋, x)|^{−2}ν(c), summed over the (c, x, Γ, Γ′) and the occurrences of b in γ_{c,x}^{Γ,Γ′}, with the orientation signs (b may occur twice, with opposite signs, when μ is the last direction of the contour: on Γ and on [x, x′] when n_μ < 0, and on −Γ′ and on [x, x′] when n_μ > 0).
{#C10.3.21-1} For real W and the variations e^{iηA}W of #C10.3.3's convention 𝐔 = e^{iεA}U₀, d(e^{iY_c}W(c))(e^{iY_c}W(c))^{−1} = dexp_{Y_c}(i dY_c) + Ad(e^{iY_c})(dW(c)W(c)^{−1}). {#C10.3.21-2} Each occurrence in γ of a bond b = ⟨b₋, b₊⟩ contributes ±Ad(W(γ from c₋ to b₋))iηA(b) to dW(γ)W(γ)^{−1}: + when γ traverses b along its orientation, the transport then excluding b, and − against it, the transport then including b. For b on Γ this is P_{b,Γ} := Ad(W(Γ from c₋ to b₋)), the adjoint action of the transport along Γ from c₋ to b₋, and for b on [x, x′] it is isometric.
{#C10.3.21-3} For b on −Γ′ it is Ad(h)Ad(W(c))P_{b,Γ′}, with P_{b,Γ′} := Ad(W(Γ′ from c₊ to b₋)): the part of γ after this occurrence's visit to b₋ is the reverse of Γ′ from c₊ to b₋, so W(γ from c₋ to b₋) = W(γ)W(Γ′ from c₊ to b₋) = hW(c)W(Γ′ from c₊ to b₋).
{#C10.3.21-4} The coefficient of dW(c)W(c)^{−1}, from the outer factor of (0.4) and from the factors W(c)^{−1} in the h's, is Ad(e^{iY_c}) − dexp_{Y_c}∘L^{−d}Σ_x|𝐆(c₋, x)|^{−2}Σ_{Γ,Γ′}dlog_h∘Ad(h). It vanishes at W = 1, the weights summing to 1 (|B(c₋)| = L^d, #C10.1.4-0-1).
{#C10.3.21-5} It acts on each fine bond b of c through dW(c)W(c)^{−1} = Σ_{b⊂c}T′_b iηA(b), with the isometry T′_b := Ad(W(c from c₋ to b₋)), and with weight L^{d−1}: L^d from the pairings, and L^{−1} per fine bond of c in field units.
{#C10.3.21-6} Each loop γ_{c,x}^{Γ,Γ′} ∪ (−c) is contracted to the trivial loop by at most A_L := Σ_{α<β}|n_α||n_β| + L(|n|₁ − |n_μ|) plaquette moves, n the displacement of x from c₋ (the D2 step map WILSON_IT1_D2_STEP_MAP_2026_10_22.md, #MOSTC.8–#MOSTC.11). By |n_ν| ≤ L/2 (#C10.1.4-0-1) and Σ_{α<β}|n_α||n_β| ≤ (d − 1)|n|₁²/(2d), A_L ≤ (d − 1)dL²/8 + (d − 1)L²/2 = (d − 1)(d + 4)L²/8, which is at most dL² for d ≤ 5.
{#C10.3.21-7} Each move multiplies the holonomy by a conjugated plaquette variable (#MOSTC.12), and |∏g_i − 1| ≤ Σ|g_i − 1| for unitary g_i, so |h_{c,x}^{Γ,Γ′} − 1| ≤ dL²κ_W. So |Y_c| ≤ 2dL²κ_W, Y_c being a convex combination of logarithms with |log h| ≤ 2|h − 1| for |h − 1| ≤ ½, since dL²κ_W ≤ 1/32 under the condition of (ii) with {#D.c_av} c_{av} := max(4d, 1/(8ρ_𝔤)).
  {#C10.3.21-7-1} Here {#D.ρ_𝔤} ρ_𝔤 := sup{r ≤ ½ : every g ∈ G with |g − 1| < r is e^{iP} with P ∈ 𝔤 and |P| ≤ 1}, a positive constant of G, and every g ∈ G with |g − 1| < ρ_𝔤 is such an e^{iP}, so iP is its principal logarithm and |P| ≤ 2|g − 1| (exp is a local diffeomorphism at 0, and G carries the topology of U(N)); κ_W ≤ ρ_𝔤/L² < ρ_𝔤 by c_{av} ≥ 1/(8ρ_𝔤).   {#C10.3.21-7-2} Along this path the set where the principal logarithm lies in i𝔤 is closed, and open: near e^{iX}, X ∈ 𝔤, |X| ≤ 1/8, the elements of G are e^{iX′} with X′ ∈ 𝔤 near X (inverse function theorem, |ad X| < 2π), whose principal logarithm is iX′.
