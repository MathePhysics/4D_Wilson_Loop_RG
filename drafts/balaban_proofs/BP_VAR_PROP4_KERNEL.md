# Bałaban Proof: The Kernel Form of VAR Proposition 4

VAR's Proposition 4 bounds 𝒩 := δV/δA′ uniformly ((97)–(98)). Its proof, redone with one factor of the field kept at its point, gives LF #4.33: for complex A with |A|_∞ ≤ ε₃ ≤ ¼a₃ and every bond b of Δ(y), a_{j(y)}³|𝒩(A)(b)| ≤ C_Nε₃Σ_{y″}e^{−⅛δ₀d(y,y″)}‖A‖_{y″}. VAR's A′ is written A, and VAR's A = A′ − HD(A′) is written A^♯. Δ(y), ‖A‖_y, and |A|_∞ are those of LF #4.30-1; a_j = L^jη (#T2.3); c_tot(α) is the count of #T4.23. C₂, C₃, and c₄ are the constants of CMP98 Propositions 4–5, VAR's [4], which VAR uses in (44), (69), and (72).

- {#4.34} (i) *The vertices.* Each vertex reads the field on the bonds of the two blocks of a block bond or on the boundary of a plaquette; its local size is the maximum of a_j|X(b)|, and of a_j²|∇X(b)| for the vertices with differences, over these bonds, j the layer of the vertex. For a block bond c of the layer j, s_c(X) := max a_j|X(b)| over the bonds b ⊂ B^j(c₋) ∪ B^j(c₊). By CMP98 Proposition 4 (p.38 l.48–p.39 l.10), C_j(a_jX, c) is an analytic function of these X(b) for s_c(X) < c₄, with |C_j(a_jX, c)| ≤ C₂s_c(X)² ((135), VAR (44)) and C_j = C_j^{(2)} + C_j^{(3)} + ⋯ ((136)).
  {#4.34-0} {#D.𝒱(X)} 𝒱(X)(c, b) := a_j(δC_j/δA)(a_jX)(c, b) is analytic for s_c(X) < c₄, as a derivative of C_j, and vanishes unless b ⊂ B^j(c₋) ∪ B^j(c₊). By CMP98 Proposition 5 ((157), p.42), rescaled from the L^{−j}-scale as in VAR (72) and p.289 l.21, |𝒱(X)(c, b)| ≤ C₃a_j^{1−d}s_c(X) for s_c(X) < {#D.r_v} r_v, the radius ≤ c₄ for which Proposition 5 holds. VAR evaluates these vertices at s_c ≤ 2ε₃ < r_v (VAR (57), (72)).
  {#4.34-1} Let 𝒱^{(1)} be the linear part of 𝒱 and {#D.C_v} C_v := max(C₂, C₃, 2C₃/r_v). Then |C_j^{(2)}(a_jX, c)| ≤ C₂s_c(X)² and |𝒱^{(1)}(X)(c, b)| ≤ C₃a_j^{1−d}s_c(X) for every X, and |(𝒱 − 𝒱^{(1)})(X)(c, b)| ≤ C_va_j^{1−d}s_c(X)² for s_c(X) < r_v. The first two follow from Cauchy's formula on |τ| = R for τ ↦ C_j(τa_jX, c), R < c₄/s_c(X), and for τ ↦ 𝒱(τX), R < r_v/s_c(X): |C_j^{(2)}(a_jX, c)| ≤ R^{−2}C₂(Rs_c(X))² and |𝒱^{(1)}(X)(c, b)| ≤ R^{−1}C₃a_j^{1−d}Rs_c(X).
    {#4.34-1-1} For the third, g(τ) := 𝒱(τX) − τ𝒱^{(1)}(X) vanishes to the order 2 by (136), and |g(τ)| ≤ 2C₃a_j^{1−d}Rs_c(X) on |τ| = R < r_v/s_c(X). For R ≥ 1 the maximum principle for g(τ)/τ² gives |g(1)| ≤ 2C₃a_j^{1−d}s_c(X)/R, and R ↑ r_v/s_c(X) gives |g(1)| ≤ (2C₃/r_v)a_j^{1−d}s_c(X)².
  {#4.34-2} The plaquette vertices (∂/∂A(b))V₀′(·, ∂p) of (90) obey the bound (40) with the power of |A|(∂p) lowered by 1 (VAR p.291 l.58–59), of the order ≥ 2; the commutator vertices of (91)–(96) are of the order 2 in the field and its differences on ∂p. So one factor is the local size and the others are ≤ 2ε₃ in the units a_j.
  {#4.34-3} A local size at c is ≤ L·max‖X‖_{y′} over the blocks y′ meeting the bonds of the vertex for the vertices of the field alone, and ≤ L²·max‖X‖_{y′} for the vertices of (91)–(96) with differences, since adjacent layers differ by the factor L in a_j and L² in a_j².

  {#4.35-2} Let 𝔇^{(1)}(A) be the derivative of D^{(2)}(A) = C_j^{(2)}(a_jA) on Λ_j (VAR p.286 l.35–37), which is 𝒱^{(1)}(A), and {#D.𝔇₂} 𝔇₂ := 𝔇 − 𝔇^{(1)}, VAR's 𝔇₂ of p.289 l.37–39.     {#4.35-2-1} For b ∈ Δ(y), |𝔇₂(A; c, b)| ≤ C_𝔇ε₃a_j^{1−d}e^{−¼δ₀d(c₋,y)}Σ_{y″}e^{−¼δ₀d(y,y″)}‖A‖_{y″}, {#D.C_𝔇} C_𝔇 a constant of d, L, and Bałaban's constants.     {#4.35-2-2} Each local size at c′ is ≤ L·max_{y′}‖·‖_{y′} (#4.34-3), which #4.35-1-1 bounds by (1 + C_{HD}ε₃)Σ_{y″}e^{−¼δ₀d(y′,y″)}‖A‖_{y″} for A and A^♯, and by C_{HD}ε₃Σ_{y″}e^{−¼δ₀d(y′,y″)}‖A‖_{y″} for HD(A).
