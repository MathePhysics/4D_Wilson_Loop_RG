# Bałaban Proof: The Replacement of U_{k,Z} by U_k on Λ

Bałaban proof: a rederivation of the bound behind LFR II p.365 l.35–36's "transformed in the same way as above, i.e., with the configuration U_{k,Z} replaced by U_k, and with some new small terms". The way above is (1.36)'s representation around U_k, inverse to (1.16), with the expansion of the type (1.17) (p.365 l.20–31); Bałaban prints no bound on the new terms, which enter the O(1) of (1.37) (l.43–51).

  {#160.8-1} Transform U_k^0(M(U)) by G^{−1} on the block points of Λ and by 1 elsewhere.
