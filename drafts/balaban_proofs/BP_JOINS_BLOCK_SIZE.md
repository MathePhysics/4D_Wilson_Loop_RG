# Bałaban Proof: PBG's and VAR's Theorems on the Joins at the Block Size M′

PBG Theorems 3.1–3.3, PBG's expansions, and VAR Theorem 1 hold on CRE's sequences 𝐁, on 𝐁″_k, and on the joins within their printed hypotheses at the block size M′, with M₁ = K_{M′}M′ and R′ = LK_{M′} − 1, and their constants are constants of d and L; (J-W) holds for M₁ ≥ 2M_J(d, L). ## The Rate and the Layer Geometry

- {#4.19} *(J-W).* The condition (J-W) of BP_LAYER_WIDTHS #4.18-2 holds for M₁ ≥ 2M_J(d, L) (#4.29).
  - {#4.20} *The rate.* Three rates enter the counts: PRT2's local rate δ₀^{(2.43)}, through Lemma 2.1; the rate δ₀^{PBG} of PBG Theorem 3.1; and PBG's common rate δ₀ of G, G₁, H, and H₁ (p.423 l.38–40), at which the kernels of the large-field note's profile counts decay (BP_VAR_PROP4_KERNEL #4.35-1). All three depend on d and L only (PBG p.397 l.37–38, p.423 l.39; #4.28). The counts are taken at δ_* := min(δ₀^{PBG}, δ₀^{(2.43)}, δ₀). The left side of (2.59) increases with the rate, and c₀(α) = Σ_{z∈ℤ}e^{−αδ|z|} at the rate δ (PRT2 p.233 l.37) decreases with it, so the conditions at δ_* give those at each of the three rates.
  - {#4.21} *Theorem 3.1 at the joins.* The constants of PBG's theorems "do not depend on the sequence {Ω_j} … if the conditions (2.1), (2.2) are satisfied" (PBG p.399 l.2). In PRT2's counts (2.2) enters through the length > RM of each crossing in (2.57) (p.233 l.21), in Lemma 2.1 (p.234 l.2–6) and in the factors e^{−cδ₀RM·max(|j−j′|−1, 0)} of p.235 l.9 and p.237 l.39.
    {#4.21-0} The localisations also use the layer geometry. PRT2's cubes of the size 2ML^jη meet at most B^{j+1}(Λ_{j+1}) besides B^j(Λ_j) (p.230 l.1–2, p.235 l.35, p.238, the last four lines, p.239 l.40). PBG's cubes are □̃ⁿ, of the side (2 + 2n)ML^jη (p.408 l.23–24): □̃³ in its dichotomy, Ω_j(□) = □̃⁴, and Ω₀(□) ⊂ □̃⁵ in its localisation, and □̃⁸ in Sect. C (p.415 l.9–10).
    {#4.21-0-1} Ω₀(□) ⊂ □̃⁵ since dist(Ω₀(□)^c, □̃⁴) < 2R₀M₀L^jη/(L − 1) (p.408 l.30–32) ≤ ML^jη for M ≥ LR₀M₀ (#4.22). PBG's Ω₀ = {big blocks at distance ≤ RM from Ω₁} (p.396 l.17–18; p.394 l.32 also allows a thinner layer) is taken at R′M′ at the joins.
    {#4.21-1} PBG's dichotomy (p.408 l.29–30) has □̃³ contained in B^j(Λ_j) or meeting B^{j+1}(Λ_{j+1}), and leaves out the cubes □̃³ that also meet B^{j−1}(Λ_{j−1}). They occur on PBG's own sequences, for which its theorems are stated (p.399 l.2), so domains meeting the layers j − 1, j, and j + 1 are within them. A cube of 𝒟_j has a big block in B^j(Λ_j) (p.408 l.21), so for □ touching ∂Ω_j from inside, □̃⁵, of the side 12ML^jη, reaches 5ML^jη beyond ∂Ω_j. A layer Λ_{j−1} that is LM wide in its own units is ML^jη wide, and then □̃⁵ meets Λ_{j−2}, which PBG's sequences, with R large, exclude.
    {#4.21-2} CRE's maximal sequences 𝐁 have such layers at M = M₁: they are LM₁ wide in their own units (p.256 l.58–61), M₁ being CRE's size of the cubes on which the earlier theorems hold (p.245 l.36–37). So the theorems of PBG and VAR are applied on 𝐁, 𝐁″_k, and the joins at a smaller block size M′, at which no localisation cube meets Λ_{j−2} (#4.25).

## The Block Size

- {#4.22} *The block size M′.* Let {#D.M′} M′ := a power of L with M′ ≥ max(M₁^{PBG}, LR₀M₀, R₁M₁^{VAR}). M₁^{PBG} is the threshold of PBG Theorems 3.1–3.3 (p.397 l.38), R₀M₀ that of PBG's local sequences (p.408 l.26–28), and R₁M₁^{VAR} VAR's product, with which the results of PRT2, PBG, and REG hold (VAR p.277 l.17, p.309; #T2.7). R₀M₀ and R₁M₁^{VAR} are powers of L (PBG p.408 l.26; Cauchy #C9.1-2), so M′ is a multiple of each, as the divisibility conditions require (#4.24-1-1). VAR's own M′ is written M′_V (#T2.13).
  {#4.22-0} Take {#D.K_M′} K_{M′} := a power of L with K_{M′} ≥ 12d + 1, so that CRE's {#D.M₁} M₁ := K_{M′}M′ (#T2.15, #T2.9) is a power of L, as PBG's big-block lattice is T^{(j+m)} for M = L^m (p.408 l.20). Then R′ = LK_{M′} − 1 ≥ 12dL (#T2.21), since L(12d + 1) − 1 ≥ 12dL.
  {#4.22-1} Every M₁-cube of the scale j is a union of K_{M′}^d M′-cubes, so the domains of 𝐁 and of 𝐁″_k, unions of MR_j-cubes with M′ dividing M₁ and M, are unions of M′-blocks, as PRT2 (2.1)–(2.2) require.
    {#4.22-1-1} R′ is not a power of L. The joins use PRT2's conditions (2.1)–(2.4), whose R is "a big positive integer which will be fixed later" (PRT2 (2.2), p.224 l.11–12). PBG takes R and M as powers of L where it fixes R₀ and M₀ for its local sequences (p.408 l.26–28), whose M = KR₀M₀ has K any positive integer (l.28), and its theorems require (2.1), (2.2) (p.399 l.2).
  {#4.22-2} *R′ against PBG's R.* PBG requires R and M sufficiently large (p.408 l.26; p.426 l.38–39 for its localisation property). Let {#D.R_thr(d,L)} R_thr(d, L) := the largest threshold on R alone in PRT2, PBG, and VAR at the block size M′; it is a constant of d and L, since M′ = M′(d, L) and their constants depend on d and L only (PBG p.397 l.37–38).
    {#4.22-2-1} Then R′ = LK_{M′} − 1 ≥ L(K_{M′} − 1) ≥ R_thr(d, L) for K_{M′} ≥ R_thr(d, L)/L + 1, a further lower bound on K_{M′} (table #T2.15).
- {#4.22.1} *K_{M′} and M′ for the passage at a real base.* The passage draft's §9 (WILSON_IT1_PASSAGE_LAYERED_DRAFT_2026_11_04.md, *Result of (ii)*) runs PRT2's layering at R = R′ and M = M′ (#4.24), with VAR's product at the cube parameter M′ (#4.23). Its condition RM ≥ C(d)R₁M₁L (from *The cube Q_z*, *The block cubes*, the balls of Lemma R and of *The comparison near z*, and the distance of V from the rise of ñ) reads (LK_{M′} − 1)M′ ≥ C(d)LM′, which holds for K_{M′} ≥ C(d) + 1, since LK_{M′} − 1 ≥ L(K_{M′} − 1).
  {#4.22.1-1} Its §2 conditions M ≥ L and RM ≥ L² hold for M′ ≥ L, since then R′M′ ≥ L(K_{M′} − 1)L ≥ L².
  {#4.22.2} The large-field M enters there through the base's regularity: the base's data parameters carry M⁶, through M₀ = (1 + κ_s)O(1)B₅M⁶ (#3.96) and the data parameter ε_d = O(1)B₃²B₅M⁶ε_k (#4.284.12). M is fixed after K_{M′} and M′, since its lower bound M ≥ 2(d + 1)L²M₁ (#4.445.6, #4.449) involves M₁ = K_{M′}M′; the order of choice is #T1.5's.
- {#4.23} *The product R₁M₁ = M′.* VAR reads R₁ alone only in R ≥ R₁ (p.277 l.17), and otherwise only the product R₁M₁: in the cube class of Theorem 1 (p.279 l.2–4), the Sect. F sequences (144) and cubes □̃ (p.300 l.31–36), (163), (165)–(166), and M(ε₁). VAR's block size M₁^{VAR} (p.277 l.12) is kept, and VAR's R₁ is raised from its printed value to M′/M₁^{VAR}, a power of L (Cauchy #C9.1-2), so that R₁M₁ = M′ (#T2.7-1). Every printed condition on R₁ is a lower bound or one on R/R₁ (#4.24-1–#4.24-2).
  {#4.23-0} VAR applies the results of [3, 5, 6] (PRT2, PBG, REG; VAR p.277, p.309) on its local sequences at the block size M₁^{VAR}, whose layers are R₁ blocks wide, and those results need only lower bounds on R (#4.24-1), so they hold at the raised R₁. A sequence of unions of M′-blocks whose layers are R′M′ wide is a sequence of unions of M₁^{VAR}-blocks whose layers are R′M′/M₁^{VAR} = R′R₁ blocks wide, so VAR (1) holds at R := R′R₁ ≥ R₁ (p.277 l.17).

## The Printed Conditions

- {#4.24} *The separation.* CRE's layers satisfy dist(Ω_n, Ω_{n−1}^c) ≥ L^nξM₁ = (LK_{M′})M′L^{n−1}ξ (p.256 l.61), and every layer of 𝐁″_k and of the joins below the top one is ≥ LM₁ wide in its own units (#4.17, #4.18-2-2). So on all of them PRT2 (2.2) holds at the size M′ with {#D.R′} R′ := LK_{M′} − 1, as LM₁ > R′M′, and VAR (1) holds with R′R₁ blocks of M₁^{VAR} (#4.23-0). Every layer below the top one is ≥ R′M′ wide, and ½LM₁ ≤ R′M′ < LM₁ since LK_{M′} ≥ 2.
  {#4.24-1} The printed conditions on R, M, R₁, and M₁ are lower bounds, divisibility conditions (#4.24-1-1), ratios (#4.24-2), and upper bounds tied to M (#4.24-2-1). The lower bounds are on the products RM and R₁M₁, among them VAR's (163), B₃e^{−½δ₀R₁M₁} ≤ ½ (p.304 l.2–4), met as R′M′ ≥ ½LM₁ and R₁M₁ = M′ ≥ R₁M₁^{VAR}; and separate lower bounds, R ≥ R₁ (VAR p.277 l.17), PBG's on R₀ and M₀, unchanged here (#4.22-1-1), and any threshold on R alone, met by K_{M′} ≥ R_thr(d, L)/L + 1 (#4.22-2). Otherwise R enters PRT2 through RM, in (2.59) and (2.83), and through the layer geometry.
    {#4.24-1-1} *Divisibility.* M = KR₀M₀ (PBG p.408 l.28), and VAR's cube parameters are multiples of R₁M₁ (VAR p.279 l.2, p.300 l.28–29; REG p.098 l.8). Both hold: M′ is a multiple of R₀M₀ (#4.22), and VAR's cube parameters M′, 2M′, and 2LM′ (BP_VAR_COVERING.md) are multiples of R₁M₁ = M′ (#4.23).
  {#4.24-2} *Ratios.* REG's example "L^{−1}RM > 2dR₁M₁", which gives □̃ ⊂ Ω_{k−1} (REG p.098 l.18), reads R′ > 2dL at R = R′ and M = R₁M₁ = M′, true as R′ ≥ 12dL (#4.22-0). VAR states □̃ ⊂ Ω_{j−1} for its Sect. F cubes without a printed ratio (p.300 l.36). For its cubes of the side 2R₁M₁a_j, □̃ reaches at most d(2R₁M₁ + 2R₁M₁)a_j = 4dR₁M₁a_j in ℓ¹ beyond Ω_j, and the layer j − 1 is RM₁a_{j−1} wide by VAR (1), so R > 4dLR₁ suffices; at R = R′R₁ (#4.23-0) this reads R′ > 4dL, true as R′ ≥ 12dL. The reach of PBG's cubes is #4.25.
    {#4.24-2-1} The conditions tied to M from above are smallness conditions on α₀ and ε (PBG p.397 l.38, p.408 l.4; VAR p.304 l.21–24; REG p.098 l.34–36) and VAR's M ≤ M(ε₁) (p.279 l.17), which hold for γ small.
- {#4.25} *The reach.* PBG's cubes at the size M′ are □̃ⁿ of the side (2 + 2n)M′L^jη, so an enlargement by m adds 2mM′L^jη to the side, and □₀ = □̃². In Sect. C, PBG takes G′_{□̃₀²} with the boundary conditions outside □̃₀⁵, which it identifies in the original cubes with the outside of □̃⁸, or of □̃₁⁸ with □₁ ∈ 𝒟_{j+1} (p.415 l.9–10, with p.411 l.22–25 and p.413 l.6–10); indeed □̃₀⁵ = □̃⁷ ⊂ □̃⁸.
  {#4.25-0} Theorems 3.9 and 3.10 state that their terms depend on U restricted to the enlargements X̃⁵ of their localisation domains (p.413 l.19–20, p.416 l.7–8). The largest domain has 5^d cubes of 𝒟 (p.413 l.6), the side 10M′L^jη of X = □̃⁴ = □̃₀² (p.415 l.9), whose X̃⁵ has the side 20M′L^jη, that of □̃⁹. So □̃⁹ bounds both.
  {#4.25-1} Every other cube of PBG is □̃ⁿ with n ≤ 5 (#4.21-0), and PRT2's are at most □̃³ (PRT2 p.238, the last four lines). The centre of □ ∈ 𝒟_j is a corner of a big block in Ω_j, so □̃⁹ reaches at most 10M′L^jη beyond ∂Ω_j, an ℓ¹ reach of at most 10dM′L^jη. The layer Λ_{j−1}, ≥ LM₁ wide in its own units (#4.17), is ≥ M₁L^jη = K_{M′}M′L^jη wide in PRT2's ℓ¹ distance (#4.10), and K_{M′} ≥ 10d + 1. For □₁ ∈ 𝒟_{j+1} the same holds in the units L^{j+1}η against the layer Λ_j.
  {#4.25-2} The geometric two-layer conditions, PBG's class cubes in B^j(Λ_j) ∪ B^{j+1}(Λ_{j+1}) (the class of (3.35), p.396 l.15–16, which contains □̃⁵ by p.409 l.2) and PRT2's torus □̃³ carrying two scales (p.238, the last two lines, and p.239 l.40), are covered by the same count. Inwards they hold with a large margin, since Λ_{j+1} is ≥ L²K_{M′}M′ wide in the units L^jη. So every localisation domain of PBG meets at most the layers j − 1, j, and j + 1, as on PBG's own sequences (#4.21-1).
- {#4.26} *The constants.* PBG's constants are then those at M′, which depend on d and L only (p.397 l.37–38). The regularity (3.35) at the cube size M′, |A| < O(1)M′α₀(L^jη)^{−1} and the like (p.396 l.25), scales with the size, so it does not follow from (3.35) at M₁. It follows at the same α₀ from the plaquette regularity in an axial gauge: REG's argument on p.098 gives (3.35) on every cube in Ω_j and not in Ω_{j+1} that is a union of R₁M₁-cubes, under L^{−1}RM > 2dR₁M₁, which holds here (#4.24-2).
  {#4.26-1} That argument derives (1.128) from REG's hypotheses (1.7)–(1.9) by Proposition 2 of [3] (p.098 l.22–26), then the axial-gauge bound (1.129), (M + 4R₁M₁)dL²α₀, linear in the size (l.31), and then (3.35) by applying Lemma 1 many times (l.36–37 and the next page). The hypotheses are the plaquette bound α₀η²(L^jη)^{−2} and the bound α₀L^{−2j}(L^jη)^{−1} of D^{η*}_U∂U (REG p.077 l.36–38); at real points of the class they are CRE's (2.34) and (2.36), p.261 l.15–20, with {#D.α₀.2} α₀ := max_n α_{0,n}, small for γ small. The smallness O(1)M′α₀ ≤ a₀ is weaker than at M₁.
  {#4.26-2} VAR's constants at M′ are constants of d and L, since M′ = M′(d, L). Its smallness thresholds that contain R₁M₁, M′_Vε₀ ≤ a₅ with (166) (VAR p.304 l.21–24; VAR's regularity parameter ε₀, not the Hölder exponent ε₀ of π) and α₀ ≤ O(1)(R₁M₁)^{−1} (REG p.098 l.35–36), are upper bounds on VAR's ε₀ and on α₀, which hold for γ small.
- {#4.27} So PBG's and VAR's theorems hold on 𝐁, 𝐁″_k, and the joins at the block size M′ within their printed hypotheses.
  {#4.27-0} The top N₀ layers of 𝐁″_k are LM wide in their own units (#4.17), less than RM at the block size M for every R ≥ L. Their domains are unions of M′-blocks and their widths LM exceed R′M′ < LM₁ < M, so PBG's localisation applies there at M′ as well, with the reach of #4.25.
  {#4.27-1} So Theorems 3.1–3.3 hold with RM replaced by a common lower bound of the layer widths, under (J-W) at the width LM₁.

## The Rate and M₁

- {#4.28} *RDG's big blocks.* PRT2's δ₀ of (2.43) comes from Lemmas 2.2 and 2.4 and Proposition 2.3 of its reference [3], RDG (PRT2 p.230 l.40–43, p.250 l.33). RDG's rates are proportional to the inverse size of RDG's own big blocks ((2.22), p.579 l.11–22, and Corollary 2.3, p.580 l.62–p.581 l.11). That size is "a large positive integer defined later in this paper" (RDG p.572 l.11–13), fixed by 3^dc₂O(1)M^{−1} ≤ e^{−1} (p.579 l.21–22), so it is a constant of RDG's proof, not the size of the cubes of the domain. So δ₀ does not depend on M₁.
- {#4.29} *The choice of M₁.* c₀(1/256) < 1024/δ_* at the rate δ_* (PRT2 p.233 l.37). So (J-W), that is (2.59) at α = 1/128, δ_*LM₁/512 > 2d log c₀(1/256) + 1, and ⅛δ_*LM₁ ≥ 4 log L + 1, holds once M₁ ≥ {#D.M_J(d,L)} M_J(d, L) := max{8(4 log L + 1), 512(2d log(1024/δ_*) + 1)}/(δ_*L). So M₁ = K_{M′}M′ (#T2.9) is taken as a power of L at least 2M_J(d, L), the factor 2 for the width R′M′ ≥ ½LM₁ (#4.24), and with ½δ_Q^{pl}LM₁ ≥ 3 log L + 1 (#4.40.2-2), before M₂ = L²M₁ and M (CRE p.245 l.35–38).
