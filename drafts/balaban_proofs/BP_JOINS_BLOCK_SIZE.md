# Bałaban Proof: PBG's and VAR's Theorems on the Joins at the Block Size M′

Bałaban proof: the theorems of PBG and VAR that Bałaban applies to his joins of determining sets hold there within their printed hypotheses, at the block size M′. Used by the LF note (WILSON_IT1_LARGE_FIELD_DEPTH_2026_10_27.md, #4.10-2). Tags continue the LF's numbering; a cited #4.x not in this file is in the LF or in another file of this folder (the widths are in BP_LAYER_WIDTHS.md).

- {#4.19} (J-W) holds for M₁ large.
  - {#4.20} The rate. Two rates enter the counts: PRT2's local rate δ₀^{(2.43)}, through Lemma 2.1, and the rate δ₀^{PBG} of PBG Theorem 3.1; both depend on d and L only (PBG p.397 l.37–38, #4.28). The counts are taken at δ_* := min(δ₀^{PBG}, δ₀^{(2.43)}): the left side of (2.59) increases with δ₀ and c₀ decreases with it, so the conditions at δ_* give those at either rate.
  - {#4.21} Theorem 3.1 at the joins. PBG states the constants of Theorems 3.1–3.3, which its proofs reduce to PRT2's theorems, independent of {Ω_j} "if the conditions (2.1), (2.2) are satisfied" (PBG p.399). In PRT2's counts, (2.2) enters through the length > RM of each crossing in (2.57) (p.233): Lemma 2.1 (p.234) and the factors e^{−cδ₀RM·max(|j−j′|−1, 0)} of p.235 and p.237.
    {#4.21-0} The localisations also use the layer geometry. PRT2's cubes of the size 2ML^jη meet at most B^{j+1}(Λ_{j+1}) besides B^j(Λ_j) (p.230 l.1–2, p.235 l.35, p.238, the last four lines, p.239 l.40–42). PBG's cubes are □̃ⁿ, "a cube of the size (2 + 2n)ML^jη" (p.408 l.23–24), with n ≤ 5 in its localisation (□̃³ in its dichotomy, Ω_j(□) ⊂ □̃⁴, Ω₀(□) ⊂ □̃⁵) and n = 8 in Sect. C (p.415 l.9–10). PBG's Λ₀ of the regularity class (p.396 l.17), a neighbourhood of Ω₁ at distance ≤ RM ("e.g.", p.394 l.32), is taken at R′M′ at the joins.
    {#4.21-1} PBG's dichotomy, "The cube □̃³ is either contained in B^j(Λ_j), or intersects also the domain B^{j+1}(Λ_{j+1})" (p.408 l.29–30), omits the cubes that also meet B^{j−1}(Λ_{j−1}). These occur in PBG's own sequences at every width, and PBG's theorems on those sequences are accepted as printed (p.399). A cube of 𝒟_j has a big block in B^j(Λ_j) (p.408 l.21), so a cube □ next to ∂Ω_j has □̃⁵, of the side 12ML^jη, reaching about 6ML^jη beyond ∂Ω_j. A layer Λ_{j−1} that is LM wide in its own units is M wide in the units L^jη, so there □̃⁵ also meets Λ_{j−2}, which PBG's sequences exclude.
    {#4.21-2} CRE's maximal sequences 𝐁 have such layers at M = M₁ (p.256 l.57–62, p.245 l.35–37). So the theorems of PBG and VAR are applied on 𝐁, 𝐁″_k, and the joins at a smaller block size M′, at which no localisation cube meets Λ_{j−2} (#4.25):
    - {#4.22} *The block size M′.* Let {#D.M′} M′ := a power of L with M′ ≥ max(M₁^{PBG}, R₀M₀, R₁M₁^{VAR}): M₁^{PBG} the threshold M₁(d, L) of PBG Theorems 3.1–3.3 (p.397 l.38, "for M ≥ M₁"), R₀M₀ that of PBG's local sequences, and R₁M₁^{VAR} VAR's printed product (#T2.7), which is also REG's (VAR p.277 l.17, p.281 l.14; Cauchy #C9.1), the last two powers of L, so that M′ is a multiple of each: PBG takes M = KR₀M₀ (p.408 l.28), and VAR's cube parameters are multiples of R₁M₁ (VAR p.279 l.3, p.300 l.29–30). VAR's own M′ is written M′_V (#T2.13).
      {#4.22-0} Take {#D.K_M′} K_{M′} := a power of L with K_{M′} ≥ 12d + 1 (a power since PBG identifies the big-block lattice with T^{(j+m)} for M = L^m, p.408 l.20), and CRE's {#D.M₁} M₁ := K_{M′}M′ (#T2.15, #T2.9). Then R′ = LK_{M′} − 1 ≥ 12dL (#T2.21), since L(12d + 1) − 1 ≥ 12dL.
      {#4.22-1} Every M₁-cube of the scale j is a union of K_{M′}^d M′-cubes, so the domains of 𝐁 and of 𝐁″_k (unions of MR_j-cubes, M′ dividing M₁ and M) are unions of M′-blocks, PRT2 (2.1).
        {#4.22-1-1} R′ is not a power of L; the joins use PRT2's conditions (2.1)–(2.4), whose R is "a big positive integer" (PRT2 (2.2)). PBG's "We take these numbers as powers of L" (p.408 l.26–27) fixes R₀ and M₀ of its local sequences, whose M = KR₀M₀ has K any positive integer (l.28); its theorems require (2.1), (2.2) (p.399).
      {#4.22-2} *R′ against PBG's R.* PBG states that the theorems of [4] were proved "under the assumption that R, M are sufficiently large" (p.408 l.26) and, for its localisation property of p.426, sequences "with M and R sufficiently large" (p.426 l.38–39), so the print does not exclude a separate threshold on R. Let {#D.R_thr(d,L)} R_thr(d, L) := the largest threshold on R in PRT2, PBG, and VAR at the block size M′, a constant of d and L since M′ = M′(d, L) and their constants depend on d and L only (PBG p.397 l.37–38).
        {#4.22-2-1} Then R′ = LK_{M′} − 1 ≥ L(K_{M′} − 1) ≥ R_thr(d, L) for K_{M′} ≥ R_thr(d, L)/L + 1, a further lower bound on K_{M′} (table #T2.15).
    - {#4.22.1} *K_{M′} and M′ for the passage at a real base.* The passage draft's §9 (WILSON_IT1_PASSAGE_LAYERED_DRAFT_2026_11_04.md, *Result of (ii)*) runs PRT2's layering at R = R′ (#4.24) and M = M′, with VAR's product at the cube parameter M′ (#4.23). Its condition RM ≥ C(d)R₁M₁L (collecting *The cube Q_z*, *The block cubes*, the balls of Lemma R and of *The comparison near z*, and the distance of V from the rise of ñ) reads (LK_{M′} − 1)M′ ≥ C(d)LM′, which holds for K_{M′} ≥ C(d) + 1, since LK_{M′} − 1 ≥ L(K_{M′} − 1).
        {#4.22.1-1} Its §2 conditions M ≥ L and RM ≥ L² hold for M′ ≥ L, since then R′M′ ≥ L(K_{M′} − 1)L ≥ L².
      {#4.22.2} The large-field M enters there through the base's regularity: the base's data parameters carry M⁶, through M₀ = (1 + κ_s)O(1)B₅M⁶ (#3.96) and ε₁ = O(1)B₃²B₅M⁶ε_k (#4.281). M is fixed after K_{M′} and M′, since its lower bound M ≥ 2(d + 1)L²M₁ (#4.445.6, #4.449) involves M₁ = K_{M′}M′; the order of choice is #T1.5's.
    - {#4.23} *The product R₁M₁ = M′.* VAR's own constructions read R₁M₁ only as the product: the cube class of Theorem 1 (p.279 l.2–4), the Sect. F sequences (144) and cubes □̃ (p.300 l.33–37), (163), (165), and M(ε₁). They keep VAR's block size M₁^{VAR} (p.277 l.12) and raise VAR's R₁ from its printed value to M′/M₁^{VAR}, a power of L (Cauchy #C9.1-2), so that VAR's product is R₁M₁ = M′ (#T2.7-1). This is admissible: every printed condition on R₁ is a lower bound or one on R/R₁ (#4.24-1–#4.24-2).
      {#4.23-0} The results of [3, 5, 6] (PRT2, PBG, REG; VAR p.309) that VAR invokes on its local sequences (VAR p.277) are applied at the block size M₁^{VAR} and this R₁, admissible since they need only lower bounds on R and R₁: a sequence of unions of M′-blocks whose layers are R′M′ wide is a sequence of unions of M₁^{VAR}-blocks whose layers are R′M′/M₁^{VAR} = R′R₁ blocks wide, so VAR (1) holds at R := R′R₁ ≥ R₁ (p.277 l.17). It is not "[3, 5, 6] at the block size M′ with R = 1", under which a cube of the index j would cross a layer of the index j − 1.
    - {#4.24} *The separation.* CRE's layers satisfy dist(Ω_n, Ω_{n−1}^c) ≥ L^nξM₁ = (LK_{M′})M′L^{n−1}ξ (p.256 l.57–62), and every layer of 𝐁″_k and of the joins below the top one is ≥ LM₁ wide in its own units (#4.17, #4.18-2-2). So on all of them PRT2 (2.2) holds at the size M′ with {#D.R′} R′ := LK_{M′} − 1, and VAR (1) with R′R₁ blocks of M₁^{VAR} (#4.23-0), and every layer below the top one is ≥ R′M′ wide, with ½LM₁ ≤ R′M′ < LM₁ since LK_{M′} ≥ 2.
      {#4.24-1} The printed conditions on R, M, R₁, and M₁ are of four kinds. Lower bounds on the products RM and R₁M₁, among them VAR's "R₁M₁ is sufficiently big, so that B₃e^{−½δ₀R₁M₁} ≤ ½" (p.304 l.2–4). Separate lower bounds: R ≥ R₁ (VAR p.277 l.17), PBG's on R₀ and M₀, unchanged here (#4.22-1-1), and any threshold on R alone, met by K_{M′} ≥ R_thr(d, L)/L + 1 (#4.22-2); otherwise R enters PRT2 through RM, in (2.59) and (2.83), and through the layer geometry.
        {#4.24-1-1} Divisibility: M = KR₀M₀ (PBG p.408 l.28), and VAR's cube parameters are multiples of R₁M₁ (VAR p.279 l.3, p.300 l.29–30; REG p.098 l.7–9). Both hold: M′ is a multiple of R₀M₀ (#4.22), and VAR's cube parameters M′, 2M′, and 2LM′ (BP_VAR_COVERING.md) are multiples of R₁M₁ = M′ (#4.23).
      {#4.24-2} Ratios: REG's example "L^{−1}RM > 2dR₁M₁", which gives □̃ ⊂ Ω_{k−1} (REG p.098 l.18–19) and reads R′ > 2dL at R = R′, M = R₁M₁ = M′, true as R′ ≥ 12dL (#4.22-0); and the reach (#4.25). VAR p.300 l.36–37 states □̃ ⊂ Ω_{j−1} for its Sect. F cubes without a printed ratio; for its cubes of the side 2R₁M₁ the derived condition is R > 4dLR₁, with R counted in blocks of VAR's M₁ (the W-δ note's #W12.237-3), which at R = R′R₁ (#4.23-0) reads R′ > 4dL and holds for K_{M′} ≥ 4d + 1, since LK_{M′} − 1 ≥ 4dL + L − 1.
        {#4.24-2-1} The conditions tied to M from above are smallness conditions on α₀ and ε (PBG p.397 l.38, p.408 l.4; VAR p.304 l.24; REG p.098 l.36–37) and VAR's M ≤ M(ε₁) (p.279 l.17), which hold for γ small.
    - {#4.25} *The reach.* PBG's cubes at the size M′ are □̃ⁿ of the side (2 + 2n)M′L^jη, so an enlargement by m adds 2mM′L^jη to the side, and □₀ = □̃². In Sect. C, PBG takes G′_{□̃₀²} with the boundary conditions outside □̃₀⁵, which it identifies in the original cubes with the outside of □̃⁸, or of □̃₁⁸ with □₁ ∈ 𝒟_{j+1} (p.415 l.9–10, with p.411 l.22–25 and p.413 l.6–10); indeed □̃₀⁵ = □̃⁷ ⊂ □̃⁸.
      {#4.25-0} Theorems 3.9 and 3.10 state that their terms depend on U restricted to the enlargements X̃⁵ of their localisation domains, among them X = □̃₀² = □̃⁴, of the side 10M′L^jη, whose X̃⁵ has the side 20M′L^jη, that of □̃⁹. So □̃⁹ bounds both.
      {#4.25-1} Every other cube of PBG is □̃ⁿ with n ≤ 5, and PRT2's are at most □̃³ (PRT2 p.238, the last four lines). □̃⁹ reaches at most 10M′L^jη beyond ∂Ω_j, an ℓ¹ reach of at most 10dM′L^jη, while the layer Λ_{j−1}, ≥ LM₁ wide in its own units (#4.17), is ≥ M₁L^jη = K_{M′}M′L^jη wide in PRT2's ℓ¹ distance (#4.10), and K_{M′} ≥ 10d + 1. For □₁ ∈ 𝒟_{j+1} the same holds in the units L^{j+1}η against the layer Λ_j.
      {#4.25-2} The geometric two-layer conditions, PBG's class cubes in B^j(Λ_j) ∪ B^{j+1}(Λ_{j+1}) (the class of (3.35), p.396, used for □̃⁵ on p.409) and PRT2's torus □̃³ carrying two scales (p.238, the last two lines, and p.239), are covered by the same count; inwards they hold with a large margin, since Λ_{j+1} is ≥ L²K_{M′}M′ wide in the units L^jη. So every localisation domain of PBG meets at most the layers j − 1, j, and j + 1, as in PBG's own sequences (#4.21-1).
    - {#4.26} *The constants.* PBG's constants are then those at M′, which depend on d and L only (p.397 l.38–39). The regularity (3.35) at the cube size M′, |A| < O(1)M′α₀(L^jη)^{−1} and the like (p.396 l.25), scales with the size, so it does not follow from (3.35) at M₁. It follows at the same α₀ from the plaquette regularity in an axial gauge: REG's argument on p.098 gives (3.35) for every cube in Ω_j and not in Ω_{j+1} that is a union of R₁M₁-cubes, under L^{−1}RM > 2dR₁M₁, which holds here.
      {#4.26-1} It derives (1.128) from REG's hypotheses (1.7)–(1.9) by Proposition 2 of [3], then the axial-gauge bound (1.129), (M + 4R₁M₁)dL²α₀, linear in the size, and then (3.35) by applying Lemma 1 many times (p.098 l.37–38 and the next page). The hypotheses are the plaquette bound α₀η²(L^jη)^{−2} and the bound α₀L^{−2j}(L^jη)^{−1} of D^{η*}_U∂U, the current bound α₀(L^jη)^{−3}; at real points of the class they are CRE's (2.34) and (2.36), p.261, with {#D.α₀.2} α₀ := max_n α_{0,n}, small for γ small. The smallness O(1)M′α₀ ≤ a₀ is weaker than at M₁.
      {#4.26-2} VAR's constants at M′ are constants of d and L, since M′ = M′(d, L); its smallness thresholds that contain R₁M₁, M′_Vε₀ ≤ a₅ of VAR p.304 l.21–24 (VAR's regularity parameter ε₀, not the Hölder exponent ε₀ of π) and α₀ ≤ O(1)(R₁M₁)^{−1} of REG p.098 l.36–37, become upper bounds on VAR's ε₀ and α₀, which hold for γ small.
    {#4.27} So PBG's and VAR's theorems hold on 𝐁, 𝐁″_k, and the joins at the block size M′ within their printed hypotheses; R′ need not be a power of L (#4.22-1-1).
      {#4.27-0} At the top N₀ layers of 𝐁″_k, LM wide in their own units (#4.17), the block size M would need RM below the layer width, which at the width LM allows only R < L, and R < L need not be the "big positive integer which will be fixed later" of PRT2 (2.2) (p.224 l.11–12); the same device covers them, since their domains are unions of M′-blocks and their widths LM exceed R′M′ < LM₁ < M, so PBG's localisation is applied there at M′ as well, with the reach above.
      {#4.27-1} So Theorems 3.1–3.3 hold with RM replaced by a common lower bound of the layer widths, under (J-W) at the width LM₁.
  - {#4.28} RDG's big blocks. PRT2's δ₀ of (2.43) comes from Lemmas 2.2 and 2.4 and Proposition 2.3 of its reference [3], RDG (p.230 l.40–43, p.250 l.33). RDG's rates are proportional to the inverse size of its own big blocks ((2.22), p.579 l.11–22, and Corollary 2.3, p.580 l.62–p.581 l.11). That size is "a large positive integer defined later in this paper" (RDG p.572 l.11–13), fixed by 3^dc₂O(1)M^{−1} ≤ e^{−1}, so it is a constant of RDG's proof, not the cubes of the domain. So δ₀ does not depend on M₁.
  - {#4.29} The choice of M₁. With c₀(1/256) < 1024/δ_* at the rate δ_*, (J-W) holds once M₁ ≥ {#D.M_J(d,L)} M_J(d, L) := max{8(4 log L + 1), 512(2d log(1024/δ_*) + 1)}/(δ_*L). So M₁ = K_{M′}M′ (#T2.9) is taken as a power of L at least 2M_J(d, L), the factor 2 for the width R′M′ ≥ ½LM₁ (#4.24), and with ½δ_Q^{pl}LM₁ ≥ 3 log L + 1 (#4.40.2-2), before M₂ = L²M₁ and M (CRE p.245 l.35–38).
