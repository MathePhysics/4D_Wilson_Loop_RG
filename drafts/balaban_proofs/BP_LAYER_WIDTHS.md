# Bałaban Proof: Layer Widths of 𝐁″_k and of the Joins

Bałaban proof: properties of Bałaban's determining sets that his papers use but do not state. Used by the LF note (WILSON_IT1_LARGE_FIELD_DEPTH_2026_10_27.md, #4.10–#4.10-1). Tags continue the LF's numbering; a cited #4.x not in this file is in the LF or in another file of this folder. The joins and the units are as in LF #4.10.

- {#4.11} *The collar.* 𝐁_{j₀}(Ω) on T_ξ, ξ = L^{−j₀}, has the domains Ω = Ω_0 ⊃ Ω_1 ⊃ … ⊃ Ω_{j₀} with dist(Ω_n, Ω_{n−1}^c) ≥ L^nξM₁ (CRE p.256 l.56–61), so its layer n − 1 is at least LM₁ wide in its own units, and Ω^{∼−2} ⊂ Ω_{j₀}, "where the operation ∼ is taken for M₁-cubes" (p.256 l.62–63). So the strip {#D.S} S := Ω ∖ Ω^{∼−2} lies within ℓ∞-distance 2M₁, hence ℓ¹-distance 2dM₁, of Ω^c, and dist(Ω^{∼−2}, Ω^c_{j₀−1}) ≥ M₁.
- {#4.12} *Where the joins are formed.* By Bałaban's convention (R-Z) (#4.172–#4.172-1), which LFR II p.371 l.26–27 forces, 𝐁₁ and 𝐁₂ are formed within Z, with j₀ = h, Ω = Ω″^∼_{h+1} ∪ Z^c for 𝐁₁, and Ω = (Ω″^∼_{h+1})^c ∩ Z for 𝐁₂; off Z, 𝐁₁ is 𝐁″_k and 𝐁₂ is void (#4.172). For 𝐁_{1.70}, j₀ = h + 1 and Ω = Z″_{h+2} (#4.5-1-0). Ω″^∼_{h+1} adds one layer of MR_{h+1}-cubes of the scale h + 1 to Ω″_{h+1} (LFR I p.179 l.7–8), LMR_{h+1} wide in the units a_h.
  {#4.12-1} *Distance from Z^c.* Z is a union of components of Λ_k^c (LFR I p.177 l.17, l.33), so a nearest-neighbour path from x ∈ Z to y ∈ Z^c meets Λ_k ⊂ Ω_k ⊂ Ω_{h+1} (CRE (3.20), (2.1)), and a path monotone in every coordinate stays within ℓ∞-distance |x − y|_∞ of x. So Z″_{h+1} = (Ω^{∼5}_{h+1})^c ∩ Z (LFR I (1.11)–(1.12)) lies at ℓ∞-distance ≥ 5LMR_{h+1} from Z^c in the units a_h, and Z″_{h+2} ⊂ Z″_k = (Ω^{∼5}_{k−N₀+1})^c ∩ Z at ≥ 5MR_{k−N₀+1}a_{k−N₀+1} ≥ 5LMa_{h+1} (h + 1 ≤ k − N₀), under either reading of the enlargements (#4.18-2-1-2).
    {#4.12-1-1} So the Ω of 𝐁₂ and of 𝐁_{1.70}, which lie in Z″_{h+1} and Z″_{h+2}, are that far from Z^c, and the strip S of 𝐁₁, within ℓ∞-distance 2M₁ of Ω^c ∩ Z ⊂ Z″_{h+1}, lies at ℓ¹-distance ≥ 5LMR_{h+1} − 2dM₁ ≥ 2M₁ from Z^c.
  {#4.12-2} *∂Ω and (2.14)'s margin.* For 𝐁₁ and 𝐁₂, ∂Ω lies on ∂Ω″^∼_{h+1} ∩ Z, inside 𝐁″_k's layer h: that layer is ≥ 3LMR_{h+1} wide on Z (#4.18-1), and Ω″^∼_{h+1} adds LMR_{h+1}, so Ω″^∼_{h+1} ∩ Z ⊂ Ω″_h at ≥ 2LMR_{h+1} from (Ω″_h)^c. For 𝐁_{1.70}, ∂Ω lies on ∂Ω″_{h+2} ∩ Z, inside the layer h + 1. None meets ∂Z (#4.12-1-1). Of the two boundaries in (2.14), the one on Ω's outer side is not constrained: ∂Ω″_h ∩ Z for 𝐁₁ (Ω ∩ Z ⊂ Ω″_h), ∂Ω″_{h+1} for 𝐁₂ (Ω″_{h+1} ⊂ Ω^c), and ∂Ω″_{h+2} for 𝐁_{1.70} (Ω″_{h+2} ∩ Ω = ∅).
    {#4.12-2-1} The boundary contained in Ω is ∂Ω″_{h+1} for 𝐁₁, at ≥ LMR_{h+1} from ∂Ω; ∂Ω″_h for 𝐁₂, at ≥ 2LMR_{h+1}; and ∂Ω″_{h+1} ∩ Z for 𝐁_{1.70}, at ≥ LMR_{h+2} in the units a_{h+1}, the width of 𝐁″_k's layer h + 1 ≤ k − N₀ (#4.18-1). Their parts off Z lie outside Ω for 𝐁₂ and 𝐁_{1.70} (Ω ⊂ Z) and, for 𝐁₁, in Z^c, at ≥ 5LMR_{h+1} − 2dM₁ from ∂Ω (#4.12-1-1). All exceed 2M₁, since M > L²M₁ (#4.18-1), so (2.14)'s margin holds (CRE p.257 l.10–11).
  {#4.12-3} *M₁-cubes.* M = L^m (CRE p.245 l.34), M₁ < M is a power of L (#4.22-0, #4.29), the R_j are powers of L (CRE (2.5)), and the partitions are compatible (CRE p.245 l.38–40). Ω″^∼_{h+1}, Ω″_{h+2} ∩ Z, and Z are unions of MR_j-cubes of scales j ≥ j₀ + 1 (Z of the scale k, #4.18-2-1-2), hence of M₁-cubes of T_{L^{−j₀}}, and so is each Ω, as (2.14) requires (CRE p.257 l.9). So (2.14) holds for the three joins.
- {#4.13} *The layers of a join.* By (2.14) the join's domains are Ω^J_j = (Ω″_j ∩ Ω^{∼−2}) ∪ (Ω_j ∩ S), Ω_j the collar's. For j ≤ j₀ − 1 the distance from Ω^J_{j+1} to (Ω^J_j)^c is at least LM₁ in the units a_j, that is L^{j+1−j₀}M₁ ≤ M₁ in the units a_{j₀}, in each of four cases. From the collar to the collar or to Ω^c ⊂ Ω_j^c it is the collar's width (#4.11). 𝐁″_k to 𝐁″_k it is ≥ M (#4.18-2-2). From Ω″_{j+1} ∩ Ω^{∼−2} to S ∖ Ω_j or to Ω^c it is ≥ dist(Ω^{∼−2}, Ω^c_{j₀−1}) ≥ M₁ (#4.11).
  {#4.13-1} From Ω_{j+1} ∩ S to (Ω″_j)^c ∩ Ω^{∼−2} it is ≥ M₁. For 𝐁₂ and 𝐁_{1.70}, Ω^c ∩ Z ⊂ Ω″_{j₀} (#4.12-2), which lies at ≥ Ma_{j₀−1} = M/L from (Ω″_{j₀−1})^c ⊇ (Ω″_j)^c (#4.18-2-2), and S ⊂ Z lies within ℓ¹-distance 2dM₁ of Ω^c ∩ Z (#4.11, #4.12-1-1), so the distance is ≥ M/L − 2dM₁ > (L − 2d)M₁ ≥ M₁, by M > L²M₁ and L ≥ 2d + 1 (table #T1.3: L ≥ 12d + 6). For 𝐁₁, (Ω″_j)^c ∩ Ω lies in Z^c, since Ω ∩ Z ⊂ Ω″_h ⊂ Ω″_j, and S lies at ≥ 5LMR_{h+1} − 2dM₁ ≥ M₁ from Z^c (#4.12-1-1).
  {#4.13-2} *The layer h of 𝐁₁.* Ω″_{h+1} ⊂ Ω^{∼−2} (#4.12-2-1), so the layers of 𝐁₁ above h are 𝐁″_k's. Its layer h is ≥ LMR_{h+1} − 2dM₁ > (L³ − 2d)M₁ ≥ LM₁ wide: Ω″_{h+1} ∩ Z lies at ≥ LMR_{h+1} from Ω^c ∩ Z, hence at ≥ LMR_{h+1} − 2dM₁ from S ∖ Ω_h; Ω″_{h+1} ∩ Z^c lies at ≥ 5LMR_{h+1} − 2dM₁ from S and from Ω^c (#4.12-1–#4.12-1-1); and against (Ω″_h)^c ∩ Ω^{∼−2} it is 𝐁″_k's width.
- {#4.14} *The top layer of 𝐁₂ and of 𝐁_{1.70}.* Their top layer is j₀, since Ω ∩ Ω″_{j₀+1} = ∅ (#4.12-2). Its width is not used: it is not crossed (#4.15), and the covering of #4.7-1 does not use it (#4.7-1-2–#4.7-1-3-1).
- {#4.15} *Not crossed.* In PRT2's decomposition of a contour, each crossing joins two surfaces Σ_{j_l} and Σ_{j_{l+1}} with |j_l − j_{l+1}| = 1 and lies in the layer min{j_l, j_{l+1}} ((2.47), p.231 l.20–36). A crossing in the top layer j₀ would join Σ_{j₀} with Σ_{j₀+1}, and these joins have no Σ_{j₀+1}. So (2.57) uses only the widths of the layers below the top.
- {#4.16} The count at a smaller width. Bałaban's count (2.58) uses (2.2) only through (2.57), the length > RM of each crossing between two surfaces Σ_j (PRT2 p.233 l.18–23), and Lemma 2.1's (2.60), the decay e^{−αδ₀RM·max(|j−j′|−1, 0)} (p.234 l.2–6), follows from (2.48) and (2.57). So (2.57)–(2.61) and every count of the LF note's §4(b) hold with RM replaced by any common lower bound of the layer widths in their own units, each line unchanged.
- {#4.17} At the joins that common lower bound is LM₁: every layer of 𝐁₁, 𝐁₂, and 𝐁_{1.70} below the top is ≥ LM₁ wide in its own units (#4.13–#4.13-2), the layers of 𝐁″_k inside them ≥ M > LM₁ (#4.18-1, #4.18-2-2), and the top N₀ layers of 𝐁″_k LM (#4.17-1).
  {#4.17-1} LFR I p.179 l.21–24: Z″_k^c and Z″_{k−1} "are separated by one layer of M-cubes" of T_η, the fine lattice, whose cubes are measured in the units of the scale k (the scale-j cubes are "L^{−(k−j)}MR_j-cubes of the lattice T_η", LFR I p.179 l.7–8), and Z″_{k−1}^c and Z″_{k−2} "by one layer of L^{−1}M-cubes", and so on. So the layer j is one M-cube of the scale j + 1 wide, Ma_{j+1} = LMa_j.
    {#4.17-2} This holds for k − N₀ + 1 ≤ j ≤ k − 1. The top layer k is Ω″_k itself, has no inner boundary, and is not crossed.
- {#4.18} *The layers of 𝐁″_k.* CRE's step layers Ω_j ∖ Ω_{j+1} are at least 2MR_j wide in the units a_j: the boundaries of Ω_j and Λ_j are at least 2MR_j apart (CRE p.256 l.21–24), and Ω_{j+1} ⊂ Λ_j ⊂ Ω_j (CRE (2.1)). On Z, by LFR I (1.10)–(1.12) (p.179), the layer h is Ω_h ∖ Ω^{∼5}_{h+1}, the layer j is Ω_j^{∼5} ∖ Ω_{j+1}^{∼5} for h + 1 ≤ j ≤ k − N₀ − 1, the layer k − N₀ is Ω^{∼5}_{k−N₀} ∖ Ω^{∼7}_{k−N₀+1}, and the layers above are one M-cube of the next scale wide (#4.17-1); Ω_j^{∼n} adds n layers of the cubes of Ω_j, of the side MR_ja_j (LFR I p.179 l.7–8).
  {#4.18-0} *The Ω_j on Z are the step domains.* LFR I (1.2) writes the density on Z over the last N steps as χ_k(Ω_k^{∼4})∏_{j=k−1}^{h}𝕋^{(j)}(Z_{j+1})χ_h(Ω_h ∖ Ω_{h+1}^∼)𝕋_h(Z_h)exp A_k, h = k − N, "using the conditions (i), (ii), and the factorization property (2.22) [III]" (p.178 l.2–9).
    {#4.18-0-0} Between the scales h and k only the one-step operations 𝕋^{(j)}(Z_{j+1}) occur, which "are given by the formula (2.21) [III], in which the functions ζ, χ have the simplest form, namely by the condition (ii) no large field characteristic functions are included in them" (p.178, below (1.2)), so their ζ(Ω_{j+1}^c ∩ Z_{j+1})χ(Ω_{j+1} ∩ Z_{j+1}) are the small-field tests (1.3)–(1.9) of the step j.
    {#4.18-0-1} An R-operation at a time k′ sets domains of the scales ≤ k′ only (LFR I (1.10)–(1.12)) and outputs "The new large field region Z″_k" (p.192 l.40), which (ii) excludes inside Z in the last N steps (p.177 l.27–28). So Ω_{j+1} on Z, h ≤ j ≤ k − 1, is the domain determined by the (j + 1)-st renormalisation transformation (CRE p.256 l.21–22), the step j of the LF note.
  {#4.18-1} By CRE (3.5) (p.265), the renormalisation step j → j + 1 puts Ω_{j+1} three layers of MR_{j+1}a_{j+1}-cubes inside B^{j+1}(P^1_{j+1}), which lies five further layers inside the complement of Z′_j, the union of the MR_{j+1}a_{j+1}-cubes meeting Z_j = Λ_j^c (CRE p.264 l.52–55, p.265 l.16–22). So dist(Ω_{j+1}, Λ_j^c) ≥ 8MR_{j+1}a_{j+1}, hence dist(Ω_{j+1}, Ω_j^c) ≥ 8MR_{j+1}a_{j+1}, since Λ_j ⊂ Ω_j (CRE (2.1)); on Z this holds for h ≤ j ≤ k − 1 (#4.18-0-1).
    {#4.18-1-0} The enlargements ∼5 and ∼7 move ∂Ω_{j+1} outwards by at most 5MR_{j+1}a_{j+1} and 7MR_{j+1}a_{j+1}, and moving ∂Ω_j outwards only widens the layer. So on Z the layer j is at least 3MR_{j+1}a_{j+1} = 3LMR_{j+1}a_j wide for h ≤ j ≤ k − N₀ − 1 and the layer k − N₀ at least LMR_{k−N₀+1}, both ≥ LM > LM₁ in their own units (R_{j+1} ≥ 1, M > M₂ = L²M₁, CRE p.245 l.35–38).
    {#4.18-1-1} These bounds give VAR's (1) for 𝐁″_k on Z, (L^jη)^{−1}dist(Ω″_j^c, Ω″_{j+1}) > RM₁^{VAR}, with R and M₁^{VAR} the parameters of VAR's (1) (REG (1.4)), not CRE's M₁, under LM > RM₁^{VAR}, which holds for M large (CRE p.245 l.35–38); at the joins these parameters are R′ and M′ (#4.24), and LM > LM₁ > R′M′.
  {#4.18-2} The conditions that the counts of the LF note's §4(b) put on RM become the condition (J-W): (2.59) at α = 1/128 and ⅛δ_*LM₁ ≥ 4 log L + 1, at the rate δ_* of #4.20. Their left sides increase with the width and the right side of (2.59) decreases with α, so (J-W) implies these conditions at every α ≥ 1/128 and every width ≥ LM₁, and it replaces the conditions on R and M wherever a crossed layer is narrower than RM: at the joins and at the layers of 𝐁″_k narrower than RM (#4.17, #4.18-2-2).
  {#4.18-2-1} *Earlier R-operations and ∂Z.* An R-operation changes the Ω_j only on its component Z (Ω″_j = Ω_j off Z, LFR I (1.12)) and only enlarges them there: Ω″_j = Ω_j^{∼5} ⊇ Ω_j for h + 1 ≤ j ≤ k − N₀, and Ω″_j ⊇ Ω^{∼5}_{k−N₀+1} ⊇ Ω_j above, since Z″_j ⊂ Z″_k = (Ω^{∼5}_{k−N₀+1})^c ∩ Z (LFR I (1.10)–(1.12)).
    {#4.18-2-1-0} A renormalisation step at the time k adds Ω_{k+1} only (CRE (3.5)). So 𝐁″_k contains layers set by earlier R-operations, each with the widths of #4.17-1 and #4.18-1 at its time, and at ∂Z layers assembled from pieces, Ω″_j = (Ω″_j ∩ Z) ∪ (Ω_j ∩ Z^c). By induction over the times, from the step layers (≥ 2MR_j, #4.18), every layer is at least M wide in its own units:
    {#4.18-2-1-1} At ∂Z the pairs within Z or within Z^c are the operation's layers and the earlier ones. For the cross pair (Ω″_{j+1} ∩ Z, Ω_j^c ∩ Z^c): Ω_j^c ⊂ Ω_k^c and Λ_k^c ⊇ (Ω_k^c)^{∼2} (CRE (3.20), p.269 l.9), so a point of Ω_k^c ∩ Z^c and its two-layer cube neighbourhood lie in one component of Λ_k^c, not in Z, and the pair is ≥ 2MR_ka_k ≥ 2ML^{k−j}a_j apart. For the cross pair (Ω_{j+1} ∩ Z^c, Z ∖ Ω″_j): Z ∖ Ω″_j ⊂ Ω_j^c, so it is ≥ dist(Ω_{j+1}, Ω_j^c) before the operation, ≥ Ma_j by the induction.
    {#4.18-2-1-2} *The enlargements.* On Z the readings of ∼5 and ∼7 in LFR I (1.10)–(1.11) as enlargements of Ω_{j+1} or of Ω_{j+1} ∩ Z agree. Z is a union of MR_k-cubes of the scale k (CRE p.256 l.21–23), which the cubes of the scale j + 1 subdivide (L^{k−j−1}R_k/R_{j+1} is a power of L and ≥ 1, CRE (2.5), R_i ≤ LR_{i+1} by #4.41-1-3-1). By #4.18-2-1-1 at a point of Ω_k^c ∩ Z, every point of Z within 2MR_ka_k of Z^c lies in Ω_k ⊂ Ω_{j+1}, so a chain of touching cubes from Ω_{j+1} ∩ Z^c into Z enters Z in Ω_{j+1} ∩ Z, and a cube of Z in Ω_{j+1}^{∼n} lies in (Ω_{j+1} ∩ Z)^{∼n}.
    {#4.18-2-2} So every layer of 𝐁″_k below the top is at least M > LM₁ wide in its own units, with the widths of #4.17-1 and #4.18-1 on Z; (J-W) and the device of #4.27–#4.27-0 cover the layers narrower than RM.
