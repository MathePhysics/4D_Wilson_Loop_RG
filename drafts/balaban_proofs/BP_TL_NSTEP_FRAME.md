{#3.2.1} *The stage blocks and the choice of N (quoted and checked).* The stages' determining sets are LFR I (1.15)–(1.16) (p.180 l.6–27), with h = k − N ((1.11), p.179 l.27). 𝔹_k^{(j−h)} keeps 𝔹_k on Ω_k ∪ Z^c and on Ω_h^c ∩ Z. It keeps Γ_{k−1}, …, Γ_{j+1}, covers (Ω_{j+1}^c∖Z″_j) ∩ Z by layer-j blocks, and puts Γ″_{j−1}, …, Γ″_{h+1}, Γ_h ∩ Z″_{h+1} below, for j = h + 1, …, k. So a point of layer n ≥ h lies in a stage block of layer J with n ≤ J ≤ k, and a point of layer n < h lies in one of layer n. Hence J − n ≤ k − h = N.
