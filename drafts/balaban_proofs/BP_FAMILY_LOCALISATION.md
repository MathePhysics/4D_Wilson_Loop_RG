## Proof of Lemma 10.14 (BETA #B10.177)

{#BFL.181} We follow RG2 §1 (pp.002–011), which localises functions 𝐄(X, 𝐔, 𝐉, 𝐀), analytic and localized to X in 𝐔, 𝐉 and the field 𝐀, with 𝐀 replaced by a nonlocal expression built from propagators, from D and from the solution of an equation of the form VAR (111) (p.002 l.11–14, (1.2)–(1.4)).
1. {#BFL.182} *Form.* By #B10.171-1 and #BFP.175-4, R_bg = −½⟨A*, Δ̂₁A*⟩ + V^A(A*) − ⟨A*, (δV^A/δA)(A*)⟩, with A* = h^{1/2}δ′ and δ′ the solution of (111) with B = 0, whose source can be taken to be J_γ := (h_i^{−1}γ_iJ_{c,i})_i = h^{−1/2}s_A (#BFP.175), local in U as J_c is (Lemma 10.10(d)); at (𝐔, 𝐉) the operators and V^A are built with J♮(𝐔, 𝐉). ⟨A*, (δV^A/δA)(A*)⟩ is (d/dt)V^A(tA*) at t = 1.
   {#BFL.182-0} A* is tangent, so ⟨A*, h^{1/2}J♮⟩ = ⟨δ′, hJ♮⟩ = Σ_iγ_i⟨δ′_i, J_{c,i}⟩ = ⟨A*, s_A⟩ by Lemma 10.13(b), and (82) at A* (#BFP.175-4) gives −⟨A*, Δ̂₁A*⟩ = ⟨A*, s_A⟩ + ⟨A*, (δV^A/δA)(A*)⟩. Hence R_bg = ½⟨A*, s_A⟩ + V^A(A*) − ½⟨A*, (δV^A/δA)(A*)⟩, in which Δ̂₁ no longer appears; this form, defined for every s, is the one localised below.
   {#BFL.182-1} By #BFP.175-3, each term of R_bg is a pairing of two fields, or for V₀ a sum over plaquettes of a local function of one field, the fields being s_A, J♮, or obtained from A* by Ĥ, h^{1/2}Ĥh^{−1/2}, Δ̂_πĤ, C, D^A and its derivatives. Δ̂_π enters only through Δ̂_πĤ = Q*(QĜQ*)^{−1}(L^{j(·)}η)^{−1} − Q*a(L^{j(·)}η)^{−1}, from RD*Ĥ = 0 and L^jηQ_jĤ = 1 as in VAR (137) (p.298 l.52–57), so no covariant Laplacian appears, whose bounds PBG excludes (p.423 l.36).
   {#BFL.182-2} Restricting each pairing to the bonds, and each plaquette sum to the plaquettes, of a cube □ ∈ π_j, a bond or plaquette belonging to the cube whose half-open version contains its lowest corner, gives R_bg = Σ_□E(□), with E(□) analytic and local in U, J and these fields on □, of RG2's form.
2. {#BFL.183} *Operators.* At (U, J), Ǧ = h^{−1/2}𝔊̂h^{1/2} (#BFP.164-1), which is Ǧ₁ − Ǧ₁DRD*Ǧ₁ − Ȟ₁QǦ₁ with PBG's normalisation of Q, Ǧ₁ := h^{−1/2}Ĝ₁h^{1/2} and Ȟ₁ := h^{−1/2}Ĥ₁h^{1/2}, as D, R and Q commute with h^{±1/2}.
   {#BFL.183-0} So every operator of step 1 is built by the formulas and series of Lemma 10.11's steps 4–6 and 8, and conjugation by h^{±1/2}, from local factors (D, Q, R(𝐔_b), E_±, m, K_C, J♮ and C^{(2)}) and from the *basic operators*: P₀^{−1}, G′, (Q′G′²Q′*)^{−1} and X_♭^{−1} (step 5) for each G_i at Ad_i𝐔, with PBG's walk expansions (3.107), (3.90) and (3.98) and #BX.7-1a's; P enters as the product of its factors (#BG.3-1).
   {#BFL.183-0-1} On 𝒰(Y) the operators are defined by these formulas with J♮(𝐔, 𝐉), as RG1 extends propagators (p.269 l.38–p.270 l.2); (3.124) and (3.152) are not used off (U, J).
   {#BFL.183-1} Those steps use of the basic operators only the bounds of the sums over the walks of the absolute values of their terms (#BG.11-3, with #BG.6's formulas, in which symmetry has removed the transposes of G′ and P; #BFP.156-2, #BFP.157; #BX.8-2, with P's types from its factors by Lemma C, #BG.4), and of the local factors only their bounds.    {#BFL.183-1a} A term of a walk ω obeys #BG.3-1's bound with ½δ₀d(ω, y, y′) for ½δ₀d(y, y′), or, for X_♭^{−1}, #BX.7-1a's with d(ω, c, c′): (3.94), (3.99) and (3.108), at 𝐔 by #BX.5–#BX.5-2 and #BX.7-1a–#BX.7-1c, each term a function of 𝐔 on its domain. ## Proof of Lemma 10.15 (BETA #B10.189)
