# Bałaban Proof: χ_{k,Λ} on the First Class

On the components of the first class, at the soft thresholds, |U_{k,Z}(∂p) − 1| < 2ε_kη² for every plaquette p of Ω_k^c (#3.89–#3.90), so χ_{k,Λ} = 1 there; LFR II states this as "This is clear for χ_{k,Λ}" (p.378 l.10). #-tags not in this file are those of the large-field note (WILSON_IT1_LARGE_FIELD_DEPTH_2026_10_27.md, #3.83).

χ_{k,Λ} tests inf_{V_k⌈Λ}sup_{p∈Ω_k^c}|U_{k,Z}(∂p) − 1| < 2ε_kη², U_{k,Z} = U(𝔹_k(Z), M^·(Q_k^{s*}V_k)) being the global minimiser (LFR I (1.74)–(1.75)), so it is 1 when the supremum at V_k itself is < 2ε_kη². χ_k tests the local minimisers U_{k,□} = U(𝔹_k(□^{∼4}), M^·(Q_k^{s*}V_k)) (CRE (2.16)–(2.17)). U_{k,Z} is defined in each component of Z separately (LFR I p.192 l.55), so below Z, Z^c, 𝔹_k(Z), and the Z-problem are those of one component X_i of Z.

- {#3.84} *The geometry.* Λ_k ⊂ Ω_k^{∼−2} by CRE (3.20) (p.269 l.9), which builds Λ_k at the step to the scale k; the modification of p.269 l.14–16 only enlarges Λ_k^c, and the 𝐑-operation in which χ_{k,Λ} enters follows that step, so the case Λ_j = Ω_j that p.255 l.2–3 admits as a result of an 𝐑-operation does not occur for j = k. So every plaquette p of Ω_k^c ∩ X_i is at distance ≥ 2MR_k from Λ_k, and so from X_i^c, whose points lie in Λ_k or in other components of Λ_k^c (CRE p.264 l.52), separated from X_i by Λ_k.
  {#3.84-0} Every LM₂R_k-cube □ ⊂ X_i, the cube of p included, lies in Ω_k^{∼4} or in (Ω_k^{∼4})^c ∩ X_i, the partitions being compatible (CRE p.245 l.38–40), so on the first class it passes the soft test, from χ_k(Ω_k^{∼4}) of LFR I (1.71) or from the decomposition of p.378 l.6 in the components of Z, both of width κ_s: sup_{p′⊂□^∼}|U_{k,□}(∂p′) − 1| < (1 + κ_s)ε_kη² (CRE (2.17)). For the cube of p, |U_{k,□}(∂p) − 1| < (1 + κ_s)ε_kη².
  {#3.84-1} The operation ∼ on □ adds a layer of M₂R_k-cubes, and M is much larger than M₂ > M₁ (CRE p.245 l.34–41), so □^{∼4} lies within (dL + 4)M₂R_k ≤ MR_k of p (as in #3.76) and inside Z^{∼−2} (∼ for M₁-cubes), which lies in the deepest domain of 𝔹_k(Z), made of k-blocks (CRE p.256 l.58–63).
  {#3.84-1.1} *The data.* By CRE (1.3) and the Cauchy note's #C11b.2-4 for the chain's average RG I (0.4), M^j(Q_k^{s*}V_k) is 1 on the level-j bonds inside k-blocks and V_k(c) on those joining the k-blocks of a bond c, so its plaquettes are 1 or plaquettes of V_k. On the k-plaquettes of each □ of #3.84-0, V_k = M^k(U_{k,□}), the constraint of the □^{∼4}-problem on its deepest domain, which contains (□^{∼4})^{∼−2} ⊃ □^∼ (CRE p.256 l.62–63; 3M₂R_k > 2M₁).
    {#3.84-1.1-1} The soft test bounds the plaquettes of U_{k,□} in □^∼ by aη², a := (1 + κ_s)ε_k (#3.84-0), and Proposition 2 of CMP98, valid for (0.4) (Cauchy #C10.1.4-0-3) and applied as at REG p.87 l.35 with L^kη = 1, gives |∂V_k − 1| < a + 2C₀a² < 2a for γ small (#T1.9; C₀ the constant of CMP98 (54), p.026 l.46–52, written C₁ at REG p.87 l.35).
      {#3.84-1.1-1-1} This is p.378 l.10–11's 2ε_k at the soft threshold, so VAR (7) holds for the Z- and □^{∼4}-problems with ε₁ = 2(1 + κ_s)ε_k. On the deepest domain, VAR Theorem 1 ((8), p.279) then gives U_{k,Z} the bounds of VAR (2) (p.278) at the level k, which imply those of every level j ≤ k.
  {#3.84-1.2} So on □^{∼4}, |U_{k,Z}(∂p) − 1| < α₀η², and U_{k,Z} ∈ 𝔘_k({Ω_j}, α₀) for the domains of 𝔹_k(□^{∼4}), with REG's regularity parameter α₀ of (1.7) (p.77) equal to 2B₃(1 + κ_s)ε_k. This α₀, and the α₁ of #3.86.1 (REG's of (1.35)), are local to #3.84-1.2–#3.87; they are not those of #T3.9 and #T3.12.
- {#3.85} *U_{k,Z} as the background.* 𝔹_k(□^{∼4}) is the determining set of the domains □^{∼4} = Ω_0 ⊃ Ω_1 ⊃ … ⊃ Ω_k of CRE p.256 l.57–63 with (2.2) (p.255 l.15), with support in □^{∼4} (p.256 l.58–59). Its data M^·(U_{k,Z}) are U_{k,Z} itself on □^{∼4} ∖ Ω_1, its layer of the scale 0, and L^n-block averages on Ω_n ∖ Ω_{n+1} (Ω_{k+1} := ∅).
  {#3.85.1} Each Ω_n is a union of L^n-blocks (p.256 l.60), so, top-down, every k-block average of 𝔹_k(Z) on a bond meeting □^{∼4} (bonds as at REG p.77) is a composition of these averages and of U off □^{∼4}, since each one-step average Ū^n(c) reads Ū^{n−1} only on the bonds with both end-points in the blocks of c₋ and c₊ (RG I (0.4), p.253; balaban_proofs/BP_DERIVED_CONFIGS.md #BC.3-2).
    {#3.85.1-1} A variation of U_{k,Z} that keeps the data of 𝔹_k(□^{∼4}) is supported in Ω_1 (#3.85), so it keeps those of 𝔹_k(Z): the k-block averages on bonds meeting □^{∼4} by #3.85.1, the other k-block averages because they read only k-blocks off □^{∼4}, whose bonds miss Ω_1 by dist(Ω_1, Ω_0^c) ≥ LξM₁ (CRE p.256 l.61), and the data of the levels j < k because they lie in the layers within 2M₁ of Z^c (p.256 l.61–62), at distance ≥ MR_k − 2M₁ from □^{∼4} (#3.84-1).
    {#3.85.1-2} It changes A^η by the same amount in both problems. So U_{k,Z}, a minimum of the Z-problem, is a local minimum of the □^{∼4}-problem at its own data: a small neighbourhood of it there lies in the Z-problem's space, open as VAR (2)'s bounds (p.278 l.5–6) are strict inequalities between continuous functions of the bond variables.
  {#3.85-1} These data satisfy VAR (7) with ε₁ = 2α₀ (REG p.87 l.35, from #3.84-1.2), and U_{k,Z} lies in that problem's space (6) at the regularity (1 + 2B₃)α₀ ≤ a₀ (#3.84-1.2; #T3.29; γ small). So, being critical, it is that problem's minimiser by the uniqueness of the critical orbit (VAR Theorem 1, p.279 l.15; CRE p.256 l.43–53), the property of U_{k+1,□′} that CRE (3.6) uses. As in (3.6), by VAR Proposition 9 (its hypothesis (172) is checked at #3.86-1.1), U_{k,□} = (exp(iη𝐇_{k,□}(B))U_{k,Z})^{u^{−1}}, with {#D.B} B := (1/i)log[M^·(Q_k^{s*}V_k)(M^·(U_{k,Z}))^{−1}].
- {#3.86} *The argument B.* The minimisers are gauge covariant (CMP98 (11), p.19 l.13, with the gauge invariance of A^η), so #3.85-1 may be read after one gauge transformation of the data and of U_{k,Z} together. Take w constant on each k-block: on the k-blocks of □^{∼4} and of the layer of k-blocks around it, which also lie in the deepest domain of 𝔹_k(Z) (#3.84-1), equal to the complete axial gauge of V_k there, and 1 on the other k-blocks. It turns Q_k^{s*}V_k into Q_k^{s*}V_k^w (CRE (1.3)).
  {#3.86-0} For U_{k,Z}^w take the element of its orbit under REG's subgroup (1.14) (p.78) for the k-block centres, which keeps the data of the Z-problem, in REG's axial gauge (1.15) (p.78) inside these k-blocks, unique by p.78.
  {#3.86.1} Off the boundary layer of □^{∼4} of width 2M₁ both data are V_k^w: the constraint of U_{k,Z}, and M^k(Q_k^{s*}V_k) = V_k as in (3.6). So B = 0 there. □^{∼4} with its layer of k-blocks is a cube of side (L + 8)M₂R_k + 2 ≤ (dL + 4)M₂R_k ≤ MR_k (d = 4, L ≥ 2; #3.84-1), so each bond of V_k^w on it, those of the data at ∂□^{∼4} included, is the holonomy of a loop of tree bonds and itself, spanning at most (d − 1)MR_k plaquettes, hence a product of as many transported plaquette variables of V_k.
    {#3.86.1-1} Each of these plaquettes is a k-plaquette of an LM₂R_k-cube □′ meeting this cube, and □′ ⊂ X_i: its points differ from p by less than MR_k + LM₂R_k < 2MR_k in every coordinate (M ≥ (dL + 4)M₂, #3.76), so they lie outside Λ_k and are joined to p in Λ_k^c (#3.84). So |V_k^w − 1| < α₁ := 2(d − 1)MR_k(1 + κ_s)ε_k (#3.84-1.1-1), with α₁ → 0 as γ → 0 (#T2.23, #T3.27). So do the bonds of M^j(Q_k^{s*}V_k^w), which are 1 or bonds of V_k^w (#3.84-1.1).
  {#3.86-1} On these k-blocks REG's iteration of Lemma 1 (p.87 l.35–40), local by p.80 l.4–5, applies with U₀ = 1 and U′ = U_{k,Z}^w: for U₀ = 1 the relative axial gauge (1.19) is (1.15), Proposition 2 of CMP98 turns the bounds of #3.84-1.2 on □^{∼4} and of #3.84-1.1-1-1 on the k-blocks around it into Lemma 1's (1.7) at every level, and its start is |Ū′^k − 1| = |V_k^w − 1| < α₁. So |M^j(U_{k,Z}^w) − 1| < 11d²α₀ + α₁ there for γ small.
    {#3.86-1.1} So V′ := M^·(Q_k^{s*}V_k^w)(M^·(U_{k,Z}^w))^{−1} = e^{iB} has |V′ − 1| < ε₁′ := 11d²α₀ + 2α₁ on the layer, by |ab^{−1} − 1| = |a − b| for unitary a, b, and |B| ≤ (π/2)(11d²α₀ + 2α₁) there (#T11.26). This is VAR (172) with its constant 1 and VAR's ε₁ raised to ε₁′.
    {#3.86-1.1-1} At ε₁′ VAR (7) still holds for both data (2α₀, 2(1 + κ_s)ε_k ≤ 2α₁ ≤ ε₁′), ε₁′ ≤ a₁ and B₃ε₁′ ≤ a₀ for γ small (ε₁′ → 0 with α₁), and the minimal orbit is the same by the uniqueness of the critical orbit (VAR p.279 l.15).     {#3.86-1-1} B depends on the choice of U_{k,Z}^w in #3.86-0; #3.85.1–#3.85-1 hold for every element of the orbit. U_{k,□}^w is the minimiser at the data M^·(Q_k^{s*}V_k^w) = e^{iB}M^·(U_{k,Z}^w), and exp(iη𝐇_{k,□}(B))U_{k,Z}^w is its Landau-gauge representative relative to U_{k,Z}^w (REG (1.36)–(1.38); CRE p.251 l.35), which does not carry the data (REG p.81 l.23–24) and enters only through gauge-covariant plaquettes (#3.88). - {#3.87} *The decay.* dist(□^∼, layer) ≥ 3M₂R_k − 2M₁ ≥ 2M₂R_k. At B = 0 the minimiser is U_{k,Z}^w, so 𝐇_{k,□}(0) = 0 and 𝐇_{k,□}(B) = ∫₀¹(δ𝐇_{k,□}/δB)(tB)B dt, each tB satisfying (172) as B does (#3.86-1.1).   {#3.87-1} Summed over the layer as in (3.7) (p.266 l.14–22), with CRE's rate δ and constant B₃ there (#T10.1-1), this gives |𝐇_{k,□}|, |∇^η_{U_{k,Z}^w}𝐇_{k,□}| ≤ B₃e^{−δ2M₂R_k}sup|B| ≤ τε_k on □^∼, with {#D.τ} τ := (π/2)B₃(22d²B₃ + 4(d − 1)MR_k)(1 + κ_s)e^{−δ2M₂R_k}, by #3.86-1.1 and #3.84-1.2. - {#3.88} *The plaquette.* REG (1.43) with U₀ = U_{k,Z}^w and U₁ = exp(iη𝐇_{k,□}) gives W − 1 = PXQ + XS + X + E₂, with {#D.X} X := U_{k,Z}^w(∂p) − 1, of the norm of U_{k,Z}(∂p) − 1, and {#D.W} W := (U₁U₀)(∂p). Here S = Q − 1, so W − 1 = (1 + P)XQ + E₂, and 1 + P and Q, products of factors U₁(b) and transports R(U₀(·)), are unitary. So |X| ≤ |W − 1| + |E₂|.
  {#3.88-1} E₂ = (∂_{U₀}U₁)(p) − 1, by REG (1.22) (p.079), read with U₁(y, z) as its third factor, as in (1.21), for which (1.43) is an identity (the print has U′(z, y)), the product of the four factors e^{iA_i} := U₁(b)^{±1} of p, two of them conjugated by U₀, minus 1, with |A_i| ≤ s := τε_kη (#3.87). Its linear part iΣ±A_i is iη times two U₀-covariant differences of 𝐇_{k,□}, of norm ≤ 2τε_kη² (#3.87).
    {#3.88-1-1} The rest is Σ(e^{iA_i} − 1 − iA_i), each ≤ s²/2, plus the products of two or more factors e^{iA_i} − 1, each ≤ s, so |E₂| ≤ 2τε_kη² + 8s² + 4s³ + s⁴ ≤ (2τ + 9τ²)ε_kη² by ε_k, η ≤ 1 and s ≤ 1/20. And |W − 1| = |U_{k,□}(∂p) − 1|, U₁U₀ being gauge equivalent to U_{k,□} (#3.86-1-1). Hence
  {#3.89} |U_{k,Z}(∂p) − 1| ≤ (1 + κ_s + 2τ + 9τ²)ε_kη² < 2ε_kη²
  {#3.90} for κ_s ≤ 1/4 and τ ≤ 1/20, the middle term being then at most 1.3725ε_kη². So χ_{k,Λ} = 1 on the first class at the soft thresholds.
