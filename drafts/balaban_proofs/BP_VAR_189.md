## Statement

- {#BV.1} *Statement.* Let d = 4 and L ≥ 5, let U₀ be as in VAR Sects. B–D (G-valued, VAR (14)), and let ⅛δ₀RM ≥ 4 log L + 1, RM ≥ 4dL, and PRT2 (2.59) at α = 1/128 hold, the conditions of LF #4.33, which VAR allows. There are ε̄ > 0 and C₁₈₉, constants of d and L, such that for complex A′ with |A′|_∞ := max{|A′|_{(−1)}, |∇A′|_{(−2)}} < ε₃ ≤ ε̄, for 𝔄 with supp 𝔄 ⊂ Δ̃(y′), y′ ∈ Λ_{j′}, and for b ∈ Δ(y), y ∈ Λ_j,
  {#BV.1-1} (L^jη)³|((δ²V/δA′²)(A′)𝔄)(b)| ≤ C₁₈₉ε₃e^{−¼δ₀d(y,y′)}|𝔄|_∞.
  {#BV.1-2} This is (189) with O(1) = C₁₈₉. (δ²V/δA′²)(A′)𝔄 := (d/dτ)(δV/δA′)(A′+τ𝔄)|_{τ=0} as in (185); V is (80); δ₀ is the common rate of PBG p.423 l.39–40; d is PRT2's distance (2.46) (p.231 l.10–13; #BQ.2-1); Δ(y) = B^j(y), and Δ̃(y) is the cube of side 2L^jη centred at y (PBG p.397 l.30–31).
  {#BV.1-3} The chain's local sequences meet RM ≥ 4dL by RM ≥ R₀M₀ (#BD.14-3b) and the condition R₀ > 5dL that #W11.105-6 imposes on PBG's R₀ (#T2.6). RG1's L is odd and > 11 (p.251 l.14–15).

A block bond c of 𝔅 of the layer j has c₋ ∈ Λ_j, and j(c) := j, y(c) := c₋: the block bonds are indexed by their lower endpoints, as in PBG (3.133), where H_{μν}(x,y′) has y′ ∈ Λ_{j′} (p.422 l.25–27), in VAR (69), with d(c₋,c′₋) for c ∈ Λ_j (p.288 l.45), and in #BQ.1.

  {#BV.3-1d} *Case I*, Ω_{j+1} ∩ C = ∅. The blocks B^j(y + a_jm), m ∈ {−2,…,2}^d, lie in C, as above. Γ: the bonds of T^{(j)} from y to Z, one coordinate at a time (at most 2d, through the blocks above), then those of T^{(j−1)} from Z to w in B^j(Z), as w − Z ∈ a_{j−1}{0,…,L−1}^d (at most d(L−1)).
  {#BV.3-1e} *Case II*, Ω_{j+1} meets C.   {#BV.3-1e-1} Γ: the bonds of T^{(j)} from y to Y in B^{j+1}(Y) (at most d(L−1)), those of T^{(j+1)} from Y to W (at most d), and, if w = Z, those of T^{(j)} from W to Z in B^{j+1}(W) (at most d(L−1)).
  {#BV.3-1f} *The bound.* In both cases, where Γ runs a bond of T^{(s)}, the blocks of order s at its ends each lie in one B^r(Λ_r), as a block of Λ_s or a union of blocks of Λ_{s−1}, and the bonds of order s − 1 run inside blocks of the second kind. ## The Bounds

- {#BV.12} *Lemma C (chains).* Let T(u₀) := Σ_{u₁,…,u_n}Π_{i=1}^nν(u_i)k_i(u_{i−1},u_i)·g(u_n), where each k_i is a link of rate ≥ ½δ₀ and constant κ_i, or a local link with constant κ_i. Put {#D.c_C} c_C := 2dLe^{δ₀ρ₀}c_tot(1/128).
  {#BV.12-1} (i) If |g(v)| ≤ γe^{−rd(y(v),y′)} with δ₀/128 ≤ r ≤ 15δ₀/32, then |T(u₀)| ≤ γΠ_i(c_Cκ_i)e^{−(r−δ₀/128)d(y(u₀),y′)}. (ii) If |g| ≤ γ, then |T(u₀)| ≤ γΠ_i(c_Cκ_i).
