## What is expected

- {#DP.1} At the stopping scale the effective coupling is g_{K′} ≈ ḡ, small, on the whole fixed torus. In this regime the covariance of two small loops decays as a power of D: perturbatively it is of order g²(ℓ₁ℓ₂)²/D⁴ when π_i has a nonzero central part, and of order g⁴(ℓ₁ℓ₂)⁴/D⁸ otherwise. Exponential decay in D at scales below the torus size would need a mass gap at the torus scale, and is not what holds here.
- {#DP.2} Target (Theorem D). For ḡ ≤ ḡ_D, |Cov_{𝐏_{ℓ₀}}(O₁, O₂)| ≤ C_D|e^{E^{(1)}_{const,K′}+E^{(2)}_{const,K′}}|ḡ²(ℓ₁ℓ₂/D² + (T_ph/u)²ℓ₁ℓ₂/N_s²), with C_D fixed before the coupling. The first term is the fluctuation part (#DP.3, #DP.8), whose D-dependence comes from the decay of the fluctuation covariances. The second is the torus-scale part (#DP.9); it does not depend on D, and (T_ph/u)²ℓ₁ℓ₂/N_s² = ℓ₁^{ph}ℓ₂^{ph}/u² (#PF0.2).
  - {#DP.2-1} A remainder |e^{E^{(1)}_{const,K′}+E^{(2)}_{const,K′}}| times a superpolynomially small function of ḡ is absorbed into the second term for ḡ small, since ℓ_i ≥ 1 and N_s² ≤ C_q^{1/2}(log ḡ^{−2})^{2r} (Cauchy #C11c.7-2); each use is stated.

## Structural facts at the stopping scale

- {#DP.3.0} *The torus is a bounded number of MR-cubes wide.* N_s = (T_ph/u)L^{j(C)} with j(C) the least j ≥ q + q̄(j), L^{q̄(j)} ≤ L⁴M(1 + γ²β′(j+1))^{β₀}(log g_min^{−2})^r (#MOMGD.30, #MD2.3.4.15-6), and MR_{K′} ≥ M(log g_{K′}^{−2})^r (CRE (2.5), p.255). Since j(C) ≤ q + q̄(j(C)) (q̄ is nondecreasing and j(C) − 1 fails the condition), and under the prescription g_K = ḡ with g_{K′} ≤ (1 + β₀)ḡ (CRE (2.6)), N_s/(MR_{K′}) ≤ 4(T_ph/u)L^{q+4} for ḡ small, where the 4 absorbs (1 + γ²β′(j(C) + 1))^{β₀} and (log ḡ^{−2}/log g_{K′}^{−2})^r. Bałaban's localised steps, built on MR-cubes, do not continue above K′. Above K′ the analysis uses ρ_{ℓ₀} itself, VAR's minimisers and Bałaban's change of variables (RG I pp.265–268), which do not need MR-cubes.
- {#DP.3.0-1} *𝐄 is analytic only at the scale of the coupling.* The terms of 𝐄 are analytic on U_j^c(X, α_{0,j}, α_{1,j}) with α_{0,j} = g_jC₀(log g_j^{−2})^{q₀} and α_{1,j} = g_jC₁(log g_j^{−2})^{q₁} (CRE (2.26)–(2.28), p.259). In the scaled variables B (B′ = g_kCB), the Cauchy estimate gives Hessians of order (log g^{−2})^{−2q₁}: small relative to the Gaussian part, but only by a power of log g^{−2}. Lemma A needs only relative smallness, so this suffices for convexity. It does not suffice for errors that grow with the volume N_s⁴, so no step of the route bounds an error by such a sum.
- {#DP.3.0-2} *Gauge directions.* Bałaban's step carries the soft axial gauge fixing exp(−G(V)/g_k²) (RG I (2.1), p.265; G^{(2)} in (2.5), p.266). It makes the Hessian nondegenerate along the block gauge directions. That its insertion multiplies the integral of a gauge-invariant integrand by a constant independent of W, so that the step computes the unfixed conditional expectation, is to be derived from G's definition, which RG I takes from Bałaban's earlier papers (p.265 refers to Sect. G of [15]); it is read before use.

## Route: one Helffer–Sjöstrand bound in the joint fluctuation variables

- {#DP.3} *Decomposition.* By the law of total covariance, Cov_{𝐏_{ℓ₀}}(O₁, O₂) = 𝐄_{ℓ₀}[Cov(O₁, O₂ | V_K)] + Cov_{𝐏_{ℓ₀}}(F₁, F₂), with F_i := 𝐄_{ℓ₀}[O_i | V_K] and V_K = M^{K−K′}V the field on the final lattice. Given V_K, iterate Bałaban's change of variables V_k = e^{iB′_k}V^{(k)}(V_{k+1}), V^{(k)} = M^kU_{k+1}(V_{k+1}) and B′_k = g_kCB_k − hD̃(g_kCB_k) (RG I (2.3), p.265; p.267; p.268) for K′ ≤ k < K. The conditional law of 𝐁 := (B_{K′}, …, B_{K−1}) has the explicit density ρ_{ℓ₀}(V_{K′}(𝐁, V_K)) times the gauge-fixing factors and the Jacobians of (2.10)–(2.12).
- {#DP.4} *Lemma A (proved).* For a uniformly convex density on ℝ^n with Hessian ≥ m and entries ≤ Ke^{−κd(a,b)}, |Cov(F, G)| ≤ (2/m)Σ_{a,b}e^{−λd(a,b)}‖∂_aF‖‖∂_bG‖ when KλS_λ ≤ m/2 (#DA.6, from #DA.3–#DA.4 and Johnsen Proposition 4.5).
- {#DP.5} *(H) The joint Hessian.* Needed: on the small-fluctuation region, the Hessian of −log of the density of #DP.3 in 𝐁 is ≥ m > 0, and its entries are ≤ Ke^{−κd} for the scale-aware pseudometric d((k, b), (j, b′)) := L^{K′−max(k,j)}|b − b′|, the distance in units of the coarser of the two scales, with m, K, κ fixed before the coupling.
  - {#DP.5-1} The diagonal blocks are (C^{(k)})^{−1} = C*Δ^{(k)}C (RG I p.268), with λ_min ≥ (B₀S₀)^{−1} by PBG Theorem 3.15, (3.187) (#MOMST.55-3), S₀ := sup_yΣ_{y′}e^{−δ₀|y−y′|}. The Gaussian part has no cross-scale blocks, since the linear term ⟨H₁B′, J⟩ vanishes on the linearised constraint (RG I p.267).
  - {#DP.5-2} The cubic and higher terms of the action, the Jacobian, log σ and G₃ give blocks of size O(ε_{K′}) within the small-fluctuation region. Their cross-scale decay in d must be derived from the derivative bounds of U_{k+1}(·) (VAR, PBG).
  - {#DP.5-3} *The 𝐄- and 𝐑-terms.* RG I's space U_j^c(X, α₀, α₁) (Definition, p.262, (1.11)–(1.16)) allows complex deformations U = exp(iξA′)U with |A′|, |∇_U^ξA′| < α₁ (1.13). A unit change of B_k moves the field by a deformation of amplitude O(g_kL^{−(k−K′)}) and gradient O(g_kL^{−2(k−K′)}) per scale-K′ unit (to be derived from VAR's derivative bounds, #DP.7). So the Cauchy radius along B_k is of order α₁L^{k−K′}/g, and each affected domain contributes O(E₀g²L^{−2(k−K′)}/α₁²) to the Hessian. A coarse variable affects O(L^{4(k−K′)}) domains, and L^{k−K′} ≤ N_s/(T_ph/u). So every entry and every Schur sum of the 𝐄-part is at most C(log g^{−2})^{−2q₁}N_s^c for a fixed c, which is ≤ C′(log ḡ^{−2})^{cr−2q₁} by #C11c.7-2. This tends to 0 when 2q₁ > cr. The project's stage-0 choice is q₁ = q₀ > p₀ + 3r ≥ 8r (#T6.17, provisional, with CRE p.246's p₀ ≥ 5r), which gives 2q₁ > 16r. The 𝐑-terms carry the extra factor g^{κ₀} (CRE (2.31)). The value of c is to be fixed in the derivation; no gauge-invariance argument is needed, because the torus is polylogarithmic.
- {#DP.6} *(C) Convexification.* Replace each local term's argument by a bondwise radial truncation T(B)_b = τ(|B_b|)B_b, with τ = 1 on [0, R] and |T(B)_b| ≤ 2R, R of order ε_ty/g_{K′}. The truncated density is uniformly convex on ℝ^n by #DP.5. It agrees with the true one on the small-fluctuation region, and 𝒱_ty lies in that region, the plaquettes of every V_k being small on 𝒱_ty (#BPA.2). The errors are 𝐏_{ℓ₀}(𝒱_ty^c), made ≤ ḡ^{10} by adding 10 log ḡ^{−1} to T_ty, and the truncated measure's mass outside the region, at most N_s⁴e^{−cR²} by Gaussian concentration, which is superpolynomially small.
- {#DP.7} *(G) Gradients.* O_i is an explicit function of 𝐁 given V_K, so only derivatives of explicit maps enter: |∂_{B_k(b)}O_i| ≤ C_Gg_k·w_i^{(k)}e^{−δ_Gd_k(b, C_i)}, with w_i^{(k)} = 1 for s_i^{(k)} := ℓ_iL^{K′−k} ≥ 1 and w_i^{(k)} = (s_i^{(k)})² for s_i^{(k)} < 1. Needed: the derivative bounds of V^{(k)}(·) = M^kU_{k+1}(·) and of B′_k(B_k) (VAR Theorem 1, PBG), with the lasso for loops below the block size.
- {#DP.8} *Summation.* #DP.4 with #DP.5–#DP.7 gives |Cov(O₁, O₂ | V_K)| ≤ C g²Σ_{k,j}(scale-k and scale-j perimeter sums)e^{−λd}. The same-scale pairs give at most 16C′g²(ℓ₁ℓ₂/D²)Σ_mt_m²e^{−λt_m}, with t_m := DL^{−m}. Since Σ_mt_m²e^{−λt_m} ≤ C(λ, L) uniformly in D, this is C g²ℓ₁ℓ₂/D². The cross-scale pairs are bounded by the same sums once #DP.5 fixes the decay of the cross-scale blocks.
- {#DP.9} *(T) The torus-scale term.* |Cov(F₁, F₂)| ≤ ‖F₁ − 𝐄F₁‖₂‖F₂ − 𝐄F₂‖₂. The contraction ‖F_i − 𝐄F_i‖₂ ≤ ‖ξ_i‖₂ only reproduces #PF4.4-1, 4ε_{K′}²ℓ₁ℓ₂/N_s². That term exceeds C g_{K′}²ℓ₁ℓ₂/D² for every D ≥ 1 once 4N_s² > C (it is ≥ 4g_{K′}²N_s²ℓ₁ℓ₂, and C g_{K′}²ℓ₁ℓ₂/D² ≤ C g_{K′}²ℓ₁ℓ₂), since #PF4.4's first condition with c_ty ≥ 1 and ε_ty ≥ g_{K′} gives ε_{K′}/g_{K′} = p₀(g_{K′}) ≥ N_s². So without (T) the decay of #DP.8 is swamped, and (T) is the essential input.
  - {#DP.9-0} Needed: ‖F_i − 𝐄F_i‖₂ ≤ C g s_i^{(K)}, s_i^{(K)} = ℓ_iL^{K′−K} = ℓ_i(T_ph/u)/N_s. It says that the conditional expectation of a loop smaller than one final-lattice bond depends on the final-lattice field only through O(g) curvature at the loop. Its proof must control the dependence on the flat moduli of V_K, the commuting holonomies around the torus cycles. This bound gives C²g²(T_ph/u)²ℓ₁ℓ₂/N_s², the torus-scale part of #DP.2.
  - {#DP.9-1} *Route for (T).* For a direction h of V_K, ∂_hF_i = 𝐄[∂_hO_i | V_K] + Cov(O_i, ∂_h log p(𝐁; V_K) | V_K), with p the density of #DP.3; the covariance is bounded by #DA.6. A moduli direction can be chosen locally pure gauge except on a hypersurface far from C_i, and both terms must then be shown to be O(g s_i^{(K)}). Integrating along paths in the typical set gives the oscillation bound, provided that set is connected by paths of bounded length. For general G the space of commuting quadruples need not be connected (no SU(N) shortcut), so the components must be compared separately; this is open.
